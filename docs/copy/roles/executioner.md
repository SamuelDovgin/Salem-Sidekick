# Executioner: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NE — Neutral Evil.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Get the assigned Town target hanged while you are alive. Other causes of death can turn you into Jester; a successful objective reduces future trial capacity. NE — Neutral Evil.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Get the assigned Town target hanged while you are alive. Other causes of death can turn you into Jester; a successful objective reduces future trial capacity.

**Win condition:** Remain alive when the assigned target is hanged through a valid route. If they die otherwise, your role and objective change.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Read your assigned target and prepare a believable case that can lead to their daytime execution while you survive. You do not select a new target on N1. No ordinary night target is required.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Read your assigned target and prepare a believable case that can lead to their daytime execution while you survive. You do not select a new target on N1.

**How to choose:** No ordinary night target is required. Learn the assigned player’s claim before inventing accusations. With no opening information, prepare questions and a sustainable cover instead of immediately shouting a fake guilty.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Learn the assigned target’s claim. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Check whether the target is alive and whether your role changed.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check whether the target is alive and whether your role changed. If they died through another route and you became Jester, stop following the Executioner plan.

**What this does and does not establish:** Only the relevant hanging routes satisfy the objective; a night kill or ordinary day shot is not interchangeable.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use a cover that supports your survival and objective.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use a cover that supports your survival and objective. Apply pressure through specific claim problems; an obviously impossible TI accusation may get you executed before your target.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Sheriff, Psychic, or Investigator can carry a pressure story but require fabricated results and are not real ability fits. Nonvisiting covers reduce movement conflict.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If still Executioner, update the case from D2 and keep the correct execution route in view.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If still Executioner, update the case from D2 and keep the correct execution route in view. If Jester, switch to its hanging objective and communication quota rather than continuing to push a dead target.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Build a plausible claim and reason for pressure.
- **Middle:** Seek the correct hanging route while remaining alive.
- **Late:** If the target dies otherwise, switch fully to Jester mechanics instead of persisting with an obsolete objective.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Sheriff (TI): pressure story; weak movement fit; Oracle (TP): nonvisit fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Pressure story; weak movement fit.** A claimed suspicious result can create a reason to question the assigned target. **What can expose it:** It is fabricated and you normally did not visit; observers, framing context, and overfocusing can expose it.

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Nonvisit fallback.** No normal night action can fit the cover’s astral footprint. **What can expose it:** It supplies no real protection and does not by itself motivate your target’s execution.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not assume the target is chosen each night. Do not celebrate a non-hanging death as victory. Do not forget the Jester participation requirement after a transition.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not assume the target is chosen each night. Do not celebrate a non-hanging death as victory. Do not forget the Jester participation requirement after a transition.

**Important interaction:** Prosecution and Tribunal can fulfill the hanging objective. The target pool excludes several role categories.

**Worked decision:** Your target dies at night: stop the Executioner plan and record the Jester transition before following its new objective.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Learn the assigned target’s claim.
2. Build survivable day pressure.
3. Recognize and follow any Jester transition.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Learn the assigned target’s claim.
2. Build survivable day pressure.
3. Recognize and follow any Jester transition.

- Create a plausible reason for public pressure rather than unexplained tunnel vision.
- Maintain a claim consistent with timing and visits.
- Switch goals immediately if you transform.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
