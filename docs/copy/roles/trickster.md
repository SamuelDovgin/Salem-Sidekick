# Trickster: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TK — Town Killing.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Place a Magic Mirror to absorb a direct attack, then Unleash the stored attack on a later night. One Self Mirror is available. TK — Town Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Place a Magic Mirror to absorb a direct attack, then Unleash the stored attack on a later night. One Self Mirror is available.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Mirror an eligible player so you can absorb a qualifying attack. There is no stored attack to Unleash at a fresh start. Prefer a credible useful player who may be attacked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Mirror an eligible player so you can absorb a qualifying attack. There is no stored attack to Unleash at a fresh start.

**How to choose:** Prefer a credible useful player who may be attacked. With no useful opening claim, choose an eligible unknown and preserve uncertainty; self-Mirror is limited, so do not spend it automatically just to avoid choosing.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Attempt a reasoned Mirror. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the Mirror target and whether an attack was actually absorbed.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the Mirror target and whether an attack was actually absorbed. No absorption does not clear the target or establish that nobody attacked elsewhere.

**What this does and does not establish:** A mirror attempt with no absorption is not an attack result. Unleash can produce the absorbed role’s death label, complicating kill-count deductions.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TK when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TK when asked. Explain the stored-attack state when necessary, but do not promise a kill you have not absorbed. Mirror is not a normal Vigilante shot.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Claim TK when appropriate. If full claiming, distinguish mirror attempts, actual absorption, and Unleash.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If an attack was absorbed, assess an evidence-backed Unleash target instead of pretending you can keep normal Mirroring while holding it.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If an attack was absorbed, assess an evidence-backed Unleash target instead of pretending you can keep normal Mirroring while holding it. If nothing was absorbed, Mirror again according to the new threat and legal options.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Mirror an ally likely to face an attack.
- **Middle:** Choose a justified Unleash and understand the stored attack’s collateral.
- **Late:** Use the active mirror/attack state to plan; do not treat the role as an always-available gun.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use obsolete taunt mechanics. Do not mirror again while holding an unused attack. Do not unleash a rampage onto crowded allied traffic.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use obsolete taunt mechanics. Do not mirror again while holding an unused attack. Do not unleash a rampage onto crowded allied traffic.

**Important interaction:** Unstoppable attacks shatter mirrors. Some passive attacks are not absorbed. Bodyguard/Trap can respond to an Unleash visit.

**Worked decision:** N1 mirror #4 absorbs an attack: record the reported role; N2 select a justified target for the stored attack.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Attempt a reasoned Mirror.
2. Track the real stored attack.
3. Unleash only on a supported target.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Attempt a reasoned Mirror.
2. Track the real stored attack.
3. Unleash only on a supported target.

- Mirror an ally likely to be attacked.
- Read the absorbed attacker role and attack properties.
- Choose an evidence-supported Unleash target and account for collateral risk.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
