# Mayor

Default role family: **Town Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5492. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Reveal from D2 to gain an additional vote immediately and more on subsequent days while alive. A reveal creates public identity and exposure.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Judge whether vote strength changes today’s outcome.
2. Reveal early enough to affect voting when necessary.
3. Recount weighted votes after every death or status change.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Assess whether revealing now produces a useful vote advantage.
- **Middle:** Coordinate with evidence-based Town pushes while accounting for exposure.
- **Late:** Recount actual weighted votes; do not wait until an unrevealable trial position.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Use discretion before revealing; claiming your exact role alone is not equivalent to the public Reveal effect.

## Read the information

Extra votes do not establish that your social reads are correct. Custom faction/conversion rules require separate allegiance reasoning.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume the ToS1 fixed-vote model. Do not wait until on trial to reveal. Do not lead votes without checking evidence.

## Interactions and limits

Town Traitor hunt affects further vote growth. Public identity can enable role guessers and direct killers.

## Worked decision

Your reveal would prevent a supported Town vote from losing today: use the actual button before the trial starts.
