# Vampire: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: Neutral Outlier.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Unique Outlier that Drains or converts eligible Town players, retaining their roles on the Vampire faction. Limited conversions and successor inheritance differ from ToS1 biting. Neutral Outlier.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Unique Outlier that Drains or converts eligible Town players, retaining their roles on the Vampire faction. Limited conversions and successor inheritance differ from ToS1 biting.

**Win condition:** Win with the Vampire faction by eliminating its opponents; converted allies can keep other role abilities.

Your actual alignment is Neutral Outlier. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Choose an eligible conversion opportunity when available. A nonconvertible target can lead to Drain instead, so the choice may become harmful. Prefer a plausible Town target without defense when building the faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose an eligible conversion opportunity when available. A nonconvertible target can lead to Drain instead, so the choice may become harmful.

**How to choose:** Prefer a plausible Town target without defense when building the faction. With no role evidence, choose an eligible unknown and accept the conversion may fail. Grace Period can permit Convert while preventing a fallback Drain kill.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Confirm an actual recruit. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the actual Convert/Drain outcome and charges.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the actual Convert/Drain outcome and charges. A successful recruit retains their role but changes faction; a failed attempt is not a teammate.

**What this does and does not establish:** A real Town-role demonstration remains real after conversion; its owner may now have a different win condition.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Coordinate with actual recruits and choose the next legal target according to charges, defense, and the current faction state.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Coordinate with actual recruits and choose the next legal target according to charges, defense, and the current faction state. Keep succession information instead of assuming a new Vampire resets conversion charges.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Consider a valuable eligible conversion and limited charges.
- **Middle:** Use retained ally abilities and track succession.
- **Late:** Reassess faction survival after a Vampire death; the old role’s death may not end the faction.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Cleric (TP): visiting fallback; Psychic (TI): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback.** A direct Convert/Drain target can share a claimed Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** Neither is a real Barrier, and conversion/fallback death can expose the effect.

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** A direct action can fit a chosen vision target. **What can expose it:** There is no real vision; maintain parity/count constraints and account for new faction members.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use old age/youngest-biter rules. Do not assume converting any defended or non-Town player succeeds. Do not assume a dead Vampire means the faction is gone.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use old age/youngest-biter rules. Do not assume converting any defended or non-Town player succeeds. Do not assume a dead Vampire means the faction is gone.

**Important interaction:** Converted teammates can inherit Vampire after its death. The page flags a solo-detection bug; do not promise innocence as guaranteed.

**Worked decision:** A previously demonstrated role is converted: keep its mechanical proof while changing its allegiance in private faction notes.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
