# Tracker: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Follow one player at night and learn visible destinations. Astral actions can appear as no visit. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Follow one player at night and learn visible destinations. Astral actions can appear as no visit.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Track one eligible player to learn their visible destination or destinations, if any. Choose someone whose future action claim you can compare with movement.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Track one eligible player to learn their visible destination or destinations, if any.

**How to choose:** Choose someone whose future action claim you can compare with movement. With no D1 lead, track an eligible unknown outside obvious visiting traps; do not treat loudness or silence as a faction test.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Get the direction right. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record where your target visibly went.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record where your target visibly went. “No visible visit” is different from “blocked/no result,” and neither identifies their role. You are not receiving a list of visitors to their house.

**What this does and does not establish:** Visiting a corpse suggests a corpse-using ability, not an ordinary visit to a living player. Visiting a death does not by itself identify the killer.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

After D2 claims, track someone whose action can resolve a concrete dispute.

**Expanded copy — same section**

After D2 claims, track someone whose action can resolve a concrete dispute. If a target has an astral/nonvisiting explanation, seek other evidence rather than assuming a missing visible visit catches them lying.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Check a player whose movement can be compared to their claim.
- **Middle:** Use earlier claims to choose more discriminating checks.
- **Late:** Recheck a consequential inconsistency where useful, remembering astral and redirection context.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not equate no visit with no action. Do not omit extra destinations. Do not assume a two-visit result proves Town.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not equate no visit with no action. Do not omit extra destinations. Do not assume a two-visit result proves Town.

**Important interaction:** Bestow changes visibility; Catalyst creates extra actions. Faction changes invalidate simple corpse-role deductions.

**Worked decision:** #4 claims a normal protection visit to #9 but you saw #4 go to #7: ask about redirection or other context, then present the conflict.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Get the direction right.
2. Compare movement with the exact claimed action.
3. Keep consistent separate from confirmed.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Get the direction right.
2. Compare movement with the exact claimed action.
3. Keep consistent separate from confirmed.

- Check unresolved visiting claims.
- Ask for the target’s action before presenting a contradiction.
- Compare a destination with other evidence about that house.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
