# Lookout: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

Watch a house at night and see visible visitors. Astral visitors are absent; the observation does not identify visitor roles. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Watch a house at night and see visible visitors. Astral visitors are absent; the observation does not identify visitor roles.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Watch one eligible house for visible visitors. Choose the house for the information its traffic might provide. A credible player expected to attract visits can be informative, but an obvious crowded call also risks rampages.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Watch one eligible house for visible visitors. Choose the house for the information its traffic might provide.

**How to choose:** A credible player expected to attract visits can be informative, but an obvious crowded call also risks rampages. With no reliable claim, watch an eligible unknown; avoid assuming a loud TP/LO call is safe or truthful.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Get one correctly directed visitor list. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record who visited the watched player, not whom that player visited.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record who visited the watched player, not whom that player visited. An empty visible visitor list and a failed watch are different. Astral actions can be absent from the list.

**What this does and does not establish:** A visitor list is not a killer list. No visible visitors does not exclude astral effects or establish that the target was safe.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Full claim Lookout (TI) and share useful observations from D2.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim Lookout (TI) and share useful observations from D2. If a list would expose protectives without solving a case, ask visitors for claims before revealing every detail; do not withhold a contradiction until your information is lost.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Normally full claim and report useful information early. You may briefly hold a visitor list to test claims; avoid losing the information entirely.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If D2 reveals a threatened useful ally or a disputed visitor story, watch the house that can resolve that question.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If D2 reveals a threatened useful ally or a disputed visitor story, watch the house that can resolve that question. If your previous target had no useful traffic, choose a new house instead of treating the first choice as permanent.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Watch a plausible useful target while accounting for visitor hazards.
- **Middle:** Use known traffic and claims to test who explains reported effects.
- **Late:** Choose the house whose traffic would most resolve the remaining uncertainty, not simply the loudest player.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not swap visitors with destinations. Do not claim you watched yourself normally. Do not expose protective identities unnecessarily.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not swap visitors with destinations. Do not claim you watched yourself normally. Do not expose protective identities unnecessarily.

**Important interaction:** Crowded calls can attract rampages. The page contains an obsolete Camouflage reference; current baseline does not use it.

**Worked decision:** #4 says they were blocked and #7 visited: ask #7 what they did, then compare with any other visitors.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Get one correctly directed visitor list.
2. Ask what those visitors did.
3. Resolve a specific effect or contradiction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Get one correctly directed visitor list.
2. Ask what those visitors did.
3. Resolve a specific effect or contradiction.

- Watch valuable players likely to draw informative traffic.
- Compare visitor claims against reported effects.
- Share contradictions without assuming every visitor attacked.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
