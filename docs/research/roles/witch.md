# Witch

Default role family: **Coven Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5401. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Control one player into a second destination. Your faction learns the controlled player’s role; you receive their borrowed ability feedback.

**Key rule:** You normally visit only the first player. Change that player on consecutive nights. Revealing them Illuminates them, making them unavailable to Blood Ritual.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Disrupt a consequential opponent or exploit a known ability.
2. Share acquired information with allies.
3. Anticipate control notifications linking your visits to the effect.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Acquire useful information or disrupt an important action.
- **Middle:** Use known roles without breaking your own cover’s movement story.
- **Late:** Compare controlling a vote-changing ability with direct book killing; anticipate immunity and charges.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Your information can support a story but control is conspicuous. Treat Sheriff/Psychic covers as conditional, not natural undetectable equivalents.

## Read the information

A learned role can still be affected by special framing. Knowing a role does not supply the complete history another TI would observe.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim Seer merely because you selected two numbers. Do not assume consecutive control works normally. Do not omit the required second target when attacking with book.

## Interactions and limits

Immunity, Hangover, and limited-use abilities constrain Control. Revealing information can impose Illumination, relevant to Ritualist.

## Worked decision

Control Sheriff 4 into 9: your visible destination is 4; the borrowed Search checks 9. No completed Control means no borrowed result.
