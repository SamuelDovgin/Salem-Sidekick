## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Dreamweave an eligible Town-faction target, unless the book requires a different action. Prefer an isolated useful enemy likely to remain unvisited by Town next night.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Dreamweave an eligible Town-faction target, unless the book requires a different action.

**How to choose:** Prefer an isolated useful enemy likely to remain unvisited by Town next night. With no role evidence, choose a legal nonally and accept that the faction requirement or later cure can stop progress; avoid claiming a guaranteed state change.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Apply a legal Dreamweave. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record whether Dreamweave applied.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record whether Dreamweave applied. The target is not immediately in the final Insomniac state, and an attempted weave is not proof of their exact role.

**What this does and does not establish:** The status concerns faction eligibility; it does not authorize claiming a precise Town role.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Check whether the pending target was cured by a Town visit before assuming Insomnia.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check whether the pending target was cured by a Town visit before assuming Insomnia. Choose a new useful legal target if appropriate, or make the book action while preserving the earlier state record.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Choose a useful Town target and plan how a cure might happen.
- **Middle:** Coordinate disruption to convert pending effects into Insomnia.
- **Late:** Target an ability that can still change the game rather than an already spent role.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Psychic (TI): visiting fallback; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** A one-player visit can fit the selected Psychic target. **What can expose it:** No real vision exists; the victim’s dream state may correlate with your action.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback.** A single ordinary visit can match the Barrier target on paper. **What can expose it:** You supply no protection, and dream effects contradict a claim that your only effect was Barrier.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

**Expanded copy — same section**
