# Covenite: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: Coven Outlier.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

An Outlier Coven role with no special ability beyond potential book use and faction cooperation. The roledeck must permit it. Coven Outlier.

**Expanded copy — same section**

An Outlier Coven role with no special ability beyond potential book use and faction cooperation. The roledeck must permit it.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is Coven Outlier. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no extra ability without the book. Check the team’s book holder and prepare a sustainable public cover. Without the book, no numbered N1 action is required.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no extra ability without the book. Check the team’s book holder and prepare a sustainable public cover.

**How to choose:** Without the book, no numbered N1 action is required. With it, coordinate a legal nonally attack. Do not invent a special utility action just because other Coven members have one.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose a cover legal in this setup. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record no role action or the actual book action privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record no role action or the actual book action privately. A plain starting state can fit a low-information cover, but it produces no real TI result.

**What this does and does not establish:** Teammate information may support a fabricated will, but does not create the ability to demonstrate its claim.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the chosen cover’s public alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the chosen cover’s public alignment. Pilgrim (Town Outlier) is a nonaction story only where legal; Vigilante (TK) explains N1 loading but creates a later shooting demand.

Your actual alignment is Coven Outlier. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Before book, nonvisiting covers such as Oracle are movement-compatible but unproved. With book, reassess direct-visiting options.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Recheck book ownership and follow the actual available action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Recheck book ownership and follow the actual available action. If a cover now requires a demonstration you cannot supply, recognize the risk instead of inventing a real ability.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Participate and consider book duties.
- **Middle:** Buy useful time with a consistent claim rather than inventing powers.
- **Late:** Focus on the actual voting/kill objective; a fragile public-reveal claim may not survive a demonstration request.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional fit without book; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback with book. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional fit without book.** No ordinary role action can fit a nonvisible-visit story. **What can expose it:** You do not protect a role, and holding the book changes the movement.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback with book.** A direct book visit can fit a single reported Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** The attack is not protection; feedback and deaths can expose the story.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim a natural role ability you do not possess. Do not treat no visible visit as safety from all investigation. Do not assume a permanent no-visit cover after receiving book.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim a natural role ability you do not possess. Do not treat no visible visit as safety from all investigation. Do not assume a permanent no-visit cover after receiving book.

**Important interaction:** Public-reveal Town roles remain poor durable covers. Outlier legality matters to both true and fake claims.

**Worked decision:** No book tonight: an Oracle story avoids a movement mismatch, but you still cannot produce a genuine protective save.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
