# Veteran: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TK — Town Killing.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Three Alerts grant Basic Defense and shoot all direct visitors with Powerful Attack that night. Friendly visitors are also at risk. TK — Town Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Three Alerts grant Basic Defense and shoot all direct visitors with Powerful Attack that night. Friendly visitors are also at risk.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Decide whether to Alert at home. You have limited Alerts, and an Alert can kill Town visitors as well as enemies. With no evidence that you will be visited, holding is reasonable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Decide whether to Alert at home. You have limited Alerts, and an Alert can kill Town visitors as well as enemies.

**How to choose:** With no evidence that you will be visited, holding is reasonable. Do not ask for TP/LO and then Alert as a beginner trick; that deliberately invites allies into your attack. An obvious bait line is not a reliable prediction of enemy traffic.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Avoid Town visitor bait. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record Alert on/off and the actual feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record Alert on/off and the actual feedback. A dead visitor is not automatically evil; an uneventful Alert still spends a charge if it resolved. Current roleblock rules matter.

**What this does and does not establish:** No kills does not disprove an alert. A visitor surviving can reflect protection; an astral action is not shot.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TK when requested.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TK when requested. If your action explains deaths, give the truthful Alert timing and ask others for their actions. Do not use the ability solely to prove the claim.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Claim TK initially where useful. Reveal Veteran when needed to explain kills or prevent allied visits.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If D2 makes you a plausible enemy target, consider an Alert while avoiding allied traffic.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If D2 makes you a plausible enemy target, consider an Alert while avoiding allied traffic. If there is no threat, conserve charges. Revise the decision each night rather than exhausting all Alerts at the start.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Avoid inviting friendly visitors into an alert.
- **Middle:** Adapt alert use to known threats and remaining charges.
- **Late:** Balance self-preservation against the ally votes needed to win; an unused alert is not automatically wasted.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use TP/LO bait as a beginner default. Do not assume roleblock immunity. Do not claim infinite alerts.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use TP/LO bait as a beginner default. Do not assume roleblock immunity. Do not claim infinite alerts.

**Important interaction:** Roleblocks can prevent Alert. Astral visitors bypass the shooting effect, and sufficient protection can preserve direct visitors.

**Worked decision:** A protector offers to visit tonight: warn them or avoid alerting rather than treating the friendly casualty as unavoidable.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Avoid Town visitor bait.
2. Spend an Alert for a reason.
3. Explain deaths without assuming visitor guilt.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Avoid Town visitor bait.
2. Spend an Alert for a reason.
3. Explain deaths without assuming visitor guilt.

- Choose alerts based on likely hostile attention.
- Avoid inviting friendly protection into an alert.
- Record alert nights and remaining charges.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
