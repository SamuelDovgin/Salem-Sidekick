# Medusa

Default role family: **Coven Deception**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5933. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Three Stone Gazes mark players so their eventual death role and will are hidden from opponents. Medusa begins self-Stoned; book can attack while applying Stone.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Hide valuable information or enable a plausible cover.
2. Coordinate with kills rather than assuming a mark kills.
3. Track which marked players are still alive.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Suppress meaningful information rather than marking random expendable players.
- **Middle:** Use hidden information to plan without leaking it through an incompatible cover.
- **Late:** Retain role-space uncertainty where useful, but account for true-role recovery by other abilities.

## Claim timing and disclosure

If Grace Period is active or unchecked and you are claiming TP, hold Stone on N1. A Stone Gaze is a direct, non-harmful visit; an enabled Medusa action does not make the claimed protective visit legal. Check the N1 notice before the action, not after a witness reports the visit.

A visiting cover fits movement better than an inherently nonvisiting one while gazing. Claiming a hidden role is conditional, not automatically safe.

## Read the information

Coven may learn information opponents cannot read. It should not appear in a claimed Town will without a legitimate explanation.

## Common mistakes

Do not use the old stay-home visitor-petrification ability. Do not assume Stone kills. Do not assume hidden bodies prevent every role from learning their truth.

## Interactions and limits

Necromancer can use Stoned bodies; normal Town Retributionist cannot. Amnesiac can remember underlying roles.

## Worked decision

You claim TP/Cleric and see the Grace Period notice: hold Stone on N1. A Tracker following you or a Lookout watching the recipient could see a Stone visit that a Cleric could not legally make that night. With Grace Period off and a cover that can actually visit, a coordinated useful mark may be reasonable.
