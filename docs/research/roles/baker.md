# Baker

Default role family: **Neutral Apocalypse**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6503. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Feed Bread to three other living players to become Famine, with a reduced-population exception. Bread recipients need to survive; you already possess Bread.

## Win condition

Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Feed players likely to survive until transformation.
2. Avoid repeatedly feeding existing recipients.
3. Reassess the remaining survivors after every death.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Feed likely survivors.
- **Middle:** Protect progress from recipient deaths without exposing the goal.
- **Late:** After Famine, focus on bread-holders and survival through the vote rather than further Feed actions.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A quiet visiting cover such as Psychic or Cleric can fit pre-transformation movement but supplies no genuine investigative/protection result.

## Read the information

Transformation is public evidence that a threshold was met, not public identification of which player transformed.

## Common mistakes

Do not count your starting Bread as an ordinary recipient. Do not assume dead recipients count. Do not reveal an evil role as a beginner survival tactic.

## Interactions and limits

Bodyguard/Trap do not ordinarily respond to a nonharmful Feed. Blocks and obstacles can delay progress.

## Worked decision

If #4 is likely to be eliminated tomorrow, feeding a less threatened eligible player can retain progress.

## Transformed state

Famine: starvation begins after transformation; bread delays opponents’ starvation. Starve accelerates a recipient’s loss of bread. Invincible Defense does not prevent hanging. Keep the transformed state distinct from Feed in private notes.
