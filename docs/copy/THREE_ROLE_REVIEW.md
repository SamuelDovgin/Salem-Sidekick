## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no night ability. Save your single day shot and prepare to question D2 claims; you cannot shoot on D1. Read D1 for a concrete contradiction worth asking about tomorrow.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no night ability. Save your single day shot and prepare to question D2 claims; you cannot shoot on D1.

**How to choose:** Read D1 for a concrete contradiction worth asking about tomorrow. With no information, leave your target undecided. “No target yet” is correct; a quiet player or a request to prove yourself is not a shooting case.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Build one supported case before shooting. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

### A concrete opening, with no forced shot

**Setup:** You are Deputy in the normal text profile. D1 has no useful claims. You have not used High Noon.

1. **N1:** No ability target is required. Start an action line, `N1: No night ability`, and a separate question list. Do not select someone merely to fill a box.
2. **D2:** Suppose #6 posts `Tracker: N1 #9 -> #4`, while #9 claims `Veteran, stayed home; no role change`. Ask #9 to explain that specific movement conflict. Check for redirection, role changes, mistaken identity, or a false Tracker before treating it as settled. This is a case to investigate, not an automatic shooting instruction.
3. **Public alignment:** If the lobby asks for alignment, answer `TK`. If explaining the full role is useful, say `Deputy; shot unused`. Neither TP nor TS is correct for your actual role.
4. **Decision:** If the case remains unresolved, hold the shot. If independent evidence supports an enemy, the day ability is enabled, nobody is on the stand, and using the shot has value, consider it. One shot is available; using it to prove yourself can waste it.
5. **Outcome branch:** Suppose you fire and get Miss. Log `D2: Shot 9 — Miss`. The charge is spent. A Town target, sufficient defense, or other relevant context can explain the miss; do not announce `9 confirmed Town`.
6. **N2:** Still no night ability. If the bullet remains, update the case. If spent, use claims, questions, and votes rather than waiting for another bullet.

**What the beginner can now answer:** Do I have an N1 button? No. What is useful instead? Prepare an honest history and a specific follow-up. What do I claim? TK. Must I shoot D2? No. Does a miss clear the target? No.

**Why this is useful:** It replaces an impossible first-night action with a legitimate preparation task and explains the irreversible decision before the player spends the charge. Success for this lesson is a reasoned hold or supported shot, not a required kill.

### A concrete Drink → cover → rotate sequence

**Setup:** You are Poisoner, actually CU. Another Coven member has the book. #4 is an eligible nonally; Coven has no plan requiring #4 to act. There is no known dangerous visitor bait. These are example facts, not facts the site knows about a live match.

1. **N1 mode:** Choose ordinary Drink on #4 for a simple disruption plan. If no enemy role is known, choose an eligible unknown nonally; the choice is not a prediction that they must be a TI or killer. Check team plans first.
2. **Private record:** Write `N1: Drink 4 — [actual feedback]`. If interrupted, keep that outcome. Do not fill “success” just because you pressed the ability.
3. **Public cover:** Select **Tavern Keeper — TS** from the unrestricted claim selector. This is logical because the target can genuinely experience a roleblock, which the cover can produce. If a full claim is requested, a compatible deliberately authored public history is `Tavern Keeper / N1: Drink 4`. Do not copy the private Poisoner heading or faction information.
4. **D2 interpretation:** If #4 reports being roleblocked, that supports the action account; it does not prove you are Tavern Keeper or Town. If they report a different effect, do not silently alter your own private action record to fit them.
5. **N2 after successful Drink:** #4 is Hungover. Choose a different useful eligible target, such as an independently identified enemy night actor. Do not promise to roleblock #4 on consecutive nights.
6. **Alternative mode:** Smog blocks incoming visits to the selected house with an obstacle effect; it does not simply stop the owner’s action. Coordinate because allied visits can also be obstructed. Socialite — TS becomes an explainable but difficult alternative, with guest-list and immunity differences. Keeping Tavern Keeper while claiming every Smog report was a roleblock creates a mismatch.
7. **Book branch:** If you receive the book, the direct attack also roleblocks. Do not plan a normal book attack plus Smog together. A dead target and a genuine roleblock effect can change how well the old cover survives.

**What the beginner can now answer:** Which button starts the simple plan? Drink. Who is a legal fallback? A nonally, after checking team interference and visiting danger. What alignment goes public? TS for the Tavern Keeper cover, not actual CU. Who next? A new eligible target after successful Drink. Is Smog the same effect? No.

**Why this cover has a reason:** It matches target feedback as well as an ordinary visit. **Why it is not guaranteed:** A real effect does not establish faction; mode changes, Hangover, the book, and independent observations can expose differences. Cleric remains selectable as TP, but supplies a weaker story because you do not actually Barrier and the target may report disruption.
