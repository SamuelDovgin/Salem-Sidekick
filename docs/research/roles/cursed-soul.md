# Cursed Soul

Default role family: **Neutral Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5714. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Soul Swap exchanges roles with a player; it does not exchange factions. After swapping, use the new role’s abilities.

**Key rule:** In the default Wandering Souls faction, the goal is to remove every remaining Cursed Soul role. Check the actual role gained; the swap can be redirected or randomized.

## Win condition

In the default Wandering Souls faction, eliminate every Cursed Soul role while a Wandering Souls member survives. Your current role and your faction are separate.

This assumes the default Wandering Souls faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Track your current role and unchanged faction separately.
2. Record the actual swap outcome rather than intended target.
3. Use the acquired role to pursue the faction objective.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Track the first actual swap and retain faction identity.
- **Middle:** Use acquired abilities toward eliminating Cursed Souls.
- **Late:** Count which Cursed Soul roles remain and whether a faction member will survive the final elimination.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Before swapping, nonvisiting covers match astral movement better. After swapping, consult the new role’s mechanics and keep a dated transition story.

## Read the information

Knowing an old role does not establish a player’s current role. Role-swapped Town abilities need not belong to Town faction.

## Common mistakes

Do not play the old role-steals-faction objective. Do not assume every chosen swap is deterministic. Do not keep using your prior role’s template after a real swap.

## Interactions and limits

The main wiki role’s Swap section is incomplete; Fandom and official changes support faction-dependent randomization and failures.

## Worked decision

Swap into Sheriff and you still belong to Wandering Souls. The player given Cursed Soul keeps their old faction.
