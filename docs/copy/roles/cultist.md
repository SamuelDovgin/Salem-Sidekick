# Cultist: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: Coven Outlier.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

An Outlier with one day Indoctrinate converting an eligible Town player into your faction while retaining their role. Book use is separate. Coven Outlier.

**Expanded copy — same section**

An Outlier with one day Indoctrinate converting an eligible Town player into your faction while retaining their role. Book use is separate.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is Coven Outlier. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Your special conversion is a daytime Indoctrinate, not an additional N1 button. At night, check the book and act only if its attack is available. Identify a possible useful Town recruit from actual claims.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Your special conversion is a daytime Indoctrinate, not an additional N1 button. At night, check the book and act only if its attack is available.

**How to choose:** Identify a possible useful Town recruit from actual claims. With no evidence, wait for a better daytime opportunity and confirm the client enables the ability; do not assert that a particular opening-day timing is always legal.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose a useful recruit from evidence. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record real book feedback or no night action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record real book feedback or no night action. A recruited player retains their role while changing faction, so future role claims and faction assumptions must be tracked separately.

**What this does and does not establish:** Historical confirmation does not guarantee current loyalty in conversion games.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If the daytime conversion succeeded, coordinate with the new ally and update defense/state from actual feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If the daytime conversion succeeded, coordinate with the new ally and update defense/state from actual feedback. If it remains unused, weigh a better recruit. Recheck the book for the next night.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Understand conversion eligibility and the special setup.
- **Middle:** Use the one conversion for a concrete faction benefit.
- **Late:** Track retained Town abilities and changed allegiance; role demonstration alone may mislead opponents.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional fit without book; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback with book. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional fit without book.** The special conversion is a day action, leaving no ordinary night visit without book. **What can expose it:** No Aegis is supplied; direct book attacks and conversion consequences create later conflicts.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback with book.** A direct book target can share a claimed Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** No real Barrier exists, and recruited role/faction changes can complicate public evidence.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not equate the converted player with a Town Traitor. Do not assume the target loses their ability. Do not apply the normal static faction model afterward.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not equate the converted player with a Town Traitor. Do not assume the target loses their ability. Do not apply the normal static faction model afterward.

**Important interaction:** Cultist’s pre-conversion defense and special slot behavior are setup details. Converted players can receive the book unlike ordinary TT.

**Worked decision:** An established Town ally becomes converted: their public role demonstration remains real, but the team objective has changed.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
