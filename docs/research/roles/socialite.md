# Socialite

Default role family: **Town Support**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5893. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Add guests during the day, then Party at a house to reject uninvited visitors.

**Key rule:** Two manual guests total, at most one added per day. Do not Party at the same house consecutively. This obstructs incoming visits, not the resident’s outgoing ability.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Invite trusted allies whose visits need access.
2. Choose a useful party target without sabotaging allied actions.
3. Track guest-list additions separately from party nights.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Invite allies whose access is likely to matter.
- **Middle:** Coordinate alternating party houses without blocking valuable Town actions.
- **Late:** Keep the necessary surviving allies able to visit and vote; guest-list access is not faction proof.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TS where appropriate. Full claim when party coordination or a blocked ally makes the distinction important.

## Read the information

You learn whether someone was rejected, not a complete list of identities. A blocked visit is an obstacle, not ordinary roleblocking.

## Common mistakes

Do not automatically invite rejected visitors. Do not repeat the same party house. Do not report identities you did not receive.

## Interactions and limits

Party immunity and astral visits matter. Poisoner smog differs from Party and can block a real Socialite.

## Worked decision

Party at 4 can stop an uninvited player reaching 4. A rejection notice does not name that visitor.
