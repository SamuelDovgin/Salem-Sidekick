# Veteran

Default role family: **Town Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6536. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Choose Alert to defend yourself and shoot your direct visitors that night; choose No Alert to save a charge.

**Key rule:** Three Alerts. Alert gives Basic Defense and Powerful attacks, including against Town visitors. Shot feedback is a count, not visitor identities.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose alerts based on likely hostile attention.
2. Avoid inviting friendly protection into an alert.
3. Record alert nights and remaining charges.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Avoid inviting friendly visitors into an alert.
- **Middle:** Adapt alert use to known threats and remaining charges.
- **Late:** Balance self-preservation against the ally votes needed to win; an unused alert is not automatically wasted.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TK initially where useful. Reveal Veteran when needed to explain kills or prevent allied visits.

## Read the information

No kills does not disprove an alert. A visitor surviving can reflect protection; an astral action is not shot.

## Common mistakes

Do not use TP/LO bait as a beginner default. Do not assume roleblock immunity. Do not claim infinite alerts.

## Interactions and limits

Roleblocks can prevent Alert. Astral visitors bypass the shooting effect, and sufficient protection can preserve direct visitors.

## Worked decision

Alert and shoot two visitors: that does not prove both died or tell you which players they were.
