# Amnesiac: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TS — Town Support.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Automatically remember an eligible dead Town player’s true role; Town Power has priority. This is a role transition, not a selectable night visit. TS — Town Support.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Automatically remember an eligible dead Town player’s true role; Town Power has priority. This is a role transition, not a selectable night visit.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You do not manually pick a corpse on N1. Wait for the automatic Remember process and prepare to switch plans if you acquire an eligible Town role. No numbered night target is needed.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You do not manually pick a corpse on N1. Wait for the automatic Remember process and prepare to switch plans if you acquire an eligible Town role.

**How to choose:** No numbered night target is needed. Read D1 claims and keep notes that will remain useful after a role change; do not promise a specific inherited role.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Observe the actual Remember. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Check the actual new role notification.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check the actual new role notification. If no eligible Remember occurred, stay on the Amnesiac plan. Preserve the night history rather than rewriting N1 as an action from the newly acquired role.

**What this does and does not establish:** A hidden or altered death does not necessarily prevent Remember. Multiple Amnesiacs cannot all acquire the same Unique role.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TS while still Amnesiac if asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS while still Amnesiac if asked. If you Remember, use the new role’s disclosure rules and explain the transition when needed; a new TI needs an honest history, not invented earlier checks.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Claim TS when appropriate, then explain the remembered role and timing if challenged.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Open the acquired role’s current action instructions.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Open the acquired role’s current action instructions. If still Amnesiac, there is no manual resurrection to perform. If transformed into an action role, start from its actual available state and phase.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Learn claims while awaiting an eligible automatic Remember.
- **Middle:** After transition, follow the new role’s strategy and retain the old-role history.
- **Late:** Explain the timeline if its credibility matters; do not keep presenting as an unchanged Amnesiac.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Amnesiac history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS when appropriate, then explain the remembered role and timing if challenged.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim a chosen remember target like ToS1. Do not remain indefinitely unremembered after suitable deaths without explanation. Do not erase the role timeline.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim a chosen remember target like ToS1. Do not remain indefinitely unremembered after suitable deaths without explanation. Do not erase the role timeline.

**Important interaction:** The lowest-number resolution for competing Unique remembers is documented; custom factions add edge cases. Treat unusual transitions as context-sensitive.

**Worked decision:** A Town role dies and you remember it next morning: keep the original Amnesiac start and open that new guide.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Observe the actual Remember.
2. Preserve the transition history.
3. Follow the acquired role without inventing past results.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Observe the actual Remember.
2. Preserve the transition history.
3. Follow the acquired role without inventing past results.

- Follow claims and deaths while waiting.
- Record the moment the role changes.
- Immediately switch to the remembered role’s strategy and action rules.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
