# Archmage

Default role family: **Coven Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5752. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Formerly Coven Leader. Retrain yourself or known allies under role-availability restrictions; retraining allies is limited to Common Coven. Book attacks are an alternative action.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Coordinate the team’s actual needs before retraining.
2. Preserve useful abilities instead of changing roles for novelty.
3. Reassess your cover after any transformation or book change.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Assess which roles the faction needs.
- **Middle:** Retrain only where the new ability has a practical purpose.
- **Late:** Prefer the action that can change the next vote/kill rather than a late transformation with no time to help.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Use a sustainable Town cover; absent book or retraining, nonvisiting claims may fit. Avoid asserting a protective effect you cannot reproduce.

## Read the information

Retraining changes role, not automatically faction. It can invalidate a role reveal or cover plan made earlier.

## Common mistakes

Do not use the old two-retrain or Basic Defense model. Do not assume every role is available. Do not claim you retrained and independently attacked normally.

## Interactions and limits

Current CPOW roles are mutually exclusive at normal spawning. Self-retraining and known-ally restrictions need separate treatment.

## Worked decision

A needed utility role is available: agree the change with your ally and retain a dated old-role/new-role history.
