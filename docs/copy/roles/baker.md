# Baker: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NA — Neutral Apocalypse.** Default faction: Apocalypse. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Feed Bread to three other living players to become Famine, with a reduced-population exception. Bread recipients need to survive; you already possess Bread. NA — Neutral Apocalypse.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Feed Bread to three other living players to become Famine, with a reduced-population exception. Bread recipients need to survive; you already possess Bread.

**Win condition:** Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

Your actual alignment is NA — Neutral Apocalypse. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Feed Bread to one eligible other player. Your first milestone is one living recipient, not a kill or a public claim that you protected them. Prefer someone likely to survive several cycles.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Feed Bread to one eligible other player. Your first milestone is one living recipient, not a kill or a public claim that you protected them.

**How to choose:** Prefer someone likely to survive several cycles. Avoid an obvious imminent execution, a planned victim you know about, or obvious visitor bait. With no useful information, choose an eligible unpressured player and accept that survival is uncertain.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Keep one other recipient alive. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

### A concrete living-recipient plan

**Setup:** You are a normal-profile solo Baker, actually NA. #4 is eligible, unpressured, not already fed, and not obvious visitor bait. You do not have confirmed knowledge of their role. Other Apocalypse teammates must not be invented; the default bucket is mutually exclusive unless the setup changes it.

1. **N1:** Feed #4. If no player stands out, an eligible unpressured unknown is a workable starting choice. The reason is retaining a living recipient, not proving their faction or granting protection.
2. **Private dawn log:** If the Feed applied and #4 is alive, write `N1: Feed 4; living other recipients: 1`. If blocked, do not add progress. If #4 died, do not keep them in the living count. Your own starting Bread is not an additional other recipient.
3. **Public cover:** If you choose Cleric from the all-role selector, the public alignment is **TP**, while your actual alignment remains NA. A deliberately fabricated cover history can say `Cleric / N1: Barrier 4`; the private truth remains Feed. This is a role-deception example for the game, not a real Barrier result. The site must never generate a saved-player message from Bread or leak the private progress counter into the public will.
4. **D2 target planning:** Read claims and pressure. A player about to be executed is a poor new Feed candidate if another likely survivor exists. A highly exposed player expected to be killed can also lose your progress. Do not fight every case solely because your recipient is involved; that can expose your priorities.
5. **N2, recipient alive:** Feed a different eligible likely survivor, say #7. If both applied recipients remain alive at the next dawn, your ordinary other-recipient count is two.
6. **N2, recipient dead or failed:** Your count is lower; choose a replacement or a fresh eligible recipient. Refeeding an already fed living player does not add another recipient. A later third living other recipient reaches the ordinary threshold; reduced-population rules can change it, so follow actual transformation feedback.
7. **After Famine:** Stop using the pre-transform Feed plan. Work on remaining bread-holders with Starve and survive the day vote. The public transformation announcement does not itself name you, but it changes the lobby’s priorities. Invincible Defense does not prevent hanging.

**What the beginner can now answer:** Who should I Feed? Someone likely to remain alive. What counts? Other living recipients, not your starting Bread or failed attempts. What is N2? A new recipient or a replacement, based on dawn. What do I claim? The chosen cover’s alignment. Did Bread heal them? No.

**Why this cover is only a fallback:** Feed and a normal Barrier can share a one-player destination, but Baker supplies no protection. Attack feedback or the demand for a genuine save can break the story. Psychic — TI remains selectable; its visions would be invented and must respect the actual Good/two, Evil/three pattern and chosen target. The beginner is shown the cost rather than told either cover is safe.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Privately record the attempted Feed and actual progress, then remove dead recipients from the living count. Your own starting Bread is not one of the three other recipients needed in the ordinary threshold.

**Expanded copy — same section**

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If the first recipient is alive and Bread was applied, Feed a different eligible player likely to survive.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If the first recipient is alive and Bread was applied, Feed a different eligible player likely to survive. If the Feed failed or the recipient died, adjust the progress count and choose accordingly. If transformed, switch to Famine/Starve instructions rather than continuing the Feed plan.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Feed likely survivors.
- **Middle:** Protect progress from recipient deaths without exposing the goal.
- **Late:** After Famine, focus on bread-holders and survival through the vote rather than further Feed actions.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

**After transformation:** Famine: starvation begins after transformation; bread delays opponents’ starvation. Starve accelerates a recipient’s loss of bread. Invincible Defense does not prevent hanging. Keep the transformed state distinct from Feed in private notes.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Cleric (TP): simple visiting fallback; Psychic (TI): visiting fallback with invented information. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Simple visiting fallback.** Feed can share the ordinary one-player destination of a Barrier claim. **What can expose it:** Bread is not protection: attack feedback, inability to save, and a later Famine state can expose the story.

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback with invented information.** One Feed destination can fit the chosen player in a vision. **What can expose it:** You receive no vision; N1 Good/two and N2 Evil/three constraints and independent claims must remain consistent.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not count your starting Bread as an ordinary recipient. Do not assume dead recipients count. Do not reveal an evil role as a beginner survival tactic.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Keep one other recipient alive.
2. Reach the required living-recipient threshold.
3. After transformation, survive the vote and pressure remaining bread-holders.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Keep one other recipient alive.
2. Reach the required living-recipient threshold.
3. After transformation, survive the vote and pressure remaining bread-holders.

- Feed players likely to survive until transformation.
- Avoid repeatedly feeding existing recipients.
- Reassess the remaining survivors after every death.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
