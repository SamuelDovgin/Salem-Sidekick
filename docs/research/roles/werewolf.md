# Werewolf

Default role family: **Neutral Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6584. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

On non-Full-Moon nights, mark a scent to keep receiving that player’s destinations. On Full Moon, make a Powerful Rampage or stay home and rampage there.

## Win condition

Eliminate opposing factions until only your allied Werewolf faction remains.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use scent information for strategic target selection.
2. Choose attacks with collateral and survival in mind.
3. Keep the scent log separate from the player attacked tonight.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Acquire useful scent coverage on eligible nights.
- **Middle:** Choose rampages without pretending persistent results are fresh personal visits.
- **Late:** Use the actual moon/action state and preserve a credible voting position.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Tracker is an information fit only when the reported target actually has scent coverage. Your visible attack destination may contradict the claimed tracking target.

## Read the information

Persistent information does not imply a fresh visit to that player. Attacking one house while reporting another player’s movement creates observer risk.

## Common mistakes

Do not invent new scent targets on every night. Do not assume roleblock simply disables Full Moon danger. Do not call the Tracker cover universally strong.

## Interactions and limits

Jail, roleblock, and Control have dangerous retaliation/redirection interactions. Use the actual Full Moon indicator rather than an unverified schedule.

## Worked decision

You marked #4 earlier but attack #9 tonight: a Tracker story about #4 can be informationally true while failing a check of your own movement.
