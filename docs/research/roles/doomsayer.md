# Doomsayer

Default role family: **Neutral Evil**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6436. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

From N2, confirm three simultaneous player/role guesses. All three must be correct in the same set.

**Key rule:** One wrong guess fails the whole set without identifying the mistake. Do not guess Town Investigative roles; Doom victims die later, not immediately on confirmation.

## Win condition

Win by correctly Dooming all three players in one set; you then leave Town in victory. You do not need to eliminate the rest of the town.

This assumes the default Doomsayer faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Collect claims and distinguish exact facts from guesses.
2. Prefer well-supported eligible guesses.
3. Confirm the complete set and continue observing if it fails.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Collect claims; no N1 confirmed guess.
- **Middle:** Build supported three-player sets and confirm them.
- **Late:** Do not wait until too few targets remain; failed feedback does not identify the wrong member.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Use a nonvisiting cover if needed; Oracle fits movement but has no true protective effect. Open Doomsayer claims expose your objective and are not universally tolerated.

## Read the information

At least one wrong guess does not reveal which one failed. Correct-looking claims can be deceptive.

## Common mistakes

Do not guess TI roles. Do not require the victims’ eventual deaths to count your win. Do not copy the outdated Neutral Outlier immunity claim.

## Interactions and limits

Current official UI fixes and the Fandom role page contradict wiki.gg’s no-Outlier-guess statement. Treat exact Outlier selection as disputed until client checked.

## Worked decision

4 Cleric, 7 Coven Utility and 9 Vigilante must all match in one submitted set. Two correct guesses are no win.
