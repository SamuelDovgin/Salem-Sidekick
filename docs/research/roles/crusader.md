# Crusader

Default role family: **Town Protective**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 3870. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Fortify grants Powerful Defense and attacks one random direct visitor with Basic Attack, regardless of whether that visitor is friendly.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose valuable targets with controlled visitor traffic.
2. Coordinate enough to reduce friendly casualties.
3. Record separately whether your target was attacked and you struck a visitor.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Avoid uncontrolled crowded protection calls.
- **Middle:** Coordinate a valuable low-traffic protection target.
- **Late:** Choose protection that preserves the necessary vote while reducing allied visitor casualties.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TP first where appropriate. Full claim when your visitor hazard matters to allied plans.

## Read the information

Your attack does not identify an evil visitor. Failure to kill can reflect defense rather than the absence of a visitor.

## Common mistakes

Do not pile onto a crowded TP/LO call unannounced. Do not treat a surviving visitor as a unique role. Do not assume you attack every visitor.

## Interactions and limits

Astral actions avoid visitor retaliation. A visiting protector can die in a rampage even when Fortify saves the target.

## Worked decision

Two allies intend to visit the same leader: coordinate another protection target rather than risking killing one of them.
