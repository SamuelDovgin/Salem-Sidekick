# Voodoo Master

Default role family: **Coven Utility**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6360. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Choose a doll: Silence stops next-day speech, Deafen hides next-day chat, or Blind hides night feedback.

**Key rule:** Do not target the same player on consecutive nights, even with a different doll. With the book, the selected effect can also carry an attack.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Disrupt information that matters for the next vote.
2. Coordinate target selection with team actions.
3. Anticipate victims reporting effects at the same destination you claim to visit.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Disrupt information useful to the next vote.
- **Middle:** Rotate legal targets and coordinate effect timing.
- **Late:** Consider which surviving speaker, reader, or investigator can actually change the outcome.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Visiting fallback covers require care because victims receive distinctive disruption. Do not treat Blind as equivalent to ordinary roleblocking.

## Read the information

Missing feedback is different from an ability failing. A victim’s silence is not evidence they voluntarily refused to claim.

## Common mistakes

Do not select the same player consecutively via different dolls. Do not interpret silence as inactivity. Do not claim to know information your cover cannot get.

## Interactions and limits

Day communication effects and night blindness differ. Voice-mode behavior must not leak into the text guide.

## Worked decision

Blind an information role to suppress its result; do not describe that effect as an ordinary roleblock or assume no victim report means failure.
