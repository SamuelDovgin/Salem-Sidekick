# Vampire

Default role family: **Neutral Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 4747. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Convert an undefended Town player into your Vampire faction, or Drain for a Basic attack. Conversion keeps their existing role.

**Key rule:** Three Convert charges. An ineligible Convert falls back to Drain. If you die, a surviving converted faction member can inherit the Vampire role and remaining charges.

## Win condition

Win with the Vampire faction by eliminating or converting its opponents. A single conversion is recruitment, not the win itself.

This assumes the default Vampire faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Convert useful eligible allies when survival and charges justify it.
2. Track remaining conversions and known faction members.
3. Update trust assumptions after each conversion.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Consider a valuable eligible conversion and limited charges.
- **Middle:** Use retained ally abilities and track succession.
- **Late:** Reassess faction survival after a Vampire death; the old role’s death may not end the faction.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting fallback such as Cleric or Psychic can fit your own movement, but cannot supply genuine protective or TI results.

## Read the information

A real Town-role demonstration remains real after conversion; its owner may now have a different win condition.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old age/youngest-biter rules. Do not assume converting any defended or non-Town player succeeds. Do not assume a dead Vampire means the faction is gone.

## Interactions and limits

Converted teammates can inherit Vampire after its death. The page flags a solo-detection bug; do not promise innocence as guaranteed.

## Worked decision

Convert a Cleric: they keep Barrier but now win with Vampires. Their survival alone does not prove recruitment.
