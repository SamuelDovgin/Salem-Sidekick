# Amnesiac

Default role family: **Town Support**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6546. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Automatically remember an eligible dead Town player’s true role; Town Power has priority. This is a role transition, not a selectable night visit.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Follow claims and deaths while waiting.
2. Record the moment the role changes.
3. Immediately switch to the remembered role’s strategy and action rules.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Learn claims while awaiting an eligible automatic Remember.
- **Middle:** After transition, follow the new role’s strategy and retain the old-role history.
- **Late:** Explain the timeline if its credibility matters; do not keep presenting as an unchanged Amnesiac.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TS when appropriate, then explain the remembered role and timing if challenged.

## Read the information

A hidden or altered death does not necessarily prevent Remember. Multiple Amnesiacs cannot all acquire the same Unique role.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim a chosen remember target like ToS1. Do not remain indefinitely unremembered after suitable deaths without explanation. Do not erase the role timeline.

## Interactions and limits

The lowest-number resolution for competing Unique remembers is documented; custom factions add edge cases. Treat unusual transitions as context-sensitive.

## Worked decision

A Town role dies and you remember it next morning: keep the original Amnesiac start and open that new guide.
