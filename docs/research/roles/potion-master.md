# Potion Master

Default role family: **Coven Utility**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5596. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Choose Barrier for Powerful Defense or Reveal to learn a player’s role with Coven. The book adds a Harmful attack potion.

**Key rule:** Use one mode for the night. Reveal also Illuminates the player, blocking Blood Ritual on them. Quiet Barrier feedback is not a save.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Compare information value with saving an important ally.
2. Share role information with the team.
3. Coordinate around Illumination and book responsibilities.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Reveal useful unknowns or save an ally when the threat is concrete.
- **Middle:** Use information for team targeting while choosing a sustainable potion-based cover.
- **Late:** Do not lose a necessary kill by assuming Barrier, Reveal, and Harmful all happen together.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Cleric naturally fits nights actually using Barrier. Sheriff or Investigator can use role knowledge only as an information-fit cover; exact-role knowledge is not their actual result.

## Read the information

Revealing a role does not reveal all its actions or crimes. A Seer story still lacks the usual two-visit pattern.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume you can heal, reveal, and attack simultaneously. Do not give a claim feedback you did not receive. Do not promise immediate Ritualist synergy.

## Interactions and limits

Illumination restricts Ritualist. Changing potion mode can undermine a protection cover if a claimed target dies.

## Worked decision

Reveal 4 supplies role knowledge; Barrier 4 supplies protection. The same visit cannot be reported as both modes without a real extra-action effect.
