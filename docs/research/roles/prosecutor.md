# Prosecutor

Default role family: **Town Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6514. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Prosecute during the day to force your chosen player’s execution without normal judgment.

**Key rule:** Two charges, at most one use per day from D2. Prosecuting Town removes the remaining charges. No night ability.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use evidence rather than impatience to select a target.
2. Consider whether ordinary voting already solves the case.
3. Preserve the remaining charge after a justified prosecution.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Save charges when ordinary discussion can resolve the case.
- **Middle:** Use a justified prosecution where voting cannot safely solve the problem.
- **Late:** Consider remaining charges and special hanging objectives before committing.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Start with TK rather than TPow; Deputy is the default temporary cover before Prosecute.

**End the cover when:** Disclose your exact role if it resolves a consequential claim dispute or enables trusted coordination. Use Prosecute when a supported case cannot safely be resolved by ordinary voting.

This is an editorial concealment default, not proof that a cover is best in every lobby. Keep real actions private and do not fabricate the claimed role’s effects.

## Read the information

This ability bypasses a normal trial; it does not make your target guilty. A Jester prosecution has special revenge consequences.

## Common mistakes

Do not prosecute merely to prove your role. Do not expect to use it during Tribunal. Do not use outdated Hangman-suicide advice.

## Interactions and limits

Prosecution counts as hanging for relevant neutral objectives. Ordinary day gunshots have different win-condition effects.

## Worked decision

A supported case against 9 can justify Prosecute; using it merely to prove your role can cost Town an ally.
