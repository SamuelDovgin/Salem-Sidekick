# Arsonist: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NK — Neutral Killing.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Douse directly and passively douse normal visitors; Ignite burns doused players with an astral Unstoppable attack. Taking no action can clean gasoline from yourself. NK — Neutral Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Douse directly and passively douse normal visitors; Ignite burns doused players with an astral Unstoppable attack. Taking no action can clean gasoline from yourself.

**Win condition:** Eliminate opposing factions until only your allied Arsonist faction remains.

Your actual alignment is NK — Neutral Killing. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Douse an eligible player as the usual opening. You are building later ignition value, not making an immediate ordinary attack. Prefer a useful nonally likely to survive until ignition; avoid obvious visitor traps.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Douse an eligible player as the usual opening. You are building later ignition value, not making an immediate ordinary attack.

**How to choose:** Prefer a useful nonally likely to survive until ignition; avoid obvious visitor traps. With no D1 lead, choose an eligible unknown. If you know you were Doused, weigh cleaning instead of assuming you are safe.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Build a real Douse set. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Privately separate direct Douse, passive visitor Douse, and self-Doused state.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Privately separate direct Douse, passive visitor Douse, and self-Doused state. Passive Douse information is not a complete Lookout investigation.

**What this does and does not establish:** New passive douses concern visitors to you, not a Lookout report on another house. A survival result is not a unique role identity.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the cover alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the cover alignment. A visiting cover can fit an active Douse night, but idle cleaning and astral Ignite change movement. Do not claim the same ordinary visit every night regardless of mode.

Your actual alignment is NK — Neutral Killing. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Psychic or Cleric can fit direct Douse movement as fallbacks. Oracle fits an ignition/idle night better than a direct-Douse night.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If another useful player needs Dousing, continue; if a meaningful ignition is available, weigh it against waiting and survival.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If another useful player needs Dousing, continue; if a meaningful ignition is available, weigh it against waiting and survival. Newly passive-Doused visitors do not automatically burn in that same ignition cycle.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Douse with a survival and vote plan.
- **Middle:** Track direct/passive progress and your own gasoline status.
- **Late:** Ignite when it creates a winning path rather than waiting for a maximal burn that never comes.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Cleric (TP): visiting fallback on douse; Oracle (TP): conditional nonvisit fit. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback on Douse.** An active Douse can fit a one-player Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** No protection exists; Clean and Ignite change the movement history.

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional nonvisit fit.** Idle cleaning or astral Ignite can fit the absence of a visible visit. **What can expose it:** An earlier direct Douse is not an Oracle action, and there is no actual Aegis.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not pretend Lookout can watch itself normally. Do not ignite while forgetting your own douse status. Do not count same-night fresh visitors as guaranteed immediate burns.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not pretend Lookout can watch itself normally. Do not ignite while forgetting your own douse status. Do not count same-night fresh visitors as guaranteed immediate burns.

**Important interaction:** Astral visitors avoid passive dousing. Multiple Arsonists can affect ignition and cleaning decisions.

**Worked decision:** You directly douse #4 and #7 becomes passively doused: a cover must not turn #7 into a visitor of #4 without evidence.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Build a real Douse set.
2. Track your own Doused risk.
3. Ignite for value with timing understood.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Build a real Douse set.
2. Track your own Doused risk.
3. Ignite for value with timing understood.

- Douse players with a plan for later survival and vote control.
- Track passive douses separately from your direct target.
- Ignite when it meaningfully improves your path rather than chasing maximum count.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
