# Executioner

Default role family: **Neutral Evil**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6370. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Get the assigned Town target hanged while you are alive. Other causes of death can turn you into Jester; a successful objective reduces future trial capacity.

## Win condition

Remain alive when the assigned target is hanged through a valid route. If they die otherwise, your role and objective change.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Create a plausible reason for public pressure rather than unexplained tunnel vision.
2. Maintain a claim consistent with timing and visits.
3. Switch goals immediately if you transform.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Build a plausible claim and reason for pressure.
- **Middle:** Seek the correct hanging route while remaining alive.
- **Late:** If the target dies otherwise, switch fully to Jester mechanics instead of persisting with an obsolete objective.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Sheriff, Psychic, or Investigator can carry a pressure story but require fabricated results and are not real ability fits. Nonvisiting covers reduce movement conflict.

## Read the information

Only the relevant hanging routes satisfy the objective; a night kill or ordinary day shot is not interchangeable.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume the target is chosen each night. Do not celebrate a non-hanging death as victory. Do not forget the Jester participation requirement after a transition.

## Interactions and limits

Prosecution and Tribunal can fulfill the hanging objective. The target pool excludes several role categories.

## Worked decision

Your target dies at night: stop the Executioner plan and record the Jester transition before following its new objective.
