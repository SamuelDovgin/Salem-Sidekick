# Town Power starting claims

Updated 19 October 2026. Defaults conceal the power category early; they are an editorial user-preferred opening policy, not a measured best strategy. Ordinary Town roles retain their truthful defaults.

| Actual role | Starting public alignment | Default exact-role cover | End concealment when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jailor | TK | Veteran | Disclose your Jailor identity when you need protection, must resolve a counterclaim, or can help Town act on a supported contradiction. Keep future prisoner choices and unneeded exact-role information private. |
| Mayor | TK | Veteran | Reveal before a decisive vote when your extra influence can secure a supported execution, prevent a mistaken execution of you, or establish needed leadership. Do not wait until on trial. |
| Marshal | TK | Deputy | Explain supported cases and coordinate a voting plan before activating Tribunal. Use it when Town can control the nominations, not solely to prove your claim. |
| Prosecutor | TK | Deputy | Disclose your exact role if it resolves a consequential claim dispute or enables trusted coordination. Use Prosecute when a supported case cannot safely be resolved by ordinary voting. |
| Monarch | TP | Trapper | Explain a consequential knighting, resolve a Monarch counterclaim, or share identity when it enables useful Town coordination. Separate proof of knighting from evidence of a Knight’s faction. |

The actual role and its controls stay unchanged. The selected claim, example/blank will and What to say panel follow the cover. The truthful role remains selectable as the third shortlist item and in the full Town picker. A manually selected claim survives reload or same-role navigation; a fresh role switch uses the new default. Existing drafts are preserved. On reload, an untouched prior default without saved public history can move to the new recommendation.

Monarch’s Trapper default assumes the recommended N1 Knight hold. If you instead Knight on N1, choose another cover before acting; a fresh Trapper cannot place a Trap on N1. Later claimed placements must match movement, and Knight effects are not trap/protection feedback.

Jailor/Mayor cannot create Veteran Alerts. Marshal/Prosecutor cannot create Deputy shots or private misses. All four start by claiming the broad TK alignment when asked, rather than automatically volunteering an exact role or TPow. Drop the cover if it obstructs Town or requires manufactured ability results.

Mechanics are supported by the existing role-card evidence and linked role references. Source mechanics establish the abilities and contradictions; the chosen ordering is an editorial concealment policy. Revealing TPow or the exact role remains useful in some games, especially for Jailor protection or coordinated use of public powers.

Validation: 63 unit tests, 129 browser checks and 10 targeted desktop/mobile cases passed. The targeted cases verify untouched-default migration, copyable cover headings, actual-role separation, deliberate truthful selection across reload and bounded layouts.
