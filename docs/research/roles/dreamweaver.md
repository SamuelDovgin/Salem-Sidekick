# Dreamweaver

Default role family: **Coven Deception**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5416. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Dreamweave Town targets; without a curing Town visit on the following night they develop Insomnia. Death or retraining of the Dreamweaver normally clears its effects.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Target useful opponents who are hard to cure safely.
2. Coordinate disruption while accounting for astral cures.
3. Track woven, cured, and insomniac states.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Choose a useful Town target and plan how a cure might happen.
- **Middle:** Coordinate disruption to convert pending effects into Insomnia.
- **Late:** Target an ability that can still change the game rather than an already spent role.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting cover can match destination, but visible Dreamweave status makes repeated correlation risky. Psychic is a fallback requiring invented vision data.

## Read the information

The status concerns faction eligibility; it does not authorize claiming a precise Town role.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old insanity behavior. Do not assume all targets are eligible. Do not treat every cured player as a confirmed permanently loyal ally.

## Interactions and limits

Poisoner and Voodoo Master can complicate cures. Official fixes make faction, not merely default role type, relevant.

## Worked decision

#4 is isolated and important: assess whether disruption prevents a cure before investing in a Dreamweave line.
