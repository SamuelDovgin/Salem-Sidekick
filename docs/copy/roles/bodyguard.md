# Bodyguard: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TP — Town Protective.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Guard against one direct harmful visitor, canceling that action and counterattacking. Successful interception endangers you. Two self-protection uses grant Basic Defense. TP — Town Protective.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Guard against one direct harmful visitor, canceling that action and counterattacking. Successful interception endangers you. Two self-protection uses grant Basic Defense.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TP — Town Protective. When the lobby asks for alignment, TP is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Guard one eligible player against an interceptable harmful visitor, if the ability is enabled. You are not granting an ordinary healing barrier. Prefer a credible useful ally who is plausibly threatened by a direct visitor.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Guard one eligible player against an interceptable harmful visitor, if the ability is enabled. You are not granting an ordinary healing barrier.

**How to choose:** Prefer a credible useful ally who is plausibly threatened by a direct visitor. With no reliable claim, choose an eligible unknown without calling them confirmed, or conserve self-protection unless you have a concrete personal threat.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose a reasoned protection target. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the guard attempt and any actual interception feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the guard attempt and any actual interception feedback. An uneventful night is not a save. Your death or their survival does not prove their faction, and astral or additional attacks can bypass the plan.

**What this does and does not establish:** A guard attempt is not a save. A death despite your guard can involve additional attacks or an astral bypass.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TP when an alignment is requested. Give Bodyguard details when coordination or a disputed event requires them; avoid unnecessarily publishing tonight’s exact guard target.

Claim TP when needed; give a full claim when coordination or a contradiction makes it useful. Keep exact protection plans out of unnecessary public discussion.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If D2 exposes a valuable ally threatened by a direct attack, consider guarding them and coordinating credible protection on yourself.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If D2 exposes a valuable ally threatened by a direct attack, consider guarding them and coordinating credible protection on yourself. If the expected threat is astral, revise the plan rather than promising an interception.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Protect a credible useful ally rather than a random loud claim.
- **Middle:** Coordinate protection on yourself if an interception is likely.
- **Late:** Consider the actual remaining attack types before sacrificing yourself for a role whose ability may be spent.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not say you grant a normal barrier. Do not expect to stop all attacks. Do not assume dying to protect someone proves their faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not say you grant a normal barrier. Do not expect to stop all attacks. Do not assume dying to protect someone proves their faction.

**Important interaction:** Barrier protection can preserve you during a counterattack. Astral attacks bypass interception; Shroud can make an otherwise friendly visitor harmful.

**Worked decision:** A revealed leader is threatened by ordinary killing: guarding them may trade your life for theirs, whereas an expected astral attack calls for another plan.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Choose a reasoned protection target.
2. Distinguish an attempt from an interception.
3. Reassess the actual attack type.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Choose a reasoned protection target.
2. Distinguish an attempt from an interception.
3. Reassess the actual attack type.

- Guard a credible high-value ally likely to be attacked.
- Consider protection on you when coordinating a chain.
- Reassess whether the expected attack is interceptable.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
