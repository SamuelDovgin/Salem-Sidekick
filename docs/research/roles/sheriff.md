# Sheriff

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5148. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Search one player at night for Innocent or Suspicious feedback.

**Key rule:** This checks apparent suspicion, not exact role or guaranteed allegiance. Some enemies appear Innocent, and framing can make allies Suspicious.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Check unresolved claims rather than already demonstrated roles.
2. Post the result with its night and target.
3. Compare suspicious feedback with claims and independent evidence.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Check an unresolved player; establish a prompt reporting habit.
- **Middle:** Compare results with the evolving faction threats rather than repeatedly checking plausible innocents.
- **Late:** Combine vote math, claims, and exceptions; an innocent result still cannot solve the endgame alone.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim Sheriff and publish results promptly, normally from D2. Give the result before arguing its meaning.

## Read the information

Innocent leaves many evils possible, including Neutral Killings and the bookholder. Suspicious can come from framing, and identifies more than Coven.

## Common mistakes

Do not call an innocent result a confirmed ally. Do not label every suspicious result Coven. Do not hide results until pressured without a reason.

## Interactions and limits

Enchant and Reap can change apparent guilt; Illusion can conceal Coven. Solo Apocalypse detection differs from a multi-member faction.

## Worked decision

A roleblocked Search is no result. Do not enter Innocent merely because no suspicion message appeared.
