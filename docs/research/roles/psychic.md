# Psychic

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6298. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Visit a chosen player included in your vision. Odd nights give two names with at least one Good; even nights give three with at least one Evil.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. On Good nights consider a genuinely suspicious target.
2. On Evil nights consider a reliably established Town reference.
3. Publish every vision with its Good or Evil label.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Report the first Good pair without overclearing either name.
- **Middle:** Use established references where legal and resolve earlier visions.
- **Late:** Treat no-vision feedback literally and use previous constraints rather than inventing a new eligible vision.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim Psychic and post the vision from D2. Mark the player you deliberately selected separately from the other names.

## Read the information

At least one is a group condition, not exactly one. Do not identify a single culprit without additional evidence.

## Common mistakes

Do not reverse night parity. Do not omit the selected target. Do not silently turn a group lead into a confirmed accusation.

## Interactions and limits

Enchant, Illusion, and Reap can affect classifications. Late-game no-vision messages are inconsistent within the source and are not modeled as hard rules.

## Worked decision

N2 Evil [4,7,9], with #4 independently Town: investigate #7 and #9; do not choose one arbitrarily.
