# Investigator

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5154. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Investigate a player for Murder, Trespassing, both crimes, or No Crime.

**Key rule:** Murder concerns an attack that night. Trespassing concerns an opposing-faction visit at some point in the game. Neither result alone reveals allegiance.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Check players with disputed action histories.
2. Ask crime-positive targets for their actions and destinations.
3. Compare against known attacks and other observations.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use the shorter visit history to ask concrete questions about Trespassing.
- **Middle:** Compare crime with actual action histories and independent observations.
- **Late:** Avoid expecting accumulated Trespassing to distinguish everyone; prioritize contradictions and immediate attack evidence.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim with the actual crime feedback, normally from D2. Separate your result from your accusation.

## Read the information

A crime does not establish evil. Town attackers and cross-faction Town visits can explain crime. Murder and Trespassing are separate concepts; preserve the feedback actually shown.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old blood-only templates. Do not invent crime for a nonvisiting action. Do not treat No Crime as a faction clearance.

## Interactions and limits

Enchant, Reap, Illusion, and Shroud complicate interpretation. The source has conflicting execution examples; avoid a hard Jailor-Murder rule.

## Worked decision

A Town player can have Trespassing; No Crime does not mean the checked player is harmless.
