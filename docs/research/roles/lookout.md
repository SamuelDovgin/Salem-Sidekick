# Lookout

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6340. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Watch a player’s house and learn the identities of its visible visitors.

**Key rule:** You see who came to the watched house, not where its owner went or the visitors’ roles. Astral visits are invisible.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Watch valuable players likely to draw informative traffic.
2. Compare visitor claims against reported effects.
3. Share contradictions without assuming every visitor attacked.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Watch a plausible useful target while accounting for visitor hazards.
- **Middle:** Use known traffic and claims to test who explains reported effects.
- **Late:** Choose the house whose traffic would most resolve the remaining uncertainty, not simply the loudest player.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Normally full claim and report useful information early. You may briefly hold a visitor list to test claims; avoid losing the information entirely.

## Read the information

A visitor list is not a killer list. No visible visitors does not exclude astral effects or establish that the target was safe.

## Common mistakes

Do not swap visitors with destinations. Do not claim you watched yourself normally. Do not expose protective identities unnecessarily.

## Interactions and limits

Crowded calls can attract rampages. The page contains an obsolete Camouflage reference; current baseline does not use it.

## Worked decision

Watch 4; see 7 and 9 visiting 4. That says nothing about whom 4 visited.
