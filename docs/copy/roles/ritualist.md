# Ritualist: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CK — Coven Killing.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

Two Blood Ritual attempts guess an eligible exact role for an astral Unstoppable attack. TIs and Illuminated targets cannot normally be ritualed. CK — Coven Killing.

Two Blood Ritual attempts guess an eligible exact role for an astral Unstoppable attack. TIs and Illuminated targets cannot normally be ritualed.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. ## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

If you do not have a well-supported eligible role guess, hold the Ritual. You have two attempts and cannot guess TI; a fresh N1 often lacks useful evidence. Use an exact supported role claim or team-provided role knowledge only after checking eligibility and Illuminated status.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If you do not have a well-supported eligible role guess, hold the Ritual. You have two attempts and cannot guess TI; a fresh N1 often lacks useful evidence.

**How to choose:** Use an exact supported role claim or team-provided role knowledge only after checking eligibility and Illuminated status. With no evidence, do not spend a guess on a random number/role pair. A book attack is a separate alternative.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Preserve both attempts without evidence. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the actual outcome and remaining attempts.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the actual outcome and remaining attempts. A wrong guess does not produce the old public exposure event; lack of that event is not proof your guess was correct.

**What this does and does not establish:** A wrong guess spends a charge without proving the target’s alternate role. Your private role certainty is not a result an Oracle could report.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Use D2 claims and Coven information for a legal exact guess.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use D2 claims and Coven information for a legal exact guess. If no supported guess exists, hold again or use the available book alternative. Do not force a TI or Illuminated target into the selector.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Collect exact eligible role knowledge without wasting guesses.
- **Middle:** Spend attempts on supported targets after checking Illumination.
- **Late:** Choose ritual or ordinary attack based on remaining defenses and win requirements.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional astral fit; Deputy (TK): difficult demonstration claim. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional astral fit.** A ritual or inactive night can fit a no-visible-visit cover. **What can expose it:** No actual Aegis is supplied; direct book attacks can conflict.

**Deputy — TK (Town Killing); Difficult demonstration claim.** Neither role requires an ordinary N1 visit. **What can expose it:** Town may reasonably ask for the one day shot, which you cannot perform.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use old three-charge or public-failure-exposure rules. Do not guess TIs. Do not assume a Potion Master reveal immediately supplies a usable ritual target.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use old three-charge or public-failure-exposure rules. Do not guess TIs. Do not assume a Potion Master reveal immediately supplies a usable ritual target.

**Important interaction:** Illumination lasts conditionally and matters to target eligibility. Ordinary book attacks and Blood Ritual are alternative actions.

**Worked decision:** A public eligible exact role is known: check defenses and Illumination before committing one of only two attempts.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
