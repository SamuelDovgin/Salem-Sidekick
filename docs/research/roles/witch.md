# Witch

Default role family: **Coven Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5401. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Control a first target toward a second target, learn role information and steal feedback. You normally visit only the first target, not both selected numbers.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Disrupt a consequential opponent or exploit a known ability.
2. Share acquired information with allies.
3. Anticipate control notifications linking your visits to the effect.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Acquire useful information or disrupt an important action.
- **Middle:** Use known roles without breaking your own cover’s movement story.
- **Late:** Compare controlling a vote-changing ability with direct book killing; anticipate immunity and charges.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Your information can support a story but control is conspicuous. Treat Sheriff/Psychic covers as conditional, not natural undetectable equivalents.

## Read the information

A learned role can still be affected by special framing. Knowing a role does not supply the complete history another TI would observe.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim Seer merely because you selected two numbers. Do not assume consecutive control works normally. Do not omit the required second target when attacking with book.

## Interactions and limits

Immunity, Hangover, and limited-use abilities constrain Control. Revealing information can impose Illumination, relevant to Ritualist.

## Worked decision

You control #4 into #9: a Tracker following you normally sees #4, so a will claiming two visible Seer visits is fragile.
