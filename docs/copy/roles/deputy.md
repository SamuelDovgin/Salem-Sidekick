# Deputy: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TK — Town Killing.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

One day shot from D2. A hit kills an eligible target; a miss can arise from Town status or defense. A miss does not publicly demonstrate the ability. TK — Town Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

One day shot from D2. A hit kills an eligible target; a miss can arise from Town status or defense. A miss does not publicly demonstrate the ability.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no night ability. Save your single day shot and prepare to question D2 claims; you cannot shoot on D1. Read D1 for a concrete contradiction worth asking about tomorrow.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no night ability. Save your single day shot and prepare to question D2 claims; you cannot shoot on D1.

**How to choose:** Read D1 for a concrete contradiction worth asking about tomorrow. With no information, leave your target undecided. “No target yet” is correct; a quiet player or a request to prove yourself is not a shooting case.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Build one supported case before shooting. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

### A concrete opening, with no forced shot

**Setup:** You are Deputy in the normal text profile. D1 has no useful claims. You have not used High Noon.

1. **N1:** No ability target is required. Start an action line, `N1: No night ability`, and a separate question list. Do not select someone merely to fill a box.
2. **D2:** Suppose #6 posts `Tracker: N1 #9 -> #4`, while #9 claims `Veteran, stayed home; no role change`. Ask #9 to explain that specific movement conflict. Check for redirection, role changes, mistaken identity, or a false Tracker before treating it as settled. This is a case to investigate, not an automatic shooting instruction.
3. **Public alignment:** If the lobby asks for alignment, answer `TK`. If explaining the full role is useful, say `Deputy; shot unused`. Neither TP nor TS is correct for your actual role.
4. **Decision:** If the case remains unresolved, hold the shot. If independent evidence supports an enemy, the day ability is enabled, nobody is on the stand, and using the shot has value, consider it. One shot is available; using it to prove yourself can waste it.
5. **Outcome branch:** Suppose you fire and get Miss. Log `D2: Shot 9 — Miss`. The charge is spent. A Town target, sufficient defense, or other relevant context can explain the miss; do not announce `9 confirmed Town`.
6. **N2:** Still no night ability. If the bullet remains, update the case. If spent, use claims, questions, and votes rather than waiting for another bullet.

**What the beginner can now answer:** Do I have an N1 button? No. What is useful instead? Prepare an honest history and a specific follow-up. What do I claim? TK. Must I shoot D2? No. Does a miss clear the target? No.

**Why this is useful:** It replaces an impossible first-night action with a legitimate preparation task and explains the irreversible decision before the player spends the charge. Success for this lesson is a reasoned hold or supported shot, not a required kill.


## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Write “N1: No night ability.” Collect D2 claims and results before deciding about the shot.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Write “N1: No night ability.” Collect D2 claims and results before deciding about the shot. A night death is not evidence you personally tested anyone.

**What this does and does not establish:** A miss does not prove the target is Town. Another player can falsely claim credit for a public shot.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TK (Town Killing) in a vote-for-alignment. Ask the player to answer the specific contradiction. From D2, shoot only when the evidence and likely defense make the one shot useful; holding is a complete valid turn.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Claim TK where useful. A successful shot has a public event but does not automatically announce the shooter’s identity.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

There is still no night action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

There is still no night action. If you held the shot, reassess the case after D2 and new results. If you used it, record hit/miss and keep contributing with votes and questions; the bullet does not replenish.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Preserve the lone shot until a supported target exists.
- **Middle:** Use it where it meaningfully changes the evidence or vote.
- **Late:** A remaining shot can be decisive, but defense means a miss does not establish Town.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use outdated shoot-Town-and-die rules. Do not expect multiple bullets. Do not treat a private miss as hard confirmation.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use outdated shoot-Town-and-die rules. Do not expect multiple bullets. Do not treat a private miss as hard confirmation.

**Important interaction:** Enchant/Reap can change eligibility. Early Neutral Evil defense and other protection can explain a miss.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**
