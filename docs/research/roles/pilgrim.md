# Pilgrim

Default role family: **Town Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6432. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

You have no active ability. Help Town by comparing claims, asking useful questions and voting.

**Key rule:** No night targets or special results. Public observations belong in day notes, and Pilgrim must be permitted by the setup.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Check whether the roledeck allows Pilgrim.
2. Keep a concise claim and evidence list.
3. Ask specific questions and vote with reasons.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Learn the deck, claims, and voting rhythm.
- **Middle:** Ask specific questions and organize contradictions honestly.
- **Late:** Explain vote choices from public evidence; do not invent night proof to compensate for lacking an ability.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim the role or alignment based on the known deck. A legal uncounterclaimed slot can support credibility but does not universally prove Town.

## Read the information

No action supplies no investigative result. Your social reads should remain visibly separate from claimed mechanical facts.

## Common mistakes

Do not fabricate results as the beginner default. Do not claim the role in an ineligible setup. Do not treat silence as helpful play.

## Interactions and limits

Custom factions and hidden deaths complicate slot reasoning. You can remain useful even without an active button.

## Worked decision

“7 changed their N1 target” records a public contradiction; it is not a private investigative result.
