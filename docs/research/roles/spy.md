# Spy

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6346. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Place a permanent Bug on a house to learn about evil-role visitors. Old Bugs can report on later nights.

**Key rule:** Record the bugged house and reported role separately. A quiet Bug is not a Town clear, and a role report does not give the visitor’s player number.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Bug players likely to attract useful traffic.
2. Keep earlier living bugged players in view.
3. Report the bugged target, trigger night, and exact role information received.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Begin a bug network on informative likely targets.
- **Middle:** Track triggers from all surviving bugged houses.
- **Late:** Interpret absent triggers cautiously; combine the accumulated log with direct evidence and votes.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim with relevant triggers or an honest no-trigger log. Do not represent an absence as a positive discovery.

## Read the information

A trigger reports evil activity, not necessarily a player number. Framed or converted visitors can trigger as their real role.

## Common mistakes

Do not use ToS1 global visit logs. Do not use the obsolete catch-all event-bug template. Do not forget that prior bugs remain active.

## Interactions and limits

The wiki gives a narrow trigger description; exhaustiveness and some astral/frame interactions need care. No-trigger feedback is not universal proof of safety.

## Worked decision

Bug 4 on N1; on N3 it reports a Jinx visit to 4 even though you placed tonight’s new Bug elsewhere.
