## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Enchant a useful nonally to distort relevant evidence. Treat optional Alterate as a separate limited-charge commitment. Prefer a player likely to be investigated or involved in a useful false case.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Enchant a useful nonally to distort relevant evidence. Treat optional Alterate as a separate limited-charge commitment.

**How to choose:** Prefer a player likely to be investigated or involved in a useful false case. With no D1 lead, choose a legal nonally but do not spend Alterate blindly unless there is a concrete death/forgery plan.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Frame for a specific evidence channel. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record your real target and any chosen forged death details privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record your real target and any chosen forged death details privately. If the Alterate victim survives, the charge can still be spent; do not assume it was free because no forged grave appeared.

**What this does and does not establish:** Death-role and will information may be manipulated; a fabricated graveyard result is not reliable proof of earlier claims.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Use D2 reactions to decide whether the frame creates a plausible case or needs abandoning.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use D2 reactions to decide whether the frame creates a plausible case or needs abandoning. Do not claim Enchant changes Tracker, Lookout, Coroner, or Spy observations when it does not.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Establish a coherent cover and plausible framing opportunities.
- **Middle:** Coordinate deceptive evidence with actual investigative attention and deaths.
- **Late:** Avoid an impossible forged will when only a few legal roles/actions remain.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Sheriff (TI): conditional frame story; Investigator (TI): conditional frame story. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Conditional frame story.** A frame can support a suspicious-looking case when another detection effect is involved. **What can expose it:** Your frame is not your own Sheriff result; unaffected observations and timing can expose it.

**Investigator — TI (Town Investigative); Conditional frame story.** Framing can align with some crime evidence in a story. **What can expose it:** You still need the current Murder/Trespassing model, and Enchant does not alter all observation channels.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not frame movement that observers can directly verify. Do not assume all TIs are fooled. Do not spend Alterate on a survivor without recognizing the cost.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not frame movement that observers can directly verify. Do not assume all TIs are fooled. Do not spend Alterate on a survivor without recognizing the cost.

**Important interaction:** Coroner, Tracker, Lookout, and Spy have resistance to this kind of information alteration. Reap can override some framing.

**Worked decision:** You alter a planned victim: prepare a chronologically plausible will instead of creating impossible ability timing.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
