# Seer: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Compare two eligible players at night for Friends or Enemies. You normally visit both; previously compared and revealed targets have restrictions. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Compare two eligible players at night for Friends or Enemies. You normally visit both; previously compared and revealed targets have restrictions.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Compare two eligible players for Friends or Enemies. Keep both numbers and do not reuse restricted targets later. If one player has independent support and the other is unresolved, that pair can be useful.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Compare two eligible players for Friends or Enemies. Keep both numbers and do not reuse restricted targets later.

**How to choose:** If one player has independent support and the other is unresolved, that pair can be useful. With no opening evidence, choose two eligible unknowns; neither is a trustworthy anchor yet.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Keep the complete pair history. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the pair and the literal result, or an interrupted action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the pair and the literal result, or an interrupted action. Friends is apparent faction compatibility; Enemies is an apparent difference. Neither identifies a particular evil by itself.

**What this does and does not establish:** Friends means apparent faction compatibility, not necessarily two Townies. Enemies means an apparent mismatch, not which player is evil.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Post “Seer (TI), N1: [a]/[b] — [result].” Request both claims and explain the pair condition.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Post “Seer (TI), N1: [a]/[b] — [result].” Request both claims and explain the pair condition. Do not push one player just because their number comes first.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Full claim Seer and publish the pair and result. Neither pair order nor a confident tone makes one member guilty.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Choose a new legal pair using D2 evidence and the recorded target history.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose a new legal pair using D2 evidence and the recorded target history. If the first pair becomes explained, pursue another uncertainty; if it remains disputed, seek independent evidence rather than illegally repeating the comparison.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Record your first legal pair and both claims.
- **Middle:** Choose new eligible players whose relationship adds useful information.
- **Late:** Work from existing comparisons; do not invent repeat checks when legal targets run short.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not reuse targets as though checks were unlimited. Do not claim a pair when blocked. Do not call all two-visit players Seer.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not reuse targets as though checks were unlimited. Do not claim a pair when blocked. Do not call all two-visit players Seer.

**Important interaction:** Framing and Illusion matter. Bestow can hide visits; Catalyst can create extra visits and War also visits twice.

**Worked decision:** N1 #4/#9 Enemies: request both claims and compare them before selecting a push.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Keep the complete pair history.
2. Collect both claims.
3. Resolve a pair with independent evidence.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Keep the complete pair history.
2. Collect both claims.
3. Resolve a pair with independent evidence.

- Compare unresolved players with useful independent evidence.
- Maintain the full history to avoid illegal target reuse.
- Resolve pairs by gathering information about both sides.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
