# Monarch

Default role family: **Town Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6477. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Knight a player at night to give them a lasting extra vote. You have two Knight charges.

**Key rule:** You gain Basic Defense while a Knight is alive. Knighthood grants voting power, not proof that its recipient is Town.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Knight players with strong faction evidence.
2. Consider vote consequences before adding weight.
3. Track living Knights and do not assume immediate first-night defense.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Prioritize confidence in allegiance before giving permanent votes.
- **Middle:** Use Knights strategically and monitor their survival.
- **Late:** Calculate how added vote weight changes control rather than treating knighting as an automatic confirmation.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Start with TP rather than TPow; Trapper is the default temporary cover for the recommended N1 Knight hold.

**End the cover when:** Explain a consequential knighting, resolve a Monarch counterclaim, or share identity when it enables useful Town coordination. Separate proof of knighting from evidence of a Knight’s faction.

This is an editorial concealment default, not proof that a cover is best in every lobby. Keep real actions private and do not fabricate the claimed role’s effects.

## Read the information

A visible Knight demonstrates knighting occurred, not who performed it without other evidence, nor the Knight’s loyalty.

## Common mistakes

Do not knight an impressive speaker solely on confidence. Do not assume Knights are unvoteable. Do not assume a future Knight saves you tonight.

## Interactions and limits

Charges are not spent if the selected player dies before knighting completes. Corpse users can reproduce knighting effects.

## Worked decision

Knight 4; check that the effect completed. If no Knight remains alive, do not assume you still have its defense.
