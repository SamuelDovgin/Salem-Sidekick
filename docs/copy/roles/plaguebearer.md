# Plaguebearer: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NA — Neutral Apocalypse.** Default faction: Apocalypse. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Infect players; contact spreads infection and provides propagation information. Adjacent neighbors begin infected in the current baseline. Infect all living opponents to become Pestilence. NA — Neutral Apocalypse.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Infect players; contact spreads infection and provides propagation information. Adjacent neighbors begin infected in the current baseline. Infect all living opponents to become Pestilence.

**Win condition:** Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

Your actual alignment is NA — Neutral Apocalypse. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Check the seeded infections, then Infect an eligible player not already infected. Seeded neighbors are not players you visited on N1. Prefer a likely active visitor or well-connected house that can spread infection.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check the seeded infections, then Infect an eligible player not already infected. Seeded neighbors are not players you visited on N1.

**How to choose:** Prefer a likely active visitor or well-connected house that can spread infection. With no D1 evidence, choose an eligible uninfected unknown outside obvious visitor bait; there is no guaranteed optimal target.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Expand beyond seeded infections. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Privately separate initial infections, your chosen Infect, and later spread.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Privately separate initial infections, your chosen Infect, and later spread. Spread can suggest contact but is not a complete Lookout report with guaranteed visitor identities.

**What this does and does not establish:** Already infected players do not create a fresh infection event for every visit. Infection history is broader and less direct than a clean observer log.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Infect a new eligible uninfected player after updating the map from actual feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Infect a new eligible uninfected player after updating the map from actual feedback. If transformation occurs, read Pestilence’s current stack/death rules rather than using old automatic-rampage advice.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Separate seeded infections from useful new spread.
- **Middle:** Target remaining uninfected opponents and use propagation carefully.
- **Late:** After Pestilence, manage stack-based pressure and the day vote rather than pretending it is a conventional rampage.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

**After transformation:** Pestilence: infection becomes stack-based danger; reaching three stacks is lethal while the effect is active. Its own spread action can force maximum stacks and is not a conventional harmful attack. Do not use the old ToS1 passive-rampage model.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Lookout (TI): partial information fit; difficult; Psychic (TI): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Lookout — TI (Town Investigative); Partial information fit; difficult.** Some spread can suggest visitor contact. **What can expose it:** Initial neighbors and incomplete propagation are not a complete visitor list; your own destination must also fit.

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** A direct Infect can fit one selected player in a vision. **What can expose it:** No real vision exists, and you must maintain group counts/parity without leaking infection knowledge.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not count starting neighbor infections as N1 visitors. Do not assert every missing infection means no visit. Do not publish unobserved destinations.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not count starting neighbor infections as N1 visitors. Do not assert every missing infection means no visit. Do not publish unobserved destinations.

**Important interaction:** Astral contact does not spread infection normally. Solo/multi-member Apocalypse changes detection and defense.

**Worked decision:** #4 becomes newly infected through contact: identify what the actual feedback establishes before writing a numbered Tracker result.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Expand beyond seeded infections.
2. Track actual spread without inventing visits.
3. Switch correctly to Pestilence when notified.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Expand beyond seeded infections.
2. Track actual spread without inventing visits.
3. Switch correctly to Pestilence when notified.

- Choose uninfected players likely to spread contact.
- Distinguish seeded infection from nightly evidence.
- Use propagation information without inventing a complete visitor list.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
