# Pirate

Default role family: **Neutral Evil**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6542. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Three players are Landlubbers; survive until all die. Scour identifies target status, and three Plunders are available; killing a non-Landlubber removes remaining Plunders.

## Win condition

Remain alive until all assigned Landlubber players are dead, regardless of who killed them.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use role clues and Scour to locate actual targets.
2. Plunder when justified rather than firing blindly.
3. Reassess when a target changes role or dies by another cause.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use clues and Scour rather than blind attacks.
- **Middle:** Preserve Plunder by checking likely Landlubbers.
- **Late:** If safe to disclose, explain aligned interests conditionally; surviving all target deaths is what matters.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting cover can match Scour, but it produces no normal TI result. Open Pirate disclosure is conditional and can invite attacks from your targets.

## Read the information

Landlubber identity belongs to the player; role changes need not change the objective. Death by other means can still advance it.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old duel/minigame templates. Do not plunder N1. Do not assume your basic attack penetrates every defense.

## Interactions and limits

At least one starting target is ordinarily Town and one opposes Town, with setup exceptions. Cursed Soul is excluded from the initial target pool.

## Worked decision

If the shown target role has multiple claimants, Scour a plausible one rather than assuming the first claimant is the Landlubber.
