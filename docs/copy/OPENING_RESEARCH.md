# Research decisions behind the opening copy

Reviewed 2026-10-15. The [source manifest](../research/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json) records revisions for all 60 roles. The role dossiers preserve the reviewed mechanics and citations. This pass used those full source texts again for opening-state constraints, then authored concrete actions, target criteria, dawn handling, D2 disclosure, and follow-up goals for every role. These priorities are strategic inference, not official winning sequences.

## What was changed for beginners

| Research finding | Copy decision | Concrete consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Some roles have no legal ordinary N1 action | Name the inactive state and give a preparation task | Deputy prepares a D2 case; Coroner waits for a valid autopsy; no invented first-night check |
| Some abilities start with preparation | Put the prerequisite before a numbered target | Trapper Builds; Vigilante Loads |
| Information is frequently conditional | Pair literal results with a limited conclusion and next question | Sheriff Innocent leaves evils possible; Seer Enemies does not identify which player is evil |
| Actual role family and public claim family differ | Display both with full names and shorthand | Actual Poisoner CU can claim Tavern Keeper TS; actual Baker NA can claim Cleric TP |
| A single action overlap can support a cover | Explain the overlap precisely | Poisoner Drink can produce Tavern Keeper-like roleblock; Wildling can publish only the Tracker or Lookout subset |
| Missing genuine effects expose some simple covers | Label the absent effect explicitly | Baker Feed does not provide Cleric Barrier; Jinx does not provide Crusader defense |
| Movement and timing can break an information-based story | Check the whole history, not only plausible text | Potion Master knowledge does not create two Seer visits; Serial Killer cannot explain N1 as a normal Vigilante shot |
| Progress often depends on real outcomes | Track surviving recipients, actual kills, or observed totals | Baker loses living progress when a recipient dies; Berserker does not level from an attempted kill |
| Roles change state | Put the next action behind the actual state notification | Mirror before Unleash; Feed before Famine/Starve; acquired role after actual Remember/Swap |
| Sources contain obsolete strategies and unresolved interactions | Prefer current mechanics/official corrections and qualify the rest | No old delayed poison, marriage Admirer, Pirate duels, or shoot-Town-and-die Deputy advice |

## Rechecked examples and source precedence

- **Deputy:** reviewed revision 6299 specifies Town Killing, one D2-or-later shot, private misses, no automatic shooter reveal, and Town/defense miss conditions with framing exceptions. The official [Season 4 changes](https://www.townofsalem2.com/news/nn2khtionvzvslwh2lcs8nmh64t8hr) corroborate the revised shot. The guide deliberately avoids the wiki strategy’s overconfident suggestion that a miss can clear a player. [Deputy](https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Deputy_(ToS2)).
- **Sheriff:** reviewed revision 5148 supports limited Innocent/Suspicious feedback, not exact faction identification. Full TI disclosure normally begins D2; strategy asks for claims and independent evidence. [Sheriff](https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Sheriff_(ToS2)).
- **Poisoner:** reviewed revision 6228 separates Drink roleblock, Hungover, Smog access obstruction, and the book attack. That makes Tavern Keeper a reasoned Drink cover and Socialite a conditional Smog resemblance. Neither makes the player Town. [Poisoner](https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Poisoner_(ToS2)).
- **Baker:** reviewed revision 6503 plus the [secondary Baker account](https://town-of-salem.fandom.com/wiki/Baker) support the ordinary three-other-living-recipient progress model, with a reduced-population exception. Bread does not provide a pre-transform heal. Avoid importing the different BetterTOS2 bread modes. [Vanilla Baker](https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Baker_(ToS2)).
- **Illusionist:** the official [Flexible Factions patch](https://www.townofsalem.io/town-of-salem-2-flexible-factions-update-patch-notes-r1-5-11/) removes the older interaction that hid Illusion from Bodyguard, Trapper, and Deputy. Current copy follows the official correction instead of the stale strategy paragraph.
- **Coroner:** the reviewed source separates daytime Autopsy from nighttime Examine. No ordinary N1 autopsy record means no useful initial comparison; later No Match concerns accumulated records, not every evil. Special counterattack cases remain uncertain. [Coroner](https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Coroner_(ToS2)).

## Claim reasoning to teach, not a fixed best-claim list

The useful pattern is **actual action → visible movement → target feedback → claimed information → next-night obligation**. A cover can fit one part while failing another. Every claim remains selectable so the player can choose a difficult or intentional alternate story; the guide explains the cost.

For example, Poisoner → Tavern Keeper has genuine effect overlap, while Baker → Cleric mainly has movement overlap. Potion Master → Seer may have enough role knowledge to invent a result but still lacks the normal two-player visit. Deputy and Mayor have simple-looking inactive nights but create demonstration pressure that a fake cannot reproduce. These distinctions are more useful to a beginner than an unsupported “amazing fit” badge.

The [complete original framework](../research/FAKE_CLAIM_LOGIC.md) supplies seven patterns, 31 opposing-role rows, 29 Town cover profiles, and worked cases. The new [shared interface text](SHARED_INTERFACE_COPY.md) turns the method into a short inline lesson. Each role’s actual copy applies it to named candidates with alignment labels and exposure notes.

## Limits of this research

No installed build or live match was inspected. The baseline is vanilla text chat with normal factions; current setup and actual feedback win over a generic instruction. Source conflicts in [the uncertainty register](../research/UNCERTAINTIES.md) remain bounded and are not turned into hard guilty/innocent rules. Opening plans are complete authored starting guidance; measured usability, win rates, and exhaustive custom-faction behavior have not been established.
