# Seer

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5395. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Compare two players and learn whether their apparent factions are Friends or Enemies.

**Key rule:** You visit both players. You cannot use yourself, revealed Town members or any player you have already selected earlier in the game.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Compare unresolved players with useful independent evidence.
2. Maintain the full history to avoid illegal target reuse.
3. Resolve pairs by gathering information about both sides.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Record your first legal pair and both claims.
- **Middle:** Choose new eligible players whose relationship adds useful information.
- **Late:** Work from existing comparisons; do not invent repeat checks when legal targets run short.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim Seer and publish the pair and result. Neither pair order nor a confident tone makes one member guilty.

## Read the information

Friends means apparent faction compatibility, not necessarily two Townies. Enemies means an apparent mismatch, not which player is evil.

## Common mistakes

Do not reuse targets as though checks were unlimited. Do not claim a pair when blocked. Do not call all two-visit players Seer.

## Interactions and limits

Framing and Illusion matter. Bestow can hide visits; Catalyst can create extra visits and War also visits twice.

## Worked decision

4 and 9 are Enemies: their apparent factions differ. It does not say which player opposes Town.
