# Illusionist: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CD — Coven Deception.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Cast astral Illusion, use one team-wide Phantasm, or Disguise your shown death role/faction. Ordinary book attacks are direct and replace the protection choice. CD — Coven Deception.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Cast astral Illusion, use one team-wide Phantasm, or Disguise your shown death role/faction. Ordinary book attacks are direct and replace the protection choice.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CD — Coven Deception. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Illusion a Coven ally whose activity may attract detection, or choose another legal mode for a concrete reason. Do not assume Illusion and a book attack both happen. Coordinate with the acting Coven member.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Illusion a Coven ally whose activity may attract detection, or choose another legal mode for a concrete reason. Do not assume Illusion and a book attack both happen.

**How to choose:** Coordinate with the acting Coven member. With no team plan, ask who needs concealment rather than randomly using the single teamwide Phantasm. Disguise is a separate death-appearance choice.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Coordinate concealment with a real ally action. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the mode and actual feedback privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the mode and actual feedback privately. Current official changes mean Illusion does not hide an ally from Bodyguard, Trapper, or Deputy; older concealment advice is unsafe.

**What this does and does not establish:** A favorable investigative appearance is not permanent allegiance proof. A death disguise can corrupt role-slot deductions.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Choose protection from detection based on D2 investigations and the team’s next action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose protection from detection based on D2 investigations and the team’s next action. If receiving the book, revise the movement/effect story. Save Phantasm unless its broader value justifies the one charge.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Protect a strategically useful ally from detection.
- **Middle:** Assess Phantasm against likely investigative pressure.
- **Late:** Reconcile the cover with direct book attacks and the death disguise; keep the will coherent.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional astral fit; Deputy (TK): difficult demonstration claim. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional astral fit.** Ordinary Illusion is astral, compatible with no visible visit. **What can expose it:** Oracle’s role targeting and real protection are different; the book can introduce direct movement.

**Deputy — TK (Town Killing); Difficult demonstration claim.** An ordinary inactive-looking night does not require a personal visit. **What can expose it:** You cannot shoot as Deputy, and Illusion no longer bypasses the current Deputy/Bodyguard/Trapper interactions.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim a normal visit you did not make. Do not assume Illusion fools every ability. Do not assume it and a normal book attack both occur.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim a normal visit you did not make. Do not assume Illusion fools every ability. Do not assume it and a normal book attack both occur.

**Important interaction:** Reap can override Illusion. Voice-only restrictions are separate from this text-mode dataset.

**Worked decision:** An ally is under investigative pressure: Illusion may help, while a simultaneous normal attack is a different action choice.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Coordinate concealment with a real ally action.
2. Distinguish Illusion, Phantasm, and Disguise.
3. Avoid relying on removed concealment effects.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Coordinate concealment with a real ally action.
2. Distinguish Illusion, Phantasm, and Disguise.
3. Avoid relying on removed concealment effects.

- Protect the ally whose discovery would be costly.
- Use Phantasm when broad protection has real value.
- Keep the death disguise and will mutually coherent.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
