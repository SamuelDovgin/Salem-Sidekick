# Voodoo Master: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CU — Coven Utility.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Use dolls to Silence, Deafen, or Blind. The same player cannot be targeted on consecutive nights regardless of chosen doll. Book adds an attack to the selected effect. CU — Coven Utility.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use dolls to Silence, Deafen, or Blind. The same player cannot be targeted on consecutive nights regardless of chosen doll. Book adds an attack to the selected effect.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. ## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Choose Silence, Deafen, or Blind for one useful nonally. Each interferes with a different part of communication or information. Prefer a player whose contribution that mode would disrupt: a public reporter for Silence, for example.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose Silence, Deafen, or Blind for one useful nonally. Each interferes with a different part of communication or information.

**How to choose:** Prefer a player whose contribution that mode would disrupt: a public reporter for Silence, for example. With no role evidence, choose an eligible nonally and keep the mode’s limits in mind; Blind is not a roleblock.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose the intended disruption. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the selected mode, target, and actual feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the selected mode, target, and actual feedback. A target’s missing message does not prove faction or guarantee that every part of their action stopped.

**What this does and does not establish:** Missing feedback is different from an ability failing. A victim’s silence is not evidence they voluntarily refused to claim.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Choose a different target because changing modes does not bypass the consecutive-player restriction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose a different target because changing modes does not bypass the consecutive-player restriction. Use D2 behavior to select a mode with a concrete purpose, and recheck the book action.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Disrupt information useful to the next vote.
- **Middle:** Rotate legal targets and coordinate effect timing.
- **Late:** Consider which surviving speaker, reader, or investigator can actually change the outcome.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Psychic (TI): visiting fallback; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** A direct visit can fit one chosen vision target. **What can expose it:** The target may identify silence/deafness/blindness, and you have no real vision.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback.** A single destination can fit a claimed Barrier visit. **What can expose it:** You supply no Barrier; the actual disruption effect may conflict with the story.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not select the same player consecutively via different dolls. Do not interpret silence as inactivity. Do not claim to know information your cover cannot get.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not select the same player consecutively via different dolls. Do not interpret silence as inactivity. Do not claim to know information your cover cannot get.

**Important interaction:** Day communication effects and night blindness differ. Voice-mode behavior must not leak into the text guide.

**Worked decision:** Blind an informative opponent: consider whether the benefit outweighs a Tracker correlating your visit with their missing results.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
