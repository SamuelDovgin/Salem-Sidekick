# Hex Master

Default role family: **Coven Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6232. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Hex living opponents toward a Hex Bomb. With the book, Hex plus a Basic Attack becomes astral. Bomb completion requires the Hex Master to remain active under its conditions.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Hex opponents likely to survive rather than die immediately.
2. Coordinate kills so they do not waste progress.
3. Compare the value of spreading hexes with taking an astral book attack.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Hex likely survivors and keep progress private.
- **Middle:** Coordinate eliminations with the shrinking unhexed set.
- **Late:** Choose between bomb progress and an astral book attack using the actual remaining opponents.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Without book, a normal visiting cover such as Psychic or Cleric may fit movement. With book, reassess visible-visit claims because the action becomes astral.

## Read the information

Your hidden progress is private strategic information, not evidence a claimed Town role could possess.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim visible visits after an astral book action without context. Do not imply Sheriff framing from the old Hex version. Do not expose your progression.

## Interactions and limits

An astral attack bypasses some interception but not every protection. A bomb has a different power and trigger from a normal attack.

## Worked decision

#4 is likely to survive while #7 is being eliminated: hexing #4 can preserve progress instead of duplicating a likely death.
