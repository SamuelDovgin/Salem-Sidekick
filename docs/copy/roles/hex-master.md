# Hex Master: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CPow — Coven Power.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Hex living opponents toward a Hex Bomb. With the book, Hex plus a Basic Attack becomes astral. Bomb completion requires the Hex Master to remain active under its conditions. CPow — Coven Power.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Hex living opponents toward a Hex Bomb. With the book, Hex plus a Basic Attack becomes astral. Bomb completion requires the Hex Master to remain active under its conditions.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CPow — Coven Power. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Place a Hex on an eligible nonally, unless the book changes your action. Your long-term goal is live opposing players carrying Hexes. Prefer a nonally likely to remain alive rather than an obvious imminent execution or the team’s planned kill, if another useful choice exists.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Place a Hex on an eligible nonally, unless the book changes your action. Your long-term goal is live opposing players carrying Hexes.

**How to choose:** Prefer a nonally likely to remain alive rather than an obvious imminent execution or the team’s planned kill, if another useful choice exists. With no D1 lead, pick an eligible nonally and coordinate to avoid wasting progress.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Mark a likely survivor. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the attempted Hex, actual feedback, and living marked players privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the attempted Hex, actual feedback, and living marked players privately. An attempt or a later death is not automatically retained Hex progress.

**What this does and does not establish:** Your hidden progress is private strategic information, not evidence a claimed Town role could possess.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the selected cover’s alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the selected cover’s alignment. A Sheriff cover (TI) can explain a one-target check story, but you do not receive genuine Sheriff feedback and Hex is not a Town investigation.

Your actual alignment is CPow — Coven Power. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Without book, a normal visiting cover such as Psychic or Cleric may fit movement. With book, reassess visible-visit claims because the action becomes astral.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Hex a new useful unmarked opponent if the state allows.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Hex a new useful unmarked opponent if the state allows. If you acquire the book, account for the astral Hex/attack behavior and the movement risk to a visiting cover. Update the living-unhexed list after deaths.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Hex likely survivors and keep progress private.
- **Middle:** Coordinate eliminations with the shrinking unhexed set.
- **Late:** Choose between bomb progress and an astral book attack using the actual remaining opponents.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Psychic (TI): visiting fallback before book; Oracle (TP): conditional fit with book. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback before book.** An ordinary direct Hex can match one chosen visited player. **What can expose it:** You have no genuine vision; the night parity and full group constraint still need a plausible story.

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional fit with book.** The astral book Hex/attack avoids a normal direct visit. **What can expose it:** There is no real Aegis, and pre-book direct visits may conflict with an all-Oracle history.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim visible visits after an astral book action without context. Do not imply Sheriff framing from the old Hex version. Do not expose your progression.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim visible visits after an astral book action without context. Do not imply Sheriff framing from the old Hex version. Do not expose your progression.

**Important interaction:** An astral attack bypasses some interception but not every protection. A bomb has a different power and trigger from a normal attack.

**Worked decision:** #4 is likely to survive while #7 is being eliminated: hexing #4 can preserve progress instead of duplicating a likely death.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Mark a likely survivor.
2. Track live progress rather than attempts.
3. Reassess cover and targeting with the book.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Mark a likely survivor.
2. Track live progress rather than attempts.
3. Reassess cover and targeting with the book.

- Hex opponents likely to survive rather than die immediately.
- Coordinate kills so they do not waste progress.
- Compare the value of spreading hexes with taking an astral book attack.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
