# Illusionist

Default role family: **Coven Deception**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6359. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Cast astral Illusion, use one team-wide Phantasm, or Disguise your shown death role/faction. Ordinary book attacks are direct and replace the protection choice.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Protect the ally whose discovery would be costly.
2. Use Phantasm when broad protection has real value.
3. Keep the death disguise and will mutually coherent.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Protect a strategically useful ally from detection.
- **Middle:** Assess Phantasm against likely investigative pressure.
- **Late:** Reconcile the cover with direct book attacks and the death disguise; keep the will coherent.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Oracle is movement-compatible while casting Illusion, but you cannot actually demonstrate Aegis. Recheck that cover when book attacks produce direct visits.

## Read the information

A favorable investigative appearance is not permanent allegiance proof. A death disguise can corrupt role-slot deductions.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim a normal visit you did not make. Do not assume Illusion fools every ability. Do not assume it and a normal book attack both occur.

## Interactions and limits

Reap can override Illusion. Voice-only restrictions are separate from this text-mode dataset.

## Worked decision

An ally is under investigative pressure: Illusion may help, while a simultaneous normal attack is a different action choice.
