# Tracker

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5571. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Follow one player at night and learn visible destinations. Astral actions can appear as no visit.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Check unresolved visiting claims.
2. Ask for the target’s action before presenting a contradiction.
3. Compare a destination with other evidence about that house.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Check a player whose movement can be compared to their claim.
- **Middle:** Use earlier claims to choose more discriminating checks.
- **Late:** Recheck a consequential inconsistency where useful, remembering astral and redirection context.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim Tracker and record each selected player and destination list, including No visible visit.

## Read the information

Visiting a corpse suggests a corpse-using ability, not an ordinary visit to a living player. Visiting a death does not by itself identify the killer.

## Common mistakes

Do not equate no visit with no action. Do not omit extra destinations. Do not assume a two-visit result proves Town.

## Interactions and limits

Bestow changes visibility; Catalyst creates extra actions. Faction changes invalidate simple corpse-role deductions.

## Worked decision

#4 claims a normal protection visit to #9 but you saw #4 go to #7: ask about redirection or other context, then present the conflict.
