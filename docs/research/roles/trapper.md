# Trapper

Default role family: **Town Protective**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6488. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Build before placing a trap. One trap can remain active; a trigger can reveal visitor roles or intercept a harmful visitor. Dismantling rebuilds for a later placement.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Build successfully before planning a placement.
2. Trap a credible valuable player likely to draw informative traffic.
3. Track built, placed, triggered, and rebuilding states explicitly.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Build and keep an accurate state log.
- **Middle:** Place on a player whose protection or visitor roles would matter.
- **Late:** Preserve a useful existing trap or justify dismantling; do not reset its history to fit a narrative.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TP first where useful; full claim with a coherent trap timeline when needed. A trigger result reports roles rather than a Lookout-style numbered list.

## Read the information

Your later trap feedback can concern a placement from an earlier night. Do not assign a visitor role to a player without independent evidence.

## Common mistakes

Do not place N1 as though already built. Do not keep two active traps. Do not claim a trigger while still rebuilding.

## Interactions and limits

Blocks can prevent building. Astral actions do not trigger the trap; some harmful visits and overlapping interception require special handling.

## Worked decision

N1 built; N2 placed on #4; N3 triggered: preserve that sequence instead of claiming a new trap every night.
