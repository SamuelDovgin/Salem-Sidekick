# Shroud

Default role family: **Neutral Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5928. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Attack directly or Shroud another player so their direct visits carry attacks. If they do not directly visit, the fallback attacks that target; visibility has documented inconsistencies.

## Win condition

Eliminate opposing factions until only your allied Shroud faction remains.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose a useful visiting target for Shroud.
2. Anticipate who actually receives the resulting attack.
3. Keep your cover conditional on the action mode and feedback.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use a plausible visiting target to carry the attack.
- **Middle:** Separate inferred destinations from exhaustive tracking claims.
- **Late:** Account for direct fallback and choose a kill route that also leaves a viable day explanation.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Oracle may fit successful astral Shrouding; Tracker is only a narrow inferred-information story, not a full observer equivalent.

## Read the information

A death can reveal something about a Shrouded player’s destination, but not all movement when nobody dies or multiple visits occur.

## Common mistakes

Do not promise invisibility in every fallback. Do not claim exhaustive visit information. Do not assume the attacked-looking Town visitor acted voluntarily.

## Interactions and limits

Shroud can make friendly visitors harmful to guards/traps and affect Investigator/Coroner interpretation.

## Worked decision

Shroud #4 and #9 dies: that may support a destination inference, but a complete fabricated Tracker history still requires more information.
