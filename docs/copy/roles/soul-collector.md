# Soul Collector: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NA — Neutral Apocalypse.** Default faction: Apocalypse. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Astrally Reap players for extra souls when they die, plus passive nightly progress. Reap also frames targets. Seven souls transform you into Death. NA — Neutral Apocalypse.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Astrally Reap players for extra souls when they die, plus passive nightly progress. Reap also frames targets. Seven souls transform you into Death.

**Win condition:** Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

Your actual alignment is NA — Neutral Apocalypse. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. ## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Reap one eligible player whose death might add to your soul progress. Reap is astral; you are not making an ordinary visible visit. Prefer someone plausibly at risk of death rather than a likely long-term survivor.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Reap one eligible player whose death might add to your soul progress. Reap is astral; you are not making an ordinary visible visit.

**How to choose:** Prefer someone plausibly at risk of death rather than a likely long-term survivor. With no evidence, choose a legal unknown and rely on the ordinary passive soul progression rather than pretending to predict the night kill.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Track passive and extra souls separately. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the real soul total, passive gain, and any extra gain from a reaped death.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the real soul total, passive gain, and any extra gain from a reaped death. Do not count every dead player as a successfully reaped target.

**What this does and does not establish:** A target appearing suspicious does not expose their actual role. Your framing cannot be treated as a genuine Town check.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Choose the next plausible death target using D2 cases and threats.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose the next plausible death target using D2 cases and threats. Use the actual total rather than an assumed schedule. If you become Death, prioritize surviving the required day instead of continuing ordinary Reap instructions.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Reap likely deaths without breaking a no-visit story.
- **Middle:** Track passive and extra souls and prepare for transformation visibility.
- **Late:** After Death, survival through the day is the urgent objective.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

**After transformation:** Death: survive the day after the public transformation so the end-of-day Apocalypse can occur. Vote pressure, not ordinary night defense, becomes the central threat. Keep private soul counts out of a cover will.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): astral movement fit; Sheriff (TI): information story; weak movement fit. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Astral movement fit.** Reap and Aegis do not require an ordinary numbered visible visit. **What can expose it:** You supply no protection and must claim role-based Aegis choices, not Reap targets.

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Information story; weak movement fit.** Reap’s framing effect can align with some suspicious-looking evidence. **What can expose it:** A real Sheriff normally visits; your astral Reap does not reproduce that, and you receive no genuine check.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use a normal visiting cover without acknowledging the risk. Do not assume Reap itself kills. Do not miss the day urgency after transformation.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use a normal visiting cover without acknowledging the risk. Do not assume Reap itself kills. Do not miss the day urgency after transformation.

**Important interaction:** Reap can override other apparent-faction effects. Source prose differs on some faction nuances; default advice is explicitly normal Apocalypse.

**Worked decision:** A prominent player is likely to die tonight: reaping them can advance progress, while your public will must not reveal private soul knowledge.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
