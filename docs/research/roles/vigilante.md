# Vigilante

Default role family: **Town Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5532. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Load by spending a night at home, then shoot a Basic Attack on another night. Up to three loads; shooting a Town member removes access to the gun.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Load successfully and track ammunition.
2. Shoot a supported evil suspect when the expected value justifies it.
3. Reassess after every shot instead of shooting merely to prove yourself.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Load and build a supported suspect list.
- **Middle:** Alternate useful shots with necessary loading and count ammunition.
- **Late:** Consider whether shooting changes the next vote; do not fire simply because a bullet remains.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TK when appropriate. Explain load nights, shots, and failures accurately if full claiming.

## Read the information

A failed shot does not uniquely identify natural defense. An Investigator finding Murder on a shooting night need not have found an evil.

## Common mistakes

Do not use the old automatic-suicide rule. Do not shoot N1. Do not claim a fresh bullet without a valid load.

## Interactions and limits

Control can redirect your shot. Harmful visits interact with guards/traps; roleblocking can stop a load.

## Worked decision

N1 load, N2 shoot #7: record the actual outcome. If the gun is lost after a Town shot, stop generating future shooting entries.
