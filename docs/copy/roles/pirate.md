# Pirate: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NE — Neutral Evil.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Three players are Landlubbers; survive until all die. Scour identifies target status, and three Plunders are available; killing a non-Landlubber removes remaining Plunders. NE — Neutral Evil.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Three players are Landlubbers; survive until all die. Scour identifies target status, and three Plunders are available; killing a non-Landlubber removes remaining Plunders.

**Win condition:** Remain alive until all assigned Landlubber players are dead, regardless of who killed them.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Scour an eligible player for information about the Landlubber objective. You cannot Plunder on N1. Use the starting clues to choose a plausible candidate.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Scour an eligible player for information about the Landlubber objective. You cannot Plunder on N1.

**How to choose:** Use the starting clues to choose a plausible candidate. With no useful clue distinguishing players, Scour an eligible unknown rather than spending an impossible first-night Plunder.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Get a real Scour result. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the actual Scour feedback and update the candidate list.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the actual Scour feedback and update the candidate list. A candidate is not confirmed until the role’s actual information supports it.

**What this does and does not establish:** Landlubber identity belongs to the player; role changes need not change the objective. Death by other means can still advance it.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the selected cover or a deliberate neutral disclosure plan suited to the lobby; an agreement is not guaranteed protection.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the selected cover or a deliberate neutral disclosure plan suited to the lobby; an agreement is not guaranteed protection. Do not use obsolete rock-paper-scissors duel language.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

A visiting cover can match Scour, but it produces no normal TI result. Open Pirate disclosure is conditional and can invite attacks from your targets.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If Scour and other evidence support a Landlubber and Plunder is enabled, consider spending a charge.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If Scour and other evidence support a Landlubber and Plunder is enabled, consider spending a charge. Otherwise gather more information. Killing a non-Landlubber disables further Plunders, so a random kill can end the plan.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Use clues and Scour rather than blind attacks.
- **Middle:** Preserve Plunder by checking likely Landlubbers.
- **Late:** If safe to disclose, explain aligned interests conditionally; surviving all target deaths is what matters.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Psychic (TI): visiting fallback; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** A Scour destination can fit the selected player of a vision. **What can expose it:** Scour is not a vision; the full parity/count constraints still apply.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback.** Scour or Plunder can share a claimed one-player destination. **What can expose it:** Neither supplies Barrier and a Plunder death may contradict the story.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use old duel/minigame templates. Do not plunder N1. Do not assume your basic attack penetrates every defense.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use old duel/minigame templates. Do not plunder N1. Do not assume your basic attack penetrates every defense.

**Important interaction:** At least one starting target is ordinarily Town and one opposes Town, with setup exceptions. Cursed Soul is excluded from the initial target pool.

**Worked decision:** If the shown target role has multiple claimants, Scour a plausible one rather than assuming the first claimant is the Landlubber.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Get a real Scour result.
2. Identify objective targets before spending Plunder.
3. Track charges and whether Plunder remains enabled.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Get a real Scour result.
2. Identify objective targets before spending Plunder.
3. Track charges and whether Plunder remains enabled.

- Use role clues and Scour to locate actual targets.
- Plunder when justified rather than firing blindly.
- Reassess when a target changes role or dies by another cause.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
