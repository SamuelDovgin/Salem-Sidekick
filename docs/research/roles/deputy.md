# Deputy

Default role family: **Town Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6299. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Use your single daytime High Noon shot on a supported suspect. You have no night ability.

**Key rule:** Available from D2. A miss can result from apparent Town status or defense and still spends your only shot; it is private feedback.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Wait for a supported target rather than using the shot for attention.
2. Consider defense and faction-altering effects.
3. Record hit or miss honestly and continue helping after the charge is spent.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Preserve the lone shot until a supported target exists.
- **Middle:** Use it where it meaningfully changes the evidence or vote.
- **Late:** A remaining shot can be decisive, but defense means a miss does not establish Town.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TK where useful. A successful shot has a public event but does not automatically announce the shooter’s identity.

## Read the information

A miss does not prove the target is Town. Another player can falsely claim credit for a public shot.

## Common mistakes

Do not use outdated shoot-Town-and-die rules. Do not expect multiple bullets. Do not treat a private miss as hard confirmation.

## Interactions and limits

Enchant/Reap can change eligibility. Early Neutral Evil defense and other protection can explain a miss.

## Worked decision

D3 shoot 9 and miss: record the miss and stop claiming further shots. A held shot is not a miss.
