# Jester

Default role family: **Neutral Evil**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6368. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Meet the day’s Chatterbox message quota, then get yourself hanged by the trial system.

**Key rule:** Failing the quota prevents your win even if hanged and can kill you of boredom. A qualifying hanging unlocks a Haunt against an eligible guilty/abstaining voter.

## Win condition

Win by being hanged after meeting that day’s Chatterbox quota. Dying to an attack or being hanged with an unmet quota is not enough.

This assumes the default Jester faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Complete the shown participation requirement.
2. Appear suspicious enough for a hanging rather than an obvious night-kill target.
3. Keep the goal distinct from ordinary survival.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Complete the quota and avoid an obvious wrong-kind death.
- **Middle:** Use believable suspicion to invite a hanging.
- **Late:** Reassess whether survival helps; a perfect cover may now deny the objective.

## Claim timing and disclosure

An imperfect Oracle/Deputy or deliberately weak investigative cover is goal-dependent. A convincing “safe” claim may work against your objective.

## Read the information

Being suspected is not sufficient; death type and participation both matter. Neutral Evil early defense expires.

## Common mistakes

Do not stay silent below the quota. Do not rely on whispers or Last Words for progress. Do not confuse being shot with being hanged.

## Interactions and limits

Prosecution and Tribunal have different revenge eligibility. Voice-mode Shufflebox is outside this baseline.

## Worked decision

Complete the quota before judgment and last words, which do not count. A night death is not the hanging you need.
