Separate public claims from role knowledge you actually possess.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Separate public claims from role knowledge you actually possess. A confident claim may be false, so a three-person guess set needs more than three names.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

When confirmation is enabled, enter and confirm all three eligible target/guess pairs only when the evidence justifies the attempt.

**Expanded copy — same section**

When confirmation is enabled, enter and confirm all three eligible target/guess pairs only when the evidence justifies the attempt. If a guess is uncertain, improve the information instead of presenting the set as known.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Collect claims; no N1 confirmed guess.
- **Middle:** Build supported three-player sets and confirm them.
- **Late:** Do not wait until too few targets remain; failed feedback does not identify the wrong member.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.
