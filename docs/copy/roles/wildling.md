# Wildling: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CU — Coven Utility.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Sense gives the selected player’s visible destinations and visitors. In text chat you also hear whispers. Book attacks retain Sense but attack the same target. CU — Coven Utility.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Sense gives the selected player’s visible destinations and visitors. In text chat you also hear whispers. Book attacks retain Sense but attack the same target.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Sense one eligible nonally to learn the available movement information. Keep destinations, visitors, and whisper information separate. Prefer a player whose movement could support a useful cover or reveal an important enemy action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Sense one eligible nonally to learn the available movement information. Keep destinations, visitors, and whisper information separate.

**How to choose:** Prefer a player whose movement could support a useful cover or reveal an important enemy action. With no D1 lead, choose an eligible unknown nonally outside obvious visitor traps.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Capture separate information channels. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record each channel exactly: where the target went, who visited them, and separately any overheard whispers.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record each channel exactly: where the target went, who visited them, and separately any overheard whispers. A missing channel is not a fabricated “no visit.”

**What this does and does not establish:** Knowing both directions is broader than either cover. Whispers can inform choices but are not evidence a Tracker would mechanically receive.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

For Tracker (TI), publish only the destination subset; for Lookout (TI), only the visitor subset.

**Expanded copy — same section**

For Tracker (TI), publish only the destination subset; for Lookout (TI), only the visitor subset. Do not leak whispers or both channels while claiming a role that receives only one.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Tracker and Lookout are natural information fits. Choose one and publish only the kind of result that role could receive.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Use D2 claims to Sense someone whose movement can answer a question.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use D2 claims to Sense someone whose movement can answer a question. If the book arrives, the selected target is also attacked; reassess a cover that depends on them surviving and confirming your account.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Choose one coherent cover and record the matching subset of Sense.
- **Middle:** Use traffic and whisper knowledge for team decisions without leaking extra information.
- **Late:** Anticipate book acquisition and preserve an explanation for both the result and any attack.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Tracker (TI): real information subset; Lookout (TI): real information subset. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Tracker — TI (Town Investigative); Real information subset.** Report the sensed player’s visible destinations, which are the channel a Tracker uses. **What can expose it:** Do not include visitors or whispers; the book also attacks your selected player.

**Lookout — TI (Town Investigative); Real information subset.** Report the visible visitors to the selected house. **What can expose it:** Do not include destinations or whispers, invent missing visitors, or normally claim to watch yourself.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not publish visitors in a Tracker will. Do not claim Lookout watching yourself normally. Do not forget that book targets now also face an attack.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not publish visitors in a Tracker will. Do not claim Lookout watching yourself normally. Do not forget that book targets now also face an attack.

**Important interaction:** Astral actions evade Sense. The stale upcoming-ability banner concerns voice-only Attune and does not replace text Sense.

**Worked decision:** Sense #4, see destination #9 and visitor #7: Tracker cover publishes #4→#9; keep #7 as private actual-role information.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Capture separate information channels.
2. Choose one public cover scope.
3. Reassess the target and story with the book.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Capture separate information channels.
2. Choose one public cover scope.
3. Reassess the target and story with the book.

- Observe players whose traffic has strategic value.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
