Attack nightly after N1. The first kill upgrades Basic to Powerful, the second adds Rampage, and the third enables transformation into War. NA — Neutral Apocalypse.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Attack nightly after N1. The first kill upgrades Basic to Powerful, the second adds Rampage, and the third enables transformation into War.

**Win condition:** Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You normally cannot attack on N1. Prepare a cover and a future kill plan; do not write a fictional first-night attack. No attack target is required in the standard opening.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You normally cannot attack on N1. Prepare a cover and a future kill plan; do not write a fictional first-night attack.

**How to choose:** No attack target is required in the standard opening. Read claims for a useful future opponent and avoid exposing your faction. Special book/modifier contexts are exceptions; follow the actual enabled controls.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Keep the first-night history legal. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record no ordinary attack and read the new claims and deaths.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record no ordinary attack and read the new claims and deaths. Other players’ kills are not your kill progression.

**What this does and does not establish:** An attack is not automatically a kill or progression. Avoid updating state merely because you selected a victim.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

**Expanded copy — same section**

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

When the attack becomes available, choose a useful eligible nonally and consider defense and visitor traffic.

**Expanded copy — same section**

When the attack becomes available, choose a useful eligible nonally and consider defense and visitor traffic. Actual kills drive upgrades: do not advance to Powerful, Rampage, or War based merely on attempts.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Survive N1 and seek a killable first target afterward.
- **Middle:** Track escalating attack strength and collateral.
- **Late:** As War, choose the legal target set and count surviving votes; Invincible Defense does not stop hanging.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

**After transformation:** War: select up to two Rampage targets, with Unstoppable attacks and Invincible Defense. It bypasses ordinary obstacles and can attack a Jailor when jailed. Public transformation makes maintaining a cover a voting problem rather than ordinary invisibility.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Vigilante (TK): conditional timeline fit; difficult later; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback on attack nights. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Vigilante — TK (Town Killing); Conditional timeline fit; difficult later.** Both can have a quiet N1 before later attacks. **What can expose it:** A real Vigilante must Load and track ammo; kill labels, rampage, and upgrades do not match indefinitely.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback on attack nights.** A direct attack can share a claimed Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** Ordinary N1 inactivity, real deaths, and no protection undermine the story.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.
