# Witch: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CPow — Coven Power.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Control a first target toward a second target, learn role information and steal feedback. You normally visit only the first target, not both selected numbers. CPow — Coven Power.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Control a first target toward a second target, learn role information and steal feedback. You normally visit only the first target, not both selected numbers.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CPow — Coven Power. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Choose an eligible player to Control and a destination to send them to. These two selections are not two personal visible visits. Coordinate with Coven so the controlled action does not ruin an allied plan.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose an eligible player to Control and a destination to send them to. These two selections are not two personal visible visits.

**How to choose:** Coordinate with Coven so the controlled action does not ruin an allied plan. Prefer a nonally with a useful suspected ability; with no reliable claim, choose an eligible unknown and avoid forcing a suspected dangerous action into an ally.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose both numbers deliberately. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Keep the actual role and feedback learned private.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Keep the actual role and feedback learned private. Distinguish your own visit to the controlled player from the controlled player’s redirected action.

**What this does and does not establish:** A learned role can still be affected by special framing. Knowing a role does not supply the complete history another TI would observe.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use your cover alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use your cover alignment. A one-target information cover may use a carefully limited story, but claiming Seer just because you selected two numbers can conflict with movement and feedback.

Your actual alignment is CPow — Coven Power. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Your information can support a story but control is conspicuous. Treat Sheriff/Psychic covers as conditional, not natural undetectable equivalents.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Use the learned role to choose a useful new Control while respecting consecutive-target restrictions.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the learned role to choose a useful new Control while respecting consecutive-target restrictions. If a target was immune or the action failed, revise the plan from that feedback rather than claiming successful control.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Acquire useful information or disrupt an important action.
- **Middle:** Use known roles without breaking your own cover’s movement story.
- **Late:** Compare controlling a vote-changing ability with direct book killing; anticipate immunity and charges.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Sheriff (TI): conditional information story; Psychic (TI): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Conditional information story.** Learned role information can guide a limited check story about the controlled player. **What can expose it:** It is not real Sheriff feedback; the victim’s Control report and framing context can break the story.

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** Your personal visit is to the controlled player, which can fit a single chosen Psychic target. **What can expose it:** The second directed destination is not a second personal visit, and you must maintain valid vision counts/parity.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim Seer merely because you selected two numbers. Do not assume consecutive control works normally. Do not omit the required second target when attacking with book.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim Seer merely because you selected two numbers. Do not assume consecutive control works normally. Do not omit the required second target when attacking with book.

**Important interaction:** Immunity, Hangover, and limited-use abilities constrain Control. Revealing information can impose Illumination, relevant to Ritualist.

**Worked decision:** You control #4 into #9: a Tracker following you normally sees #4, so a will claiming two visible Seer visits is fragile.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
