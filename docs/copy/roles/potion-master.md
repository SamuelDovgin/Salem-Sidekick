# Potion Master: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CU — Coven Utility.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Choose Barrier, Reveal, or a Harmful Potion with book. Barrier can genuinely protect; Reveal gives role information and applies Illumination. CU — Coven Utility.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose Barrier, Reveal, or a Harmful Potion with book. Barrier can genuinely protect; Reveal gives role information and applies Illumination.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Choose one legal potion mode for the team’s immediate need: Barrier an ally, Reveal an enemy, or the available book-linked harmful action. With no specific threat, coordinated Reveal of an unresolved nonally can supply useful role knowledge.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose one legal potion mode for the team’s immediate need: Barrier an ally, Reveal an enemy, or the available book-linked harmful action.

**How to choose:** With no specific threat, coordinated Reveal of an unresolved nonally can supply useful role knowledge. With a threatened ally, Barrier may be better. Do not promise all modes in one night or treat a reveal as a two-person Seer comparison.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose one useful potion mode. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record mode, target, and literal feedback privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record mode, target, and literal feedback privately. A revealed role is special information, not a Sheriff result, and Illumination can restrict a later Ritualist plan.

**What this does and does not establish:** Revealing a role does not reveal all its actions or crimes. A Seer story still lacks the usual two-visit pattern.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the chosen cover alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the chosen cover alignment. Cleric (TP) best matches a genuine Barrier night; a TI cover requires carefully limited invented result wording and can conflict with movement or the selected mode.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Cleric naturally fits nights actually using Barrier. Sheriff or Investigator can use role knowledge only as an information-fit cover; exact-role knowledge is not their actual result.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Use the new role knowledge to coordinate a legal team plan, including Ritualist eligibility.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the new role knowledge to coordinate a legal team plan, including Ritualist eligibility. Choose the next mode for the current need, and adjust a protective cover if switching to an action with different consequences.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Reveal useful unknowns or save an ally when the threat is concrete.
- **Middle:** Use information for team targeting while choosing a sustainable potion-based cover.
- **Late:** Do not lose a necessary kill by assuming Barrier, Reveal, and Harmful all happen together.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Cleric (TP): real effect fit on barrier; Sheriff (TI): conditional information story; Seer (TI): selectable but weak movement fit. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Real effect fit on Barrier.** A real Barrier potion can support an actual protective story. **What can expose it:** Changing to Reveal or the harmful mode does not also provide that Barrier.

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Conditional information story.** Real role knowledge can guide limited detection claims. **What can expose it:** A role reveal is not an actual Sheriff check and cannot justify every guilt category.

**Seer — TI (Town Investigative); Selectable but weak movement fit.** Knowing roles can let you construct a faction-comparison story. **What can expose it:** A normal Seer visits two legal players; a one-target potion does not reproduce that movement or comparison.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not assume you can heal, reveal, and attack simultaneously. Do not give a claim feedback you did not receive. Do not promise immediate Ritualist synergy.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not assume you can heal, reveal, and attack simultaneously. Do not give a claim feedback you did not receive. Do not promise immediate Ritualist synergy.

**Important interaction:** Illumination restricts Ritualist. Changing potion mode can undermine a protection cover if a claimed target dies.

**Worked decision:** If you genuinely barrier #4, a Cleric-style action matches; if you reveal #7, do not automatically log a proven Murder result.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Choose one useful potion mode.
2. Keep special role knowledge private.
3. Coordinate the next action without breaking the cover.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Choose one useful potion mode.
2. Keep special role knowledge private.
3. Coordinate the next action without breaking the cover.

- Compare information value with saving an important ally.
- Share role information with the team.
- Coordinate around Illumination and book responsibilities.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
