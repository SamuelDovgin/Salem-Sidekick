## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Check who holds the Necronomicon. If it is you, plan the team’s enabled book attack; Retrain is an alternative decision, not a free extra attack. Coordinate with the visible Coven roster before acting.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check who holds the Necronomicon. If it is you, plan the team’s enabled book attack; Retrain is an alternative decision, not a free extra attack.

**How to choose:** Coordinate with the visible Coven roster before acting. For a kill, choose a nonally with useful enemy value while avoiding obvious protected/visitor-trap targets. With no lead, choose a legal nonally rather than a teammate; do not randomly Retrain a useful ally.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Coordinate the first team action. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the actual action, destination, and feedback in private.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the actual action, destination, and feedback in private. A failed kill can involve defense or interruption; it is not an exact role identification.

**What this does and does not establish:** Retraining changes role, not automatically faction. It can invalidate a role reveal or cover plan made earlier.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If the kill was stopped, compare feedback and team information before retrying.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If the kill was stopped, compare feedback and team information before retrying. Retrain only to solve a team need, with the attack tradeoff understood. Recheck book ownership and the effect on your cover each night.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Assess which roles the faction needs.
- **Middle:** Retrain only where the new ability has a practical purpose.
- **Late:** Prefer the action that can change the next vote/kill rather than a late transformation with no time to help.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional movement fit; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional movement fit.** A genuine nonvisit period can fit Oracle’s astral action. **What can expose it:** Oracle selects roles and supplies protection; an ordinary book attack changes your movement and supplies no Aegis.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback.** A direct book attack can share the one-target visiting footprint. **What can expose it:** You do not provide Barrier, and the victim or observers may contradict the protective story.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use the old two-retrain or Basic Defense model. Do not assume every role is available. Do not claim you retrained and independently attacked normally.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use the old two-retrain or Basic Defense model. Do not assume every role is available. Do not claim you retrained and independently attacked normally.

**Important interaction:** Current CPOW roles are mutually exclusive at normal spawning. Self-retraining and known-ally restrictions need separate treatment.

**Worked decision:** A needed utility role is available: agree the change with your ally and retain a dated old-role/new-role history.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
