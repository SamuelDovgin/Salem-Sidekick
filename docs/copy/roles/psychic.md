# Psychic: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Visit a chosen player included in your vision. Odd nights give two names with at least one Good; even nights give three with at least one Evil. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Visit a chosen player included in your vision. Odd nights give two names with at least one Good; even nights give three with at least one Evil.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

On standard N1, choose one eligible player for a Good vision containing two names. The chosen player is included; record both names. A genuinely suspicious selected player can make the other name more useful, but suspicion is not proof.

**Expanded copy — same section**

On standard N1, choose one eligible player for a Good vision containing two names. The chosen player is included; record both names.

**How to choose:** A genuinely suspicious selected player can make the other name more useful, but suspicion is not proof. With no D1 evidence, choose an eligible unknown and treat the resulting pair as a starting lead only.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Publish a complete labeled vision. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Write “Good: [two names]; selected [name]” or the actual failure.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Write “Good: [two names]; selected [name]” or the actual failure. At least one is classified Good under the current effects; that does not establish that the other is Evil.

**What this does and does not establish:** At least one is a group condition, not exactly one. Do not identify a single culprit without additional evidence.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Full claim Psychic (TI), publish the complete labeled vision, and request claims where useful.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim Psychic (TI), publish the complete labeled vision, and request claims where useful. Do not post just the name you prefer or say “exactly one Town.”

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Full claim Psychic and post the vision from D2. Mark the player you deliberately selected separately from the other names.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

On the standard even-night Evil vision, select an independently supported Town reference if one exists, and record all three names.

**Expanded copy — same section**

On the standard even-night Evil vision, select an independently supported Town reference if one exists, and record all three names. If none is established, select an eligible unknown and keep all members unresolved. Check the night’s actual vision mode.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Report the first Good pair without overclearing either name.
- **Middle:** Use established references where legal and resolve earlier visions.
- **Late:** Treat no-vision feedback literally and use previous constraints rather than inventing a new eligible vision.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

Keep a truthful Psychic history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim Psychic and post the vision from D2. Mark the player you deliberately selected separately from the other names.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. ## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not reverse night parity. Do not omit the selected target. Do not silently turn a group lead into a confirmed accusation.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not reverse night parity. Do not omit the selected target. Do not silently turn a group lead into a confirmed accusation.

**Important interaction:** Enchant, Illusion, and Reap can affect classifications. Late-game no-vision messages are inconsistent within the source and are not modeled as hard rules.

**Worked decision:** N2 Evil [4,7,9], with #4 independently Town: investigate #7 and #9; do not choose one arbitrarily.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Publish a complete labeled vision.
2. Gather independent evidence about its members.
3. Use the next vision without inventing a single culprit.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Publish a complete labeled vision.
2. Gather independent evidence about its members.
3. Use the next vision without inventing a single culprit.

- On Good nights consider a genuinely suspicious target.
- On Evil nights consider a reliably established Town reference.
- Publish every vision with its Good or Evil label.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
