# Serial Killer

Default role family: **Neutral Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 4741. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Attack at night. Two consecutive kills prepare a Powerful Rampage for your next attack.

**Key rule:** A night without a kill resets Bloodlust. Cautious prevents your automatic retaliation against roleblockers; it does not cancel your chosen attack.

## Win condition

Win with the Serial Killer faction by eliminating its opponents. Bloodlust is a combat upgrade, not a separate victory condition.

This assumes the default Serial Killer faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose targets with a plan for surviving later votes.
2. Track actual Bloodlust progress.
3. Consider whether retaliation exposes more than it gains.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Choose killable targets and an intelligible cover.
- **Middle:** Track Bloodlust and set Cautious deliberately.
- **Late:** Keep enough credibility for the last vote, not merely tonight’s kill.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Cleric is a visiting fallback; Vigilante is a conditional kill story with timing, ammunition, and public-death-label problems.

## Read the information

Killing a target does not let you claim arbitrary Town attack feedback. Target defense can reset progression.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume ordinary roleblocking stops you. Do not forget to set Cautious intentionally. Do not claim the special counterattack power as ordinary basic damage.

## Interactions and limits

Sources describe retaliation through natural defense with protection exceptions; exact attack-label modeling is disputed and excluded from hard rules.

## Worked decision

Kill on two consecutive nights, then plan the stronger attack’s visitor risk. Two attempted attacks that fail do not build the same progress.
