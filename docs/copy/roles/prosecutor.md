# Prosecutor: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TPow — Town Power.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Two day Prosecutions force a hanging without normal judgment; at most one per day. Using it reveals you; prosecuting Town removes remaining charges. TPow — Town Power.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Two day Prosecutions force a hanging without normal judgment; at most one per day. Using it reveals you; prosecuting Town removes remaining charges.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TPow — Town Power. When the lobby asks for alignment, TPow is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no N1 action and no D1 prosecution. Prepare to distinguish a strong case from a merely loud accusation. Write one question you would ask a disputed claimant.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no N1 action and no D1 prosecution. Prepare to distinguish a strong case from a merely loud accusation.

**How to choose:** Write one question you would ask a disputed claimant. With no opening evidence, wait for D2 information; there is no benefit to inventing a prosecution target.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Obtain the target’s answer. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Compare investigative reports, claims, and actual deaths.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Compare investigative reports, claims, and actual deaths. Your ability can force a day execution, so an unresolved contradiction needs examination before you spend it.

**What this does and does not establish:** This ability bypasses a normal trial; it does not make your target guilty. A Jester prosecution has special revenge consequences.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TPow if asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TPow if asked. Before prosecuting, ask the target to answer the specific evidence. Use the ability when the case justifies bypassing an ordinary vote, not solely to demonstrate your role.

Your actual alignment is TPow — Town Power. When the lobby asks for alignment, TPow is your default shorthand.

Conceal exact identity when useful, but reveal through the ability when its value justifies the risk.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

You still have no night action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You still have no night action. Revisit your case after new deaths and results. Hold if the evidence weakened or a normal vote already solves the problem; evaluate the consequences of a mistaken prosecution.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Save charges when ordinary discussion can resolve the case.
- **Middle:** Use a justified prosecution where voting cannot safely solve the problem.
- **Late:** Consider remaining charges and special hanging objectives before committing.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Prosecutor history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Conceal exact identity when useful, but reveal through the ability when its value justifies the risk.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not prosecute merely to prove your role. Do not expect to use it during Tribunal. Do not use outdated Hangman-suicide advice.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not prosecute merely to prove your role. Do not expect to use it during Tribunal. Do not use outdated Hangman-suicide advice.

**Important interaction:** Prosecution counts as hanging for relevant neutral objectives. Ordinary day gunshots have different win-condition effects.

**Worked decision:** A supported evil will escape the vote due to disruption: consider Prosecute, after checking for alternative explanations and Jester risk.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Obtain the target’s answer.
2. Establish a supported case.
3. Prosecute for a concrete Town benefit.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Obtain the target’s answer.
2. Establish a supported case.
3. Prosecute for a concrete Town benefit.

- Use evidence rather than impatience to select a target.
- Consider whether ordinary voting already solves the case.
- Preserve the remaining charge after a justified prosecution.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
