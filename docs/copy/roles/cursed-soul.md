# Cursed Soul: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: Neutral Outlier.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Soul Swap changes roles, not faction. In the default Wandering Souls faction, win by eliminating every Cursed Soul role while a faction member survives. Neutral Outlier.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Soul Swap changes roles, not faction. In the default Wandering Souls faction, win by eliminating every Cursed Soul role while a faction member survives.

**Win condition:** For default Wandering Souls, eliminate every Cursed Soul role while at least one faction member remains alive. Acquiring another role does not replace your faction objective.

Your actual alignment is Neutral Outlier. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Prepare for the mandatory astral role swap. Select a desired legal target, but expect the actual acquired role to differ under the swap rules. Choose a role opportunity using real claims if available.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Prepare for the mandatory astral role swap. Select a desired legal target, but expect the actual acquired role to differ under the swap rules.

**How to choose:** Choose a role opportunity using real claims if available. With no evidence, choose a legal unknown and keep an open plan. Town/Wandering Souls swap randomness means intention is not a guaranteed destination.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Observe the actual swap result. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the actual acquired role and unchanged faction, not just the player you selected.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the actual acquired role and unchanged faction, not just the player you selected. Open the new role’s instructions while retaining the previous role history.

**What this does and does not establish:** Knowing an old role does not establish a player’s current role. Role-swapped Town abilities need not belong to Town faction.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Follow the role you actually acquired and its current legal controls.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Follow the role you actually acquired and its current legal controls. If another swap is relevant, preserve history again; do not reset charges/progress by assumption where the interaction is uncertain.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Track the first actual swap and retain faction identity.
- **Middle:** Use acquired abilities toward eliminating Cursed Souls.
- **Late:** Count which Cursed Soul roles remain and whether a faction member will survive the final elimination.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): astral opening fit; Amnesiac (TS): difficult transition story. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Astral opening fit.** The initial Swap is astral rather than an ordinary visible visit. **What can expose it:** The acquired role changes future actions; faction stays separate and no initial Aegis exists.

**Amnesiac — TS (Town Support); Difficult transition story.** A real role change may motivate a transition account. **What can expose it:** Amnesiac needs the correct eligible Town-death chronology; a random or evil acquired role does not fit.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not play the old role-steals-faction objective. Do not assume every chosen swap is deterministic. Do not keep using your prior role’s template after a real swap.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not play the old role-steals-faction objective. Do not assume every chosen swap is deterministic. Do not keep using your prior role’s template after a real swap.

**Important interaction:** The main wiki role’s Swap section is incomplete; Fandom and official changes support faction-dependent randomization and failures.

**Worked decision:** You acquire a killing role but remain Wandering Souls: use the new ability toward removing Cursed Souls, not the acquired role’s default faction goal.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
