# Necromancer: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: CU — Coven Utility.** Default faction: Coven. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Reanimate suitable corpses and direct their actions. More corpse types are available than for normal Town Ret; each evil corpse is limited, and book attacks replace ordinary reanimation. CU — Coven Utility.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Reanimate suitable corpses and direct their actions. More corpse types are available than for normal Town Ret; each evil corpse is limited, and book attacks replace ordinary reanimation.

**Win condition:** Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

In an ordinary fresh game there is no corpse to reanimate. Use a book attack only if you hold the book and it is enabled; otherwise prepare for the graveyard. No corpse should be invented on N1.

**Expanded copy — same section**

In an ordinary fresh game there is no corpse to reanimate. Use a book attack only if you hold the book and it is enabled; otherwise prepare for the graveyard.

**How to choose:** No corpse should be invented on N1. At D2, inspect actual dead roles and their permitted abilities. If holding the book, coordinate a legal nonally attack instead of claiming simultaneous normal reanimation.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Inspect the real graveyard. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record no eligible corpse or the actual book result.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record no eligible corpse or the actual book result. Keep real corpse eligibility and uses separate from any Retributionist cover draft.

**What this does and does not establish:** A corpse visit and generated effect may support a corpse-user claim, but do not establish Town faction.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use TS if you chose Retributionist as the cover.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use TS if you chose Retributionist as the cover. An empty N1 fits both starting states; later an evil or Stoned corpse, a forbidden self-protection, or an exhausted use can expose the cover.

Your actual alignment is CU — Coven Utility. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Retributionist is a natural conditional cover only while using actions, bodies, and targets a genuine Town Ret could use.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If a usable corpse exists, choose its ability for the team’s current need and log the borrowed result privately.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If a usable corpse exists, choose its ability for the team’s current need and log the borrowed result privately. If no compatible Town corpse exists, do not fabricate one for a Ret story; the book is an alternative action.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Watch for suitable bodies and avoid promising an unavailable action.
- **Middle:** Exploit corpse utility while keeping any Ret story inside legal bounds.
- **Late:** Compare the best corpse effect with a direct book attack; ordinary nights do not allow both.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Retributionist (TS): real ability overlap when eligible; Oracle (TP): conditional nonvisit fallback. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Retributionist — TS (Town Support); Real ability overlap when eligible.** Using a legal Town corpse can create an effect a real Retributionist could create. **What can expose it:** Evil/Stoned corpses, exhausted uses, forbidden self-protection, and book attack replacement break the overlap.

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional nonvisit fallback.** A genuinely inactive opening has no ordinary direct visit. **What can expose it:** Later corpse visits or direct book attacks conflict, and you do not actually supply Aegis.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim Town Ret while using a Stoned/evil corpse or self-protection normally forbidden to Ret. Do not claim simultaneous ordinary reanimation and book attack.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim Town Ret while using a Stoned/evil corpse or self-protection normally forbidden to Ret. Do not claim simultaneous ordinary reanimation and book attack.

**Important interaction:** Necromancer takes corpse-use priority over Ret. Some day-role bodies act at the next appropriate transition.

**Worked decision:** Use a legal dead Town investigator on #9 and preserve the real returned information; an evil corpse kill would require a different explanation.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Inspect the real graveyard.
2. Select a usable ability.
3. Keep corpse use compatible with any Ret cover.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Inspect the real graveyard.
2. Select a usable ability.
3. Keep corpse use compatible with any Ret cover.

- Choose a useful corpse while tracking availability.
- Decide whether its effect is worth exposing an evil corpse use.
- Recheck the will when asked to carry the book.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
