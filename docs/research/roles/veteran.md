# Veteran

Default role family: **Town Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6536. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Three Alerts grant Basic Defense and shoot all direct visitors with Powerful Attack that night. Friendly visitors are also at risk.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose alerts based on likely hostile attention.
2. Avoid inviting friendly protection into an alert.
3. Record alert nights and remaining charges.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Avoid inviting friendly visitors into an alert.
- **Middle:** Adapt alert use to known threats and remaining charges.
- **Late:** Balance self-preservation against the ally votes needed to win; an unused alert is not automatically wasted.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TK initially where useful. Reveal Veteran when needed to explain kills or prevent allied visits.

## Read the information

No kills does not disprove an alert. A visitor surviving can reflect protection; an astral action is not shot.

## Common mistakes

Do not use TP/LO bait as a beginner default. Do not assume roleblock immunity. Do not claim infinite alerts.

## Interactions and limits

Roleblocks can prevent Alert. Astral visitors bypass the shooting effect, and sufficient protection can preserve direct visitors.

## Worked decision

A protector offers to visit tonight: warn them or avoid alerting rather than treating the friendly casualty as unavoidable.
