# Wildling

Default role family: **Coven Utility**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6347. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Sense gives the selected player’s visible destinations and visitors. In text chat you also hear whispers. Book attacks retain Sense but attack the same target.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Observe players whose traffic has strategic value.
2. Use information consistently with one selected cover.
3. Tell allies relevant discoveries without leaking your extra powers publicly.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Choose one coherent cover and record the matching subset of Sense.
- **Middle:** Use traffic and whisper knowledge for team decisions without leaking extra information.
- **Late:** Anticipate book acquisition and preserve an explanation for both the result and any attack.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Tracker and Lookout are natural information fits. Choose one and publish only the kind of result that role could receive.

## Read the information

Knowing both directions is broader than either cover. Whispers can inform choices but are not evidence a Tracker would mechanically receive.

## Common mistakes

Do not publish visitors in a Tracker will. Do not claim Lookout watching yourself normally. Do not forget that book targets now also face an attack.

## Interactions and limits

Astral actions evade Sense. The stale upcoming-ability banner concerns voice-only Attune and does not replace text Sense.
