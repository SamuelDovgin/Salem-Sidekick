# Jinx

Default role family: **Coven Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5984. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Choose a house and attack one random non-Coven visitor. With the book, you also attack its resident.

**Key rule:** Other direct visitors can learn your identity and the house, including a surviving victim. An attack notice gives no full visitor list.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Predict useful enemy traffic without exposing yourself widely.
2. Coordinate around allied visits.
3. Reassess your cover immediately if survivors can identify you.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Choose traffic carefully to limit identity exposure.
- **Middle:** Exploit useful visitors while planning for survivors who can report you.
- **Late:** Judge whether an extra visitor kill outweighs being identified before the final vote.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Crusader is an effect-shaped story with serious limits: you do not give its defense and visitor identity notifications can expose Jinx. Lookout is also risky.

## Read the information

Knowing that you attacked a visitor is not a complete visitor list. A survivor can carry evidence against you even if nobody dies.

## Common mistakes

Do not label Jinx→Lookout a natural full-information fit. Do not assume all visitors die. Do not use crowded protection calls without understanding exposure.

## Interactions and limits

Astral visitors evade the passive attack. With book, the direct attack creates additional interception risks.

## Worked decision

Jinx 4; if several players visit, an unhit witness can identify you. A quiet house gives no visitor attack.
