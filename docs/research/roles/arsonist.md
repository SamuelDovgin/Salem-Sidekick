# Arsonist

Default role family: **Neutral Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5504. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Douse players directly and passively Douse your normal visitors. Choose Ignite to burn all previously Doused players.

**Key rule:** Ignite is a separate astral Unstoppable attack. Taking no action can Clean gasoline from yourself; Douse and Clean do not burn targets.

## Win condition

Win with the Arsonist faction by eliminating its opponents. You do not need to Douse every survivor before choosing Ignite.

This assumes the default Arsonist faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Douse players with a plan for later survival and vote control.
2. Track passive douses separately from your direct target.
3. Ignite when it meaningfully improves your path rather than chasing maximum count.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Douse with a survival and vote plan.
- **Middle:** Track direct/passive progress and your own gasoline status.
- **Late:** Ignite when it creates a winning path rather than waiting for a maximal burn that never comes.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Psychic or Cleric can fit direct Douse movement as fallbacks. Oracle fits an ignition/idle night better than a direct-Douse night.

## Read the information

New passive douses concern visitors to you, not a Lookout report on another house. A survival result is not a unique role identity.

## Common mistakes

Do not pretend Lookout can watch itself normally. Do not ignite while forgetting your own douse status. Do not count same-night fresh visitors as guaranteed immediate burns.

## Interactions and limits

Astral visitors avoid passive dousing. Multiple Arsonists can affect ignition and cleaning decisions.

## Worked decision

Douse 4 and 7, then choose Ignite on a later night. Do not wait for automatic ignition or mistake a passive visitor for your own visit.
