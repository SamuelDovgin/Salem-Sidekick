# Pirate

Default role family: **Neutral Evil**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6542. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Scour to find your three secretly assigned Landlubbers. From N2, Plunder can attack one, with three Plunder uses.

**Key rule:** All three assigned players must die; other killers can do it. Killing a non-Landlubber with Plunder removes your remaining Plunders. There are no duels.

## Win condition

Stay alive until all three assigned Landlubbers are dead, then leave in victory. Three arbitrary kills do not satisfy the objective.

This assumes the default Pirate faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use role clues and Scour to locate actual targets.
2. Plunder when justified rather than firing blindly.
3. Reassess when a target changes role or dies by another cause.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use clues and Scour rather than blind attacks.
- **Middle:** Preserve Plunder by checking likely Landlubbers.
- **Late:** If safe to disclose, explain aligned interests conditionally; surviving all target deaths is what matters.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting cover can match Scour, but it produces no normal TI result. Open Pirate disclosure is conditional and can invite attacks from your targets.

## Read the information

Landlubber identity belongs to the player; role changes need not change the objective. Death by other means can still advance it.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old duel/minigame templates. Do not plunder N1. Do not assume your basic attack penetrates every defense.

## Interactions and limits

At least one starting target is ordinarily Town and one opposes Town, with setup exceptions. Cursed Soul is excluded from the initial target pool.

## Worked decision

Scour 4 and find a Landlubber. If someone else kills 4, that still advances your objective while you stay alive.
