# Enchanter

Default role family: **Coven Deception**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6673. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Enchant to falsify investigative appearances. Or spend one of two Alterates to forge a player’s shown death role, faction and will.

**Key rule:** Enchant does not reveal the target’s true role. An Alterate must coincide with the target’s death that night; a surviving target does not show the forgery.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Frame where investigative attention is plausible.
2. Coordinate Alterate with an expected death.
3. Maintain consistency with any fabricated public will.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Establish a coherent cover and plausible framing opportunities.
- **Middle:** Coordinate deceptive evidence with actual investigative attention and deaths.
- **Late:** Avoid an impossible forged will when only a few legal roles/actions remain.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting TI cover is conditional: your frame can support some reported results, but observers and unaffected roles can expose it.

## Read the information

Death-role and will information may be manipulated; a fabricated graveyard result is not reliable proof of earlier claims.

## Common mistakes

Do not frame movement that observers can directly verify. Do not assume all TIs are fooled. Do not spend Alterate on a survivor without recognizing the cost.

## Interactions and limits

Coroner, Tracker, Lookout, and Spy have resistance to this kind of information alteration. Reap can override some framing.

## Worked decision

Coordinate Alterate 4 with a planned death. Do not call a living player successfully forged just because you selected a replacement will.
