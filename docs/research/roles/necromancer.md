# Necromancer

Default role family: **Coven Utility**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5465. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Reanimate an eligible corpse and direct its borrowed ability to a destination. You receive its feedback.

**Key rule:** You visit the corpse, not its destination. Each usable evil corpse is once-only; Town corpses can be reused. Book attacks replace ordinary reanimation.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose a useful corpse while tracking availability.
2. Decide whether its effect is worth exposing an evil corpse use.
3. Recheck the will when asked to carry the book.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Watch for suitable bodies and avoid promising an unavailable action.
- **Middle:** Exploit corpse utility while keeping any Ret story inside legal bounds.
- **Late:** Compare the best corpse effect with a direct book attack; ordinary nights do not allow both.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Retributionist is a natural conditional cover only while using actions, bodies, and targets a genuine Town Ret could use.

## Read the information

A corpse visit and generated effect may support a corpse-user claim, but do not establish Town faction.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim Town Ret while using a Stoned/evil corpse or self-protection normally forbidden to Ret. Do not claim simultaneous ordinary reanimation and book attack.

## Interactions and limits

Necromancer takes corpse-use priority over Ret. Some day-role bodies act at the next appropriate transition.
