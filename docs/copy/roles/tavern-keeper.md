# Tavern Keeper: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TS — Town Support.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Drink with a player to roleblock their night ability. Successful roleblocking leaves Hangover, preventing immediate repeat blocking; you are roleblock immune. TS — Town Support.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Drink with a player to roleblock their night ability. Successful roleblocking leaves Hangover, preventing immediate repeat blocking; you are roleblock immune.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Drink with one eligible player to attempt a roleblock. This disrupts an action; it does not investigate their faction. Prefer a player whose claimed or suspected night action makes disruption useful.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Drink with one eligible player to attempt a roleblock. This disrupts an action; it does not investigate their faction.

**How to choose:** Prefer a player whose claimed or suspected night action makes disruption useful. With no D1 lead, choose an eligible unknown away from obvious dangerous visitor bait. Roleblocking certain killers, including an uncautious Serial Killer, can be dangerous.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Get one honest disruption record. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record your target and actual feedback, then compare it with what they report.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record your target and actual feedback, then compare it with what they report. No deaths elsewhere does not prove you blocked a killer. A failed block is not a successful Drink.

**What this does and does not establish:** No kill after a block is a lead, not proof of the killer. A target’s immunity or Hangover can explain a failed block.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TS when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS when asked. Give Tavern Keeper and the target when coordinating or resolving a reported block. A player confirming they were blocked supports the action story, not your faction.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Claim TS where appropriate; full claim when needed to coordinate or explain a disputed result.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Choose a different eligible player after a successful Drink because Hungover prevents a consecutive block.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose a different eligible player after a successful Drink because Hungover prevents a consecutive block. Use D2 evidence to disrupt a meaningful action; do not promise to lock the same person indefinitely.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Find a reasoned disruption target.
- **Middle:** Use target feedback, claim conflicts, and Hangover to plan new blocks.
- **Late:** Distinguish preventing a lethal action from merely seeking a reassuring no-kill night.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Tavern Keeper history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS where appropriate; full claim when needed to coordinate or explain a disputed result.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim to block day abilities. Do not confuse Hangover with a new successful block. Do not assume risky killers are safe targets.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim to block day abilities. Do not confuse Hangover with a new successful block. Do not assume risky killers are safe targets.

**Important interaction:** Uncautious SK and Full Moon Werewolf can counterattack. Hangover also grants Control Immunity in the current patch baseline.

**Worked decision:** N1 block #4 and no kill occurs: ask for #4’s action and seek corroboration instead of calling the case solved.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Get one honest disruption record.
2. Compare the target’s account.
3. Rotate targets under Hungover.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Get one honest disruption record.
2. Compare the target’s account.
3. Rotate targets under Hungover.

- Block a credible harmful-action suspect.
- Compare their claimed outcome with your actual feedback.
- Alternate targets rather than assuming a permanent block lock.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.

## Will notes — lower priority

**Compact copy**

Drink target; actual block feedback / failed / hungover / manual.

**Expanded copy — same section**

For a truthful Town draft, include only actions/results you actually performed or received.

**Fields:** Drink target; actual block feedback / failed / hungover / manual.

**Illustrative formatting only — not live data:**

```text
N1: Drink 4 — [feedback]
```

Hangover affects repeat attempts. Preserve attempts without falsely labeling all successful.

Keep this below strategy. Start a new match with blank fields and no illustrative player numbers. Do not auto-fill success, a target’s role, a save, or a forged result.
