## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Choose direct Attack or Shroud for a concrete reason. Shrouding a player and directly attacking them are different plans. A likely active visitor may carry a useful Shroud attack; direct Attack is easier to understand when you want the selected player as the victim.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Choose direct Attack or Shroud for a concrete reason. Shrouding a player and directly attacking them are different plans.

**How to choose:** A likely active visitor may carry a useful Shroud attack; direct Attack is easier to understand when you want the selected player as the victim. With no role evidence, do not assume a random target will visit the house you want.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Choose mode for a specific victim plan. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the selected mode and actual outcomes.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the selected mode and actual outcomes. If the carrier did not visit, fallback and visibility details are conditional; do not invent a full destination report from the deaths.

**What this does and does not establish:** A death can reveal something about a Shrouded player’s destination, but not all movement when nobody dies or multiple visits occur.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Oracle (TP): conditional astral fit; Tracker (TI): narrow information story; difficult. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Oracle — TP (Town Protective); Conditional astral fit.** A successful Shroud mode can fit no ordinary direct visit. **What can expose it:** Fallback/direct attack visibility is conditional, and no Aegis is supplied.

**Tracker — TI (Town Investigative); Narrow information story; difficult.** A carrier’s known destination may support a limited account. **What can expose it:** You do not receive a complete Tracker report, and personal movement can conflict.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.
