# Catalyst

Default role family: **Town Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6496. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

An Outlier that Overcharges a player to enable additional actions on the following ability cycle. It is Unique and needs a compatible setup.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Confirm the role can exist in this setup.
2. Overcharge a trusted player with a useful upcoming action.
3. Coordinate what can be done without exposing unnecessary details.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Verify the setup and find a trustworthy useful recipient.
- **Middle:** Coordinate extra actions with the actual recipient’s ability rules.
- **Late:** Concentrate value on a remaining action that can change the outcome.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Use Town Outlier rather than pretending this is a normal TI role. Explain the actual Overcharge timing if asked.

## Read the information

More actions can explain unusual visit counts. An extra action is not automatic proof of a specific role.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume it can spawn from any normal bucket. Do not promise every role behaves identically. Do not use a guessed same-night timing rule.

## Interactions and limits

Role-specific Overcharge details vary; several have official bug fixes. Complex day/night timing stays in advanced notes until client checked.

## Worked decision

An established investigative ally can use another action next cycle: coordinate the benefit and log the charge application.
