# Sheriff: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Search one player at night for Innocent or Suspicious feedback. This is a limited detection check, not an exact-role reveal. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Search one player at night for Innocent or Suspicious feedback. This is a limited detection check, not an exact-role reveal.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Search one eligible unresolved player. Your result will be Innocent or Suspicious if the check resolves normally. Prefer a player whose claim or action can be questioned tomorrow.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Search one eligible unresolved player. Your result will be Innocent or Suspicious if the check resolves normally.

**How to choose:** Prefer a player whose claim or action can be questioned tomorrow. With no useful D1 information, choose an eligible unknown outside obvious Veteran bait. Quietness alone is not evidence of evil.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Publish one honest check. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the literal result.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the literal result. Innocent leaves the bookholder and several other evil roles possible; Suspicious can be caused by framing. A failed search is neither result.

**What this does and does not establish:** Innocent leaves many evils possible, including Neutral Killings and the bookholder. Suspicious can come from framing, and identifies more than Coven.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Post “Sheriff (TI), N1: [player] — [actual result].” If Suspicious, ask for a claim and compare other evidence.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Post “Sheriff (TI), N1: [player] — [actual result].” If Suspicious, ask for a claim and compare other evidence. If Innocent, leave the player unresolved rather than calling them confirmed Town.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Full claim Sheriff and publish results promptly, normally from D2. Give the result before arguing its meaning.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Check a new unresolved player whose D2 story matters.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check a new unresolved player whose D2 story matters. A roleblocked N1 gives you no check to build on; retry only if the target still deserves priority. Do not spend the game clearing everyone based on Innocent.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Check an unresolved player; establish a prompt reporting habit.
- **Middle:** Compare results with the evolving faction threats rather than repeatedly checking plausible innocents.
- **Late:** Combine vote math, claims, and exceptions; an innocent result still cannot solve the endgame alone.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Sheriff history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim Sheriff and publish results promptly, normally from D2. Give the result before arguing its meaning.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not call an innocent result a confirmed ally. Do not label every suspicious result Coven. Do not hide results until pressured without a reason.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not call an innocent result a confirmed ally. Do not label every suspicious result Coven. Do not hide results until pressured without a reason.

**Important interaction:** Enchant and Reap can change apparent guilt; Illusion can conceal Coven. Solo Apocalypse detection differs from a multi-member faction.

**Worked decision:** N1 #4 Innocent: ask #4 for a claim when useful, but do not protect them solely because of this check.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Publish one honest check.
2. Ask a specific follow-up question.
3. Combine results with claims instead of treating detection as certainty.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Publish one honest check.
2. Ask a specific follow-up question.
3. Combine results with claims instead of treating detection as certainty.

- Check unresolved claims rather than already demonstrated roles.
- Post the result with its night and target.
- Compare suspicious feedback with claims and independent evidence.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
