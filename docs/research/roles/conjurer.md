# Conjurer

Default role family: **Coven Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6365. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

One Powerful meteor during a later day; your identity is not publicly announced by the ability. With book, you can also make ordinary night attacks.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use the meteor for a high-impact removal rather than a reflex response.
2. Consider day defense and the public vote state.
3. Keep a credible will after the day ability is spent.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Keep the meteor until a valuable opportunity.
- **Middle:** Remove a consequential target when it changes control.
- **Late:** Do not die holding it merely out of habit; assess defense and the immediate vote state.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Before book, nonvisiting covers may match movement. A meteor cannot pass as a Deputy shot with a different visible event.

## Read the information

A public meteor announces the ability occurred, not which living player used it.

## Common mistakes

Do not assume night barriers persist into daytime. Do not claim a public reveal ability you cannot demonstrate. Do not use the meteor on an invulnerable target casually.

## Interactions and limits

The meteor has different strength and feedback from a basic shot. Secret Killers does not justify assuming every cinematic disappears.

## Worked decision

A confirmed leader threatens a decisive vote: assess whether meteor removal changes the outcome before spending it.
