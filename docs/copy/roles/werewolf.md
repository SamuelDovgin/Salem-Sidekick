# Werewolf: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NK — Neutral Killing.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

On non-Full-Moon nights, mark a scent to keep receiving that player’s destinations. On Full Moon, make a Powerful Rampage or stay home and rampage there. NK — Neutral Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

On non-Full-Moon nights, mark a scent to keep receiving that player’s destinations. On Full Moon, make a Powerful Rampage or stay home and rampage there.

**Win condition:** Eliminate opposing factions until only your allied Werewolf faction remains.

Your actual alignment is NK — Neutral Killing. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. ## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Check the actual Full Moon indicator. On a non-Full-Moon opening, choose a Scent target rather than inventing an unavailable attack. Scent a likely active eligible nonally whose later movement could be useful.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check the actual Full Moon indicator. On a non-Full-Moon opening, choose a Scent target rather than inventing an unavailable attack.

**How to choose:** Scent a likely active eligible nonally whose later movement could be useful. With no D1 lead, choose an unknown outside obvious visiting traps. If the setup actually permits an attack, assess the rampage and visitor risk first.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Follow the actual moon state. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the Scent target and its continuing information separately from your own destination.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the Scent target and its continuing information separately from your own destination. An old target’s later movement does not mean you personally visited them again.

**What this does and does not establish:** Persistent information does not imply a fresh visit to that player. Attacking one house while reporting another player’s movement creates observer risk.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Use the cover alignment.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Use the cover alignment. Tracker (TI) can use the movement information, but later attacking elsewhere can contradict the claimed personal visit. The information fit does not erase that risk.

Tracker is an information fit only when the reported target actually has scent coverage. Your visible attack destination may contradict the claimed tracking target.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Follow the client’s current moon state.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Follow the client’s current moon state. If attacking, choose a useful victim and account for rampage traffic; if scenting, update the information plan. Keep persistent Scent reports separate from tonight’s attack.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Acquire useful scent coverage on eligible nights.
- **Middle:** Choose rampages without pretending persistent results are fresh personal visits.
- **Late:** Use the actual moon/action state and preserve a credible voting position.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Tracker (TI): real information; conditional movement; Cleric (TP): visiting fallback on attacks. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Tracker — TI (Town Investigative); Real information; conditional movement.** Persistent Scent supplies real movement information. **What can expose it:** On later attack nights you may visibly go elsewhere; information fit does not fix personal movement.

**Cleric — TP (Town Protective); Visiting fallback on attacks.** A chosen attack house can share a claimed Barrier destination. **What can expose it:** No real protection exists; rampage and kill details differ.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not invent new scent targets on every night. Do not assume roleblock simply disables Full Moon danger. Do not call the Tracker cover universally strong.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not invent new scent targets on every night. Do not assume roleblock simply disables Full Moon danger. Do not call the Tracker cover universally strong.

**Important interaction:** Jail, roleblock, and Control have dangerous retaliation/redirection interactions. Use the actual Full Moon indicator rather than an unverified schedule.

**Worked decision:** You marked #4 earlier but attack #9 tonight: a Tracker story about #4 can be informationally true while failing a check of your own movement.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
