# Oracle

Default role family: **Town Protective**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5690. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Choose a Town role, not a player number, and grant its holders Powerful Defense astrally. No consecutive repeat role; Oracle itself is a once-only choice.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use credible exact-role information to choose Aegis.
2. Plan around the no-repeat restriction.
3. Prefer protection with genuine expected value rather than guessing a famous role.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use credible known role information rather than unsupported precise player guesses.
- **Middle:** Plan alternate Aegis choices around the no-repeat restriction.
- **Late:** Read which remaining roles are actually threatened; do not promise consecutive coverage you cannot provide.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TP when pressured, then explain role-based choices if full claiming. Coordinate privately only with appropriately trusted players.

## Read the information

A save does not necessarily tell you the saved player’s identity. The selected role and a supposed target number must not be conflated.

## Common mistakes

Do not write normal visiting protection wills. Do not repeat a role on consecutive nights. Do not claim precise player feedback the game did not supply.

## Interactions and limits

Aegis is astral and can bypass jail. An ordinary visible visit conflicts with an Oracle story unless a role/state change explains it.

## Worked decision

You know a threatened Seer exists and did not Aegis Seer last night: selecting that role can cover them without a direct visit.
