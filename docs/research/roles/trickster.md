# Trickster

Default role family: **Town Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5398. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Place a Magic Mirror to absorb a direct attack, then Unleash the stored attack on a later night. One Self Mirror is available.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Mirror an ally likely to be attacked.
2. Read the absorbed attacker role and attack properties.
3. Choose an evidence-supported Unleash target and account for collateral risk.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Mirror an ally likely to face an attack.
- **Middle:** Choose a justified Unleash and understand the stored attack’s collateral.
- **Late:** Use the active mirror/attack state to plan; do not treat the role as an always-available gun.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TK when appropriate. If full claiming, distinguish mirror attempts, actual absorption, and Unleash.

## Read the information

A mirror attempt with no absorption is not an attack result. Unleash can produce the absorbed role’s death label, complicating kill-count deductions.

## Common mistakes

Do not use obsolete taunt mechanics. Do not mirror again while holding an unused attack. Do not unleash a rampage onto crowded allied traffic.

## Interactions and limits

Unstoppable attacks shatter mirrors. Some passive attacks are not absorbed. Bodyguard/Trap can respond to an Unleash visit.

## Worked decision

N1 mirror #4 absorbs an attack: record the reported role; N2 select a justified target for the stored attack.
