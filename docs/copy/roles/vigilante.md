# Vigilante: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TK — Town Killing.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Load by spending a night at home, then shoot a Basic Attack on another night. Up to three loads; shooting a Town member removes access to the gun. TK — Town Killing.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Load by spending a night at home, then shoot a Basic Attack on another night. Up to three loads; shooting a Town member removes access to the gun.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Load on N1. You cannot shoot on the first night, so your job is to prepare a supported target rather than firing. No shooting target is required for the loading action.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Load on N1. You cannot shoot on the first night, so your job is to prepare a supported target rather than firing.

**How to choose:** No shooting target is required for the loading action. Note one question for D2 and leave the suspect list blank if D1 supplies nothing. Loading and choosing a later target are separate decisions.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Complete the first Load. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record whether loading completed and your actual ammunition state.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record whether loading completed and your actual ammunition state. Do not write a shot or a killed target for a night spent loading.

**What this does and does not establish:** A failed shot does not uniquely identify natural defense. An Investigator finding Murder on a shooting night need not have found an evil.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TK when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TK when asked. Use D2 claims and evidence to decide whether a future shot is justified. A demand to demonstrate your role is not enough reason to shoot an unresolved player.

Your actual alignment is TK — Town Killing. When the lobby asks for alignment, TK is your default shorthand.

Claim TK when appropriate. Explain load nights, shots, and failures accurately if full claiming.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If loaded and a supported target exists, consider a legal shot.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If loaded and a supported target exists, consider a legal shot. If the case is weak, hold or Load within the limit. Shooting Town loses the gun under current rules; do not use old suicide advice or assume unlimited ammunition.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Load and build a supported suspect list.
- **Middle:** Alternate useful shots with necessary loading and count ammunition.
- **Late:** Consider whether shooting changes the next vote; do not fire simply because a bullet remains.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Vigilante history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TK when appropriate. Explain load nights, shots, and failures accurately if full claiming.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use the old automatic-suicide rule. Do not shoot N1. Do not claim a fresh bullet without a valid load.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use the old automatic-suicide rule. Do not shoot N1. Do not claim a fresh bullet without a valid load.

**Important interaction:** Control can redirect your shot. Harmful visits interact with guards/traps; roleblocking can stop a load.

**Worked decision:** N1 load, N2 shoot #7: record the actual outcome. If the gun is lost after a Town shot, stop generating future shooting entries.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Complete the first Load.
2. Establish a case before firing.
3. Track ammunition and whether the gun is still usable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Complete the first Load.
2. Establish a case before firing.
3. Track ammunition and whether the gun is still usable.

- Load successfully and track ammunition.
- Shoot a supported evil suspect when the expected value justifies it.
- Reassess after every shot instead of shooting merely to prove yourself.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
