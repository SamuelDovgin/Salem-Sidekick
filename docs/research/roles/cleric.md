# Cleric

Default role family: **Town Protective**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 4540. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Barrier one player for Powerful Defense that night, stopping Basic and Powerful attacks.

**Key rule:** Your named-target attack notice means the target was attacked. Quiet nights give no save notice. Self Barrier has one use; Grace Period disables N1 protection.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Protect useful credible TI early when no stronger information exists.
2. Shift toward high-impact threatened allies or vulnerable protectors.
3. Save Self Barrier for a realistic threat to you.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Keep promising information sources alive.
- **Middle:** Move toward threatened high-impact allies or a useful protection chain.
- **Late:** Compare who must survive the next vote with your own need for Self Barrier.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TP under VFA. Reveal Cleric when necessary for coordination, credibility, or explaining a save.

## Read the information

A quiet protection night means no reported attack, not that you failed. A barrier cannot stop every attack tier.

## Common mistakes

Do not declare someone confirmed merely because you protected them. Do not walk onto an alerting Veteran casually. Do not promise protection through all threats.

## Interactions and limits

Unstoppable attacks bypass the barrier. Visiting can expose you to rampages even if the protected target survives.

## Worked decision

Barrier 4; if 4 is attacked, you can receive attack feedback while 4 receives the separate Barrier notice. Unstoppable attacks still bypass this defense.
