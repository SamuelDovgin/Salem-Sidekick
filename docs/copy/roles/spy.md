# Spy: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Plant persistent bugs on players. Bugs can later report evil visiting roles; bug placement and later triggers are different events. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Plant persistent bugs on players. Bugs can later report evil visiting roles; bug placement and later triggers are different events.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Place a Bug on one eligible player. Keep a persistent record because a Bug can remain relevant after its placement night. Choose a player likely to experience an informative effect, such as a credible useful ally under threat.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Place a Bug on one eligible player. Keep a persistent record because a Bug can remain relevant after its placement night.

**How to choose:** Choose a player likely to experience an informative effect, such as a credible useful ally under threat. With no reliable opening claim, choose an eligible unknown; do not infer that a lack of trigger makes them safe.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Track placements across nights. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record placement separately from any actual trigger.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record placement separately from any actual trigger. Preserve the feedback’s role/effect wording without guessing a player number or treating a quiet Bug as a failed placement.

**What this does and does not establish:** A trigger reports evil activity, not necessarily a player number. Framed or converted visitors can trigger as their real role.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Full claim Spy (TI) and report meaningful triggers with their night and affected player.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim Spy (TI) and report meaningful triggers with their night and affected player. Explain which part is a direct notification and which part is your interpretation.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Full claim with relevant triggers or an honest no-trigger log. Do not represent an absence as a positive discovery.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Place the next legal Bug where it can answer a new question, while retaining earlier Bugs in the log.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Place the next legal Bug where it can answer a new question, while retaining earlier Bugs in the log. If an old Bug triggers, attach the event to that Bug rather than claiming you visited there again that night.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Begin a bug network on informative likely targets.
- **Middle:** Track triggers from all surviving bugged houses.
- **Late:** Interpret absent triggers cautiously; combine the accumulated log with direct evidence and votes.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Spy history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Full claim with relevant triggers or an honest no-trigger log. Do not represent an absence as a positive discovery.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. ## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use ToS1 global visit logs. Do not use the obsolete catch-all event-bug template. Do not forget that prior bugs remain active.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use ToS1 global visit logs. Do not use the obsolete catch-all event-bug template. Do not forget that prior bugs remain active.

**Important interaction:** The wiki gives a narrow trigger description; exhaustiveness and some astral/frame interactions need care. No-trigger feedback is not universal proof of safety.

**Worked decision:** Bug #4 N1; N3 it reports Poisoner activity: record the N3 trigger on #4 rather than pretending you bugged them again.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Track placements across nights.
2. Preserve trigger scope.
3. Connect one actual effect to an independently checkable claim.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Track placements across nights.
2. Preserve trigger scope.
3. Connect one actual effect to an independently checkable claim.

- Bug players likely to attract useful traffic.
- Keep earlier living bugged players in view.
- Report the bugged target, trigger night, and exact role information received.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.

## Will notes — lower priority

**Compact copy**

New bug target; each active bug’s target and trigger night; reported role; no trigger/failure.

**Expanded copy — same section**

For a truthful Town draft, include only actions/results you actually performed or received.

**Fields:** New bug target; each active bug’s target and trigger night; reported role; no trigger/failure.

**Illustrative formatting only — not live data:**

```text
N1: Bug 4 — no trigger
N3: Bug on 4 — [reported role]
```

Bugs persist. Do not turn a reported role into an invented visitor number. Multiple active targets can generate entries.

Keep this below strategy. Start a new match with blank fields and no illustrative player numbers. Do not auto-fill success, a target’s role, a save, or a forged result.
