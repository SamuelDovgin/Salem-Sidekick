# Ritualist

Default role family: **Coven Killing**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5403. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Two Blood Ritual attempts guess an eligible exact role for an astral Unstoppable attack. TIs and Illuminated targets cannot normally be ritualed.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use genuinely supported exact-role knowledge.
2. Check target eligibility and illumination.
3. Coordinate ordinary book use against the value of a ritual.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Collect exact eligible role knowledge without wasting guesses.
- **Middle:** Spend attempts on supported targets after checking Illumination.
- **Late:** Choose ritual or ordinary attack based on remaining defenses and win requirements.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Oracle is a movement-compatible cover while using astral rituals, but lacks real protective feedback and becomes weaker after a normal book attack.

## Read the information

A wrong guess spends a charge without proving the target’s alternate role. Your private role certainty is not a result an Oracle could report.

## Common mistakes

Do not use old three-charge or public-failure-exposure rules. Do not guess TIs. Do not assume a Potion Master reveal immediately supplies a usable ritual target.

## Interactions and limits

Illumination lasts conditionally and matters to target eligibility. Ordinary book attacks and Blood Ritual are alternative actions.

## Worked decision

A public eligible exact role is known: check defenses and Illumination before committing one of only two attempts.
