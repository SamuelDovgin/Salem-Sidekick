# Admirer

Default role family: **Town Support**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6658. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Learn an Obsession’s subalignment, search for that player, then unlock two Cares. Two day Bestows grant lasting astral visits plus roleblock/control immunity.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Use claims matching the Obsession clue to narrow the search.
2. Bestow a trusted useful ally rather than a random strong claimant.
3. Save Care for a meaningful threat once unlocked.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Use the alignment clue to narrow your Obsession search.
- **Middle:** Bestow useful trusted allies and unlock Care where possible.
- **Late:** Spend remaining Care/Bestow on a concrete threat; do not assume your Obsession is the only valuable ally.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TS initially. Explain the clue and ability timeline when needed; do not expose unnecessary private ally information.

## Read the information

Failure to find an Obsession does not clear or condemn the checked player. A Bestow changes how later visits should be interpreted.

## Common mistakes

Do not use marriage/proposal or toxic-relationship templates. Do not Care before finding the Obsession. Do not invent an impossible Obsession category.

## Interactions and limits

An Obsession dying before discovery can be reassigned; death after discovery has different consequences. Care is astral.

## Worked decision

Your clue is TP and #4 claims protective: checking #4 is a reasoned search, not proof they are truthful.
