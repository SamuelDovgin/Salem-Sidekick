# Cultist

Default role family: **Coven Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6353. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

An Outlier with one day Indoctrinate converting an eligible Town player into your faction while retaining their role. Book use is separate.

## Win condition

Win with Coven by eliminating opposing factions; protecting the faction’s path can matter more than personal survival.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Check the special roledeck context.
2. Convert a player whose retained ability and influence help your team.
3. Update teammate knowledge and role/faction records.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Understand conversion eligibility and the special setup.
- **Middle:** Use the one conversion for a concrete faction benefit.
- **Late:** Track retained Town abilities and changed allegiance; role demonstration alone may mislead opponents.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Before book, a nonvisiting cover can fit movement. Your converted ally can truthfully hold a Town role while no longer being Town.

## Read the information

Historical confirmation does not guarantee current loyalty in conversion games.

## Common mistakes

Do not equate the converted player with a Town Traitor. Do not assume the target loses their ability. Do not apply the normal static faction model afterward.

## Interactions and limits

Cultist’s pre-conversion defense and special slot behavior are setup details. Converted players can receive the book unlike ordinary TT.

## Worked decision

An established Town ally becomes converted: their public role demonstration remains real, but the team objective has changed.
