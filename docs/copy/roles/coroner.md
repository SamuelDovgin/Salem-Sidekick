# Coroner: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TI — Town Investigative.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Autopsy a dead player during the day to collect killer information, then Examine living players at night against accumulated records. Without a suitable autopsy, the night check cannot provide that comparison. TI — Town Investigative.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Autopsy a dead player during the day to collect killer information, then Examine living players at night against accumulated records. Without a suitable autopsy, the night check cannot provide that comparison.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TI — Town Investigative. As TI, normally post your exact role and actual results from D2; an empty or failed N1 is still an honest history.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. ## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

In an ordinary start there is no eligible autopsy yet, so you have no useful N1 examination. Prepare a corpse-to-killer record instead of inventing a result. At D2, inspect the graveyard for an eligible autopsy.

**Expanded copy — same section**

In an ordinary start there is no eligible autopsy yet, so you have no useful N1 examination. Prepare a corpse-to-killer record instead of inventing a result.

**How to choose:** At D2, inspect the graveyard for an eligible autopsy. Choose a death whose killer record could answer an unresolved case. If no suitable body exists, record that limitation.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Create an eligible autopsy record. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the death information separately from your own later examination.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the death information separately from your own later examination. The killer shown by an autopsy is not a numbered player identification.

**What this does and does not establish:** No match means no match to collected killer records, not globally innocent. A true role result does not automatically establish faction or voluntary guilt.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

After a valid autopsy, examine an eligible living suspect and record Match/No Match or the actual failure feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

After a valid autopsy, examine an eligible living suspect and record Match/No Match or the actual failure feedback. A No Match excludes only the killer records your examination covers, not every evil role.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Wait for a useful death and collect an actual autopsy.
- **Middle:** Build multiple relevant killer records and examine plausible candidates.
- **Late:** Explain exactly what a negative examination excludes; accumulated records still are not a global role check.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not claim a meaningful N1 examination without records. Do not treat Shrouded Town killers as necessarily evil. Do not assume changing bookholders erases old evidence.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not claim a meaningful N1 examination without records. Do not treat Shrouded Town killers as necessarily evil. Do not assume changing bookholders erases old evidence.

**Important interaction:** Coroner resists ordinary Enchanter/Illusionist result changes. Role changes and Shroud need context; the source marks some counterattack interactions untested.

**Worked decision:** D2 autopsy #6 killed by Coven; N2 examine #9: record both the collected killer role and the actual examination result.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Create an eligible autopsy record.
2. Examine a relevant suspect.
3. Explain exactly which killer records a match concerns.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Create an eligible autopsy record.
2. Examine a relevant suspect.
3. Explain exactly which killer records a match concerns.

- Autopsy deaths whose killer remains relevant.
- Keep a cumulative record rather than replacing yesterday’s autopsy.
- Examine plausible suspects and share the precise scope of any match.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
