## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Jinx an eligible house to attack a visitor if the action is enabled. You do not grant Crusader-style defense to the resident. Choose a nonally house likely to draw useful enemy visitors, while coordinating to keep Coven away.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Jinx an eligible house to attack a visitor if the action is enabled. You do not grant Crusader-style defense to the resident.

**How to choose:** Choose a nonally house likely to draw useful enemy visitors, while coordinating to keep Coven away. With no useful traffic plan, holding can be better than exposing yourself to surviving witnesses. The normal visitor attack excludes Coven allies. Grace Period may disable the harmful action.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Plan around visitor traffic. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record actual attack feedback and remember that surviving witnesses can expose you.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record actual attack feedback and remember that surviving witnesses can expose you. Do not turn the action into a complete visitor list or claim the house owner received protection.

**What this does and does not establish:** Knowing that you attacked a visitor is not a complete visitor list. A survivor can carry evidence against you even if nobody dies.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If exposed, revise the survival plan rather than continuing a supposed safe protective routine.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If exposed, revise the survival plan rather than continuing a supposed safe protective routine. If not exposed, choose a useful house using D2 traffic and avoid allied visitors; recheck whether the book changes your action.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Choose traffic carefully to limit identity exposure.
- **Middle:** Exploit useful visitors while planning for survivors who can report you.
- **Late:** Judge whether an extra visitor kill outweighs being identified before the final vote.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Crusader — TP (Town Protective); Partial effect fit; difficult.** Both can create danger for visitors at a chosen house. **What can expose it:** Jinx supplies no Crusader defense; death details and surviving witnesses can identify you.

**Psychic — TI (Town Investigative); Visiting fallback.** The selected house can fit a normal one-target visit. **What can expose it:** You have no genuine vision and a survivor may directly expose the Jinx action.
