# Poisoner

Default role family: **Coven Utility**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6228. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Choose Drink to roleblock a player, or Smog to obstruct visits into their house.

**Key rule:** Drink affects the resident’s action; Smog affects incoming visitors. Smog cannot accompany a book attack and can obstruct Coven teammates.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose between disabling a player and blocking access to a house.
2. Coordinate so Smog does not sabotage allied visits.
3. Preserve the cover’s expected feedback when the book arrives.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Choose a valuable Drink target and a consistent cover.
- **Middle:** Use Smog only when blocking access is better than direct disruption.
- **Late:** Account for book constraints and whether your prior cover still explains tonight’s action.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Tavern Keeper naturally matches ordinary Drink. Socialite can resemble Smog outcomes but has different rules and is only a conditional advanced cover.

## Read the information

A target reporting roleblock can support your cover; it does not make you Town. A Smog report is not a normal roleblock.

## Common mistakes

Do not use outdated delayed-poison advice. Do not conflate Drink with Smog. Do not claim a roleblock lock through Hangover.

## Interactions and limits

Real Socialites and Spies can expose Smog-based stories. Some Coven astral interactions differ from ordinary astral bypass.

## Worked decision

Smog 4 can stop your own teammate reaching 4. Drink 4 instead tries to stop 4 from acting.
