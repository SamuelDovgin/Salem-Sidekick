# Admirer: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TS — Town Support.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Learn an Obsession’s subalignment, search for that player, then unlock two Cares. Two day Bestows grant lasting astral visits plus roleblock/control immunity. TS — Town Support.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Learn an Obsession’s subalignment, search for that player, then unlock two Cares. Two day Bestows grant lasting astral visits plus roleblock/control immunity.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Read the Obsession clue, then Admire an eligible player whose likely alignment you can meaningfully compare with it. Prefer a compatible supported claim.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Read the Obsession clue, then Admire an eligible player whose likely alignment you can meaningfully compare with it.

**How to choose:** Prefer a compatible supported claim. With no useful D1 claim, choose an eligible unknown and treat the result as a limited comparison. Check the actual eligibility restrictions rather than trying a revealed TPow, Outlier, or Amnesiac.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Use the Obsession clue. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record the admiration result and whether Care became available.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record the admiration result and whether Care became available. The Obsession clue is not a public role confirmation, and no Care charge should be invented before unlock.

**What this does and does not establish:** Failure to find an Obsession does not clear or condemn the checked player. A Bestow changes how later visits should be interpreted.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TS when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS when asked. Share a concrete useful result when it helps without overstating its scope. Daytime Bestow changes an ally’s ability behavior; explain that coordination separately from your night action.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Claim TS initially. Explain the clue and ability timeline when needed; do not expose unnecessary private ally information.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If Care is unlocked and a supported ally is threatened, consider spending it.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If Care is unlocked and a supported ally is threatened, consider spending it. Otherwise choose a useful legal admiration action. Track Care and Bestow charges independently; do not follow old marriage/death-link advice.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Use the alignment clue to narrow your Obsession search.
- **Middle:** Bestow useful trusted allies and unlock Care where possible.
- **Late:** Spend remaining Care/Bestow on a concrete threat; do not assume your Obsession is the only valuable ally.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Admirer history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS initially. Explain the clue and ability timeline when needed; do not expose unnecessary private ally information.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use marriage/proposal or toxic-relationship templates. Do not Care before finding the Obsession. Do not invent an impossible Obsession category.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use marriage/proposal or toxic-relationship templates. Do not Care before finding the Obsession. Do not invent an impossible Obsession category.

**Important interaction:** An Obsession dying before discovery can be reassigned; death after discovery has different consequences. Care is astral.

**Worked decision:** Your clue is TP and #4 claims protective: checking #4 is a reasoned search, not proof they are truthful.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Use the Obsession clue.
2. Confirm whether Care unlocked.
3. Spend support charges on a reasoned ally.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Use the Obsession clue.
2. Confirm whether Care unlocked.
3. Spend support charges on a reasoned ally.

- Use claims matching the Obsession clue to narrow the search.
- Bestow a trusted useful ally rather than a random strong claimant.
- Save Care for a meaningful threat once unlocked.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
