# Marshal

Default role family: **Town Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5387. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Start a daytime Tribunal to execute nominated players without the usual defense and judgment.

**Key rule:** Two Tribunal uses, normally up to two executions per Tribunal. Roles and wills stay hidden until the Tribunal ends. No night ability.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Have a clear evidence-supported voting plan before activating.
2. Explain the intended targets to allies.
3. Count votes and avoid handing rapid executions to an opposing bloc.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Collect evidence rather than Tribunaling solely to reveal.
- **Middle:** Prepare a coherent two-target plan before activation.
- **Late:** Check whether an opposing voting bloc would control the accelerated executions.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Start with TK rather than TPow; Deputy is the default temporary cover before Tribunal.

**End the cover when:** Explain supported cases and coordinate a voting plan before activating Tribunal. Use it when Town can control the nominations, not solely to prove your claim.

This is an editorial concealment default, not proof that a cover is best in every lobby. Keep real actions private and do not fabricate the claimed role’s effects.

## Read the information

A Tribunal is a public demonstration, not proof your targets are evil. Lack of intermediate flips makes the second execution particularly risky.

## Common mistakes

Do not activate just to confirm yourself. Do not expect immediate feedback after the first victim. Do not use an outdated three-execution default.

## Interactions and limits

Jesters and Executioners interact with Tribunal deaths; modifiers can change trial capacity. Your bonus vote is tied to the active Tribunal.

## Worked decision

If you execute 4, you must decide whether to execute 9 before seeing 4’s role. Have evidence for both first.
