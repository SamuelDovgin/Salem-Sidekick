# Cleric: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TP — Town Protective.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Barrier grants a player Powerful Defense for the night. One Self Barrier is available; attack feedback is separate from merely choosing a target. TP — Town Protective.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Barrier grants a player Powerful Defense for the night. One Self Barrier is available; attack feedback is separate from merely choosing a target.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TP — Town Protective. When the lobby asks for alignment, TP is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Barrier one eligible player if the control is enabled. With Grace Period, protection options can be restricted; an unavailable control is a setup limit, not a mistake. Prefer an independently supported useful player likely to be attacked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Barrier one eligible player if the control is enabled. With Grace Period, protection options can be restricted; an unavailable control is a setup limit, not a mistake.

**How to choose:** Prefer an independently supported useful player likely to be attacked. With no evidence, choose an eligible unknown without claiming certainty about them. Save the single self-Barrier unless a concrete reason makes using it worthwhile.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Make one legal Barrier choice. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record whom you Barrierd and any real attack/protection feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record whom you Barrierd and any real attack/protection feedback. Survival alone is not evidence that you saved them. If the action failed, preserve the failure instead of writing a successful heal.

**What this does and does not establish:** A quiet protection night means no reported attack, not that you failed. A barrier cannot stop every attack tier.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TP when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TP when asked. Give the exact Cleric claim when useful for coordination or an event dispute, and distinguish “I used Barrier” from “I prevented a death.”

Your actual alignment is TP — Town Protective. When the lobby asks for alignment, TP is your default shorthand.

Claim TP under VFA. Reveal Cleric when necessary for coordination, credibility, or explaining a save.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

Update the protection target using D2 claims, threats, and other protection.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Update the protection target using D2 claims, threats, and other protection. Protect the player whose survival is useful now, not automatically the loudest person or the same target forever.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Keep promising information sources alive.
- **Middle:** Move toward threatened high-impact allies or a useful protection chain.
- **Late:** Compare who must survive the next vote with your own need for Self Barrier.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Cleric history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TP under VFA. Reveal Cleric when necessary for coordination, credibility, or explaining a save.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not declare someone confirmed merely because you protected them. Do not walk onto an alerting Veteran casually. Do not promise protection through all threats.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not declare someone confirmed merely because you protected them. Do not walk onto an alerting Veteran casually. Do not promise protection through all threats.

**Important interaction:** Unstoppable attacks bypass the barrier. Visiting can expose you to rampages even if the protected target survives.

**Worked decision:** #4 is providing useful results and is under threat: barrier them, then record whether the game reported an attack.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Make one legal Barrier choice.
2. Record actual outcomes honestly.
3. Reassess who needs protection after D2.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Make one legal Barrier choice.
2. Record actual outcomes honestly.
3. Reassess who needs protection after D2.

- Protect useful credible TI early when no stronger information exists.
- Shift toward high-impact threatened allies or vulnerable protectors.
- Save Self Barrier for a realistic threat to you.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
