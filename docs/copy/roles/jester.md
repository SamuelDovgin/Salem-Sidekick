# Jester: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: NE — Neutral Evil.** Default faction: Neutral. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Complete the daily text-mode Chatterbox requirement and get hanged. Other deaths do not win. A valid hanging unlocks revenge against eligible voters. NE — Neutral Evil.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Complete the daily text-mode Chatterbox requirement and get hanged. Other deaths do not win. A valid hanging unlocks revenge against eligible voters.

**Win condition:** Meet the participation prerequisite and get hanged through a valid route; ordinary killing does not satisfy the objective.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no ordinary N1 kill. Prepare a believable reason Town might hang you; the Chatterbox requirement starts on D2 in this text profile. No night target is needed before you are hanged.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no ordinary N1 kill. Prepare a believable reason Town might hang you; the Chatterbox requirement starts on D2 in this text profile.

**How to choose:** No night target is needed before you are hanged. Choose a story that can attract suspicion without making the lobby ignore you as an obvious Jester. Do not assume a night death wins.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Meet the active communication quota. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Check that you survived and read the actual D2 communication quota.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Check that you survived and read the actual D2 communication quota. Your temporary defense does not last forever; track its expiration instead of assuming permanent safety.

**What this does and does not establish:** Being suspected is not sufficient; death type and participation both matter. Neutral Evil early defense expires.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Complete the actual Chatterbox requirement and participate in a plausible disputed claim.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Complete the actual Chatterbox requirement and participate in a plausible disputed claim. Your objective differs from an evil trying to look perfectly trustworthy: you need a valid hanging while meeting the role conditions.

Your actual alignment is NE — Neutral Evil. This is private actual-role information. Your public alignment comes from the selected cover, not from this label.

An imperfect Oracle/Deputy or deliberately weak investigative cover is goal-dependent. A convincing “safe” claim may work against your objective.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If hanged and the game offers Haunt, choose an eligible target using the actual selection rules.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If hanged and the game offers Haunt, choose an eligible target using the actual selection rules. If alive, update the story and quota; do not write a Haunt merely because someone voted against you.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Complete the quota and avoid an obvious wrong-kind death.
- **Middle:** Use believable suspicion to invite a hanging.
- **Late:** Reassess whether survival helps; a perfect cover may now deny the objective.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Deputy (TK): goal-dependent weak claim; Sheriff (TI): goal-dependent flawed ti story. All roles remain selectable.

**Expanded copy — same section**

**Deputy — TK (Town Killing); Goal-dependent weak claim.** An inability to produce a useful shot can create plausible suspicion. **What can expose it:** Town may identify the Jester goal and refuse to hang you; quota and hanging conditions still matter.

**Sheriff — TI (Town Investigative); Goal-dependent flawed TI story.** A plausibly disputed report can make Town suspect you. **What can expose it:** An impossible report can make you too obvious to hang, and a night kill does not satisfy your goal.

These are starting explanations, not a whitelist. Choose any role from the full roster, including a weak fit, and read that claim’s real obligations inline. Matching one visible visit is weaker than matching movement, result scope, target feedback, and later demonstration together. Keep actual actions private and deliberately author the public cover; do not treat invented cover text as a genuine game result.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not stay silent below the quota. Do not rely on whispers or Last Words for progress. Do not confuse being shot with being hanged.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not stay silent below the quota. Do not rely on whispers or Last Words for progress. Do not confuse being shot with being hanged.

**Important interaction:** Prosecution and Tribunal have different revenge eligibility. Voice-mode Shufflebox is outside this baseline.

**Worked decision:** You are being questioned on the stand: contribute eligible messages before judgment and aim for the actual hanging condition.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.
