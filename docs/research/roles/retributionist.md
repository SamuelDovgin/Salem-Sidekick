# Retributionist

Default role family: **Town Support**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5202. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Raise an eligible dead Town player, then choose where their borrowed ability acts. You receive that ability’s feedback.

**Key rule:** You visit the corpse; the zombie visits its destination. You need a usable body, cannot normally use Stoned corpses or protect yourself, and are roleblock/control immune.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose an eligible corpse with a useful action.
2. Check corpse availability and target restrictions.
3. Report useful returned information with the corpse and destination.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Observe the graveyard and avoid inventing a usable N1 corpse.
- **Middle:** Choose the corpse action that addresses the current threat.
- **Late:** Use the available body’s real limits and account for another corpse user taking priority.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TS as appropriate; share corpse-generated TI information when useful without disguising where it came from.

## Read the information

Corpse activity proves a corpse-using ability, not automatically Town allegiance. A missing corpse can reflect another user taking priority.

## Common mistakes

Do not use stoned bodies as a standard Town Ret. Do not self-protect through a corpse. Do not claim actions from an unsuitable body.

## Interactions and limits

Necromancer wins corpse-use priority. Altered bodies retain underlying ability behavior; converted/flexed Ret rules differ.

## Worked decision

Raise dead Sheriff 6 to check living 9. Report both numbers and the borrowed result; an unavailable corpse gives no check.
