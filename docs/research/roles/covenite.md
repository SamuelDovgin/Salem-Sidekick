# Covenite

Default role family: **Coven Outlier**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6354. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

You have no special role ability. Coordinate with Coven; if you hold the book, use its night attack.

**Key rule:** No book means no Covenite night action. The role must be permitted by the setup; team information is not your own investigation.

## Win condition

Help Coven eliminate Town and other opposing factions. You share Coven’s victory even if dead; your special effect is a tool, not a separate win quota.

This assumes the default Coven faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Contribute to coordination and vote pressure.
2. Consider carrying the book when it preserves more useful allies.
3. Maintain a cover appropriate to your current movement.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Participate and consider book duties.
- **Middle:** Buy useful time with a consistent claim rather than inventing powers.
- **Late:** Focus on the actual voting/kill objective; a fragile public-reveal claim may not survive a demonstration request.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Before book, nonvisiting covers such as Oracle are movement-compatible but unproved. With book, reassess direct-visiting options.

## Read the information

Teammate information may support a fabricated will, but does not create the ability to demonstrate its claim.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim a natural role ability you do not possess. Do not treat no visible visit as safety from all investigation. Do not assume a permanent no-visit cover after receiving book.

## Interactions and limits

Public-reveal Town roles remain poor durable covers. Outlier legality matters to both true and fake claims.

## Worked decision

An N1 hold without the book is normal. Do not invent a target or result merely to fill your private history.
