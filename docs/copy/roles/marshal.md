# Marshal: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TPow — Town Power.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Two day Tribunals reveal you and allow immediate executions on nomination, normally up to two victims. Reveals and wills are delayed until it ends. TPow — Town Power.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Two day Tribunals reveal you and allow immediate executions on nomination, normally up to two victims. Reveals and wills are delayed until it ends.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TPow — Town Power. When the lobby asks for alignment, TPow is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

You have no ordinary N1 action. Prepare a short list of unresolved claims and the evidence you would need before starting a Tribunal. Read D1 for contradictions and useful claims.

**Expanded copy — same section**

You have no ordinary N1 action. Prepare a short list of unresolved claims and the evidence you would need before starting a Tribunal.

**How to choose:** Read D1 for contradictions and useful claims. With no evidence, write “No case yet” and save the ability; the absence of an opening target is normal.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Build one evidence-backed case. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Collect deaths and investigative posts into separate cases.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Collect deaths and investigative posts into separate cases. A Tribunal increases voting capacity; it does not tell you which players are evil.

**What this does and does not establish:** A Tribunal is a public demonstration, not proof your targets are evil. Lack of intermediate flips makes the second execution particularly risky.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TPow when an alignment is requested.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TPow when an alignment is requested. Do not start a Tribunal just to prove your role. First identify supported targets and whether Town can organize the votes without losing control.

Your actual alignment is TPow — Town Power. When the lobby asks for alignment, TPow is your default shorthand.

Keep exact identity concealed where useful until the public ability’s benefit warrants revealing.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

There is still no night button to use.

**Expanded copy — same section**

There is still no night button to use. Review the outcome of D2 votes and prepare one question for each surviving case. Reassess numbers before a later Tribunal rather than carrying a failed push forward unchanged.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Collect evidence rather than Tribunaling solely to reveal.
- **Middle:** Prepare a coherent two-target plan before activation.
- **Late:** Check whether an opposing voting bloc would control the accelerated executions.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Marshal history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Keep exact identity concealed where useful until the public ability’s benefit warrants revealing.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not activate just to confirm yourself. Do not expect immediate feedback after the first victim. Do not use an outdated three-execution default.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not activate just to confirm yourself. Do not expect immediate feedback after the first victim. Do not use an outdated three-execution default.

**Important interaction:** Jesters and Executioners interact with Tribunal deaths; modifiers can change trial capacity. Your bonus vote is tied to the active Tribunal.

**Worked decision:** Two supported contradictions exist and allies can coordinate: plan both nominations before starting the Tribunal.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Build one evidence-backed case.
2. Check whether extra trials help Town.
3. Spend the ability with an organized voting plan.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Build one evidence-backed case.
2. Check whether extra trials help Town.
3. Spend the ability with an organized voting plan.

- Have a clear evidence-supported voting plan before activating.
- Explain the intended targets to allies.
- Count votes and avoid handing rapid executions to an opposing bloc.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.

## Will notes — lower priority

**Compact copy**

D Tribunal; nominated/executed players; optional reason; remaining charges.

**Expanded copy — same section**

For a truthful Town draft, include only actions/results you actually performed or received.

**Fields:** D Tribunal; nominated/executed players; optional reason; remaining charges.

**Illustrative formatting only — not live data:**

```text
D2: Tribunal — 4, 9
```

Two charges; one use/day. These numbers are actual executed players, not assumed guilty findings.

Keep this below strategy. Start a new match with blank fields and no illustrative player numbers. Do not auto-fill success, a target’s role, a save, or a forged result.
