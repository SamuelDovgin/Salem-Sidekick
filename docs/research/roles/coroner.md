# Coroner

Default role family: **Town Investigative**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6348. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Autopsy a dead player during the day to collect killer information, then Examine living players at night against accumulated records. Without a suitable autopsy, the night check cannot provide that comparison.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Autopsy deaths whose killer remains relevant.
2. Keep a cumulative record rather than replacing yesterday’s autopsy.
3. Examine plausible suspects and share the precise scope of any match.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Wait for a useful death and collect an actual autopsy.
- **Middle:** Build multiple relevant killer records and examine plausible candidates.
- **Late:** Explain exactly what a negative examination excludes; accumulated records still are not a global role check.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Full claim with useful information normally from D2. An honest N1 no-autopsy state is preferable to an invented result.

## Read the information

No match means no match to collected killer records, not globally innocent. A true role result does not automatically establish faction or voluntary guilt.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim a meaningful N1 examination without records. Do not treat Shrouded Town killers as necessarily evil. Do not assume changing bookholders erases old evidence.

## Interactions and limits

Coroner resists ordinary Enchanter/Illusionist result changes. Role changes and Shroud need context; the source marks some counterattack interactions untested.

## Worked decision

D2 autopsy #6 killed by Coven; N2 examine #9: record both the collected killer role and the actual examination result.
