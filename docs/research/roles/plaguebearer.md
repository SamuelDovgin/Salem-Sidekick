# Plaguebearer

Default role family: **Neutral Apocalypse**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5579. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Infect players until every other living player is infected. Contact with infected players spreads the infection; your two starting neighbors begin infected.

**Key rule:** Completing infection transforms you into Pestilence. Passive spread counts too; already infected players are not new progress.

## Win condition

Win with Apocalypse by eliminating its opponents. Transforming unlocks your stronger form; transformation alone is not a win.

This assumes the default Apocalypse faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose uninfected players likely to spread contact.
2. Distinguish seeded infection from nightly evidence.
3. Use propagation information without inventing a complete visitor list.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Separate seeded infections from useful new spread.
- **Middle:** Target remaining uninfected opponents and use propagation carefully.
- **Late:** After Pestilence, manage stack-based pressure and the day vote rather than pretending it is a conventional rampage.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Tracker/Lookout are conditional information fits. Infections can support parts of those stories but are not automatically exhaustive or equivalent every night.

## Read the information

Already infected players do not create a fresh infection event for every visit. Infection history is broader and less direct than a clean observer log.

## Common mistakes

Do not count starting neighbor infections as N1 visitors. Do not assert every missing infection means no visit. Do not publish unobserved destinations.

## Interactions and limits

Astral contact does not spread infection normally. Solo/multi-member Apocalypse changes detection and defense.

## Worked decision

Infect an active visitor so their contacts spread infection. Watch the living infection markers, not just your direct target list.

## Transformed state

Pestilence: infection becomes stack-based danger; reaching three stacks is lethal while the effect is active. Its own spread action can force maximum stacks and is not a conventional harmful attack. Do not use the old ToS1 passive-rampage model.
