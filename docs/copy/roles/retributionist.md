# Retributionist: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TS — Town Support.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Raise a suitable dead Town body and direct its ability. You visit the corpse; the corpse visits the living destination. Normally roleblock/control immune. TS — Town Support.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Raise a suitable dead Town body and direct its ability. You visit the corpse; the corpse visits the living destination. Normally roleblock/control immune.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

In an ordinary opening there is no usable dead Town role, so you normally have no N1 reanimation. No corpse target is available merely because you selected a cover template.

**Expanded copy — same section**

In an ordinary opening there is no usable dead Town role, so you normally have no N1 reanimation.

**How to choose:** No corpse target is available merely because you selected a cover template. Plan to inspect the D2 graveyard for eligible Town abilities and their remaining usability.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Identify a usable Town corpse. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Log “No eligible corpse” if that is true, then inspect real deaths.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Log “No eligible corpse” if that is true, then inspect real deaths. Do not claim the dead player’s original result as a result you personally obtained.

**What this does and does not establish:** Corpse activity proves a corpse-using ability, not automatically Town allegiance. A missing corpse can reflect another user taking priority.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TS if asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS if asked. Explain your actual corpse use when relevant. An honest empty N1 is more accurate than inventing a dead Sheriff before any Sheriff died.

Your actual alignment is TS — Town Support. When the lobby asks for alignment, TS is your default shorthand.

Claim TS as appropriate; share corpse-generated TI information when useful without disguising where it came from.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If an eligible usable corpse exists, choose its ability and a destination that answers a current need.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If an eligible usable corpse exists, choose its ability and a destination that answers a current need. If none exists, wait. Preserve corpse eligibility, exhausted uses, and restrictions on self-protection rather than forcing an action.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Observe the graveyard and avoid inventing a usable N1 corpse.
- **Middle:** Choose the corpse action that addresses the current threat.
- **Late:** Use the available body’s real limits and account for another corpse user taking priority.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Claim choices and logical fit

**Compact copy**

Keep a truthful Retributionist history. You may browse or select any claim; recommendations do not lock the picker.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TS as appropriate; share corpse-generated TI information when useful without disguising where it came from.

A genuine Town player normally needs truthful disclosure rather than a fabricated investigation. Choosing a different public role creates that role’s obligations without giving you its ability. Read the selected cover’s exact action timing and evidence limits before using it. The all-role selector remains available even when an option is difficult or unsupported.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not use stoned bodies as a standard Town Ret. Do not self-protect through a corpse. Do not claim actions from an unsuitable body.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not use stoned bodies as a standard Town Ret. Do not self-protect through a corpse. Do not claim actions from an unsuitable body.

**Important interaction:** Necromancer wins corpse-use priority. Altered bodies retain underlying ability behavior; converted/flexed Ret rules differ.

**Worked decision:** N2 use dead Sheriff #6 on #9: preserve both numbers and the returned check; do not say you personally visited #9.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Identify a usable Town corpse.
2. Choose its ability for a current need.
3. Record the borrowed action and actual outcome.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Identify a usable Town corpse.
2. Choose its ability for a current need.
3. Record the borrowed action and actual outcome.

- Choose an eligible corpse with a useful action.
- Check corpse availability and target restrictions.
- Report useful returned information with the corpse and destination.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
