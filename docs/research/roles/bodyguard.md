# Bodyguard

Default role family: **Town Protective**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 5558. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Guard against one direct harmful visitor, canceling that action and counterattacking. Successful interception endangers you. Two self-protection uses grant Basic Defense.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Guard a credible high-value ally likely to be attacked.
2. Consider protection on you when coordinating a chain.
3. Reassess whether the expected attack is interceptable.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Protect a credible useful ally rather than a random loud claim.
- **Middle:** Coordinate protection on yourself if an interception is likely.
- **Late:** Consider the actual remaining attack types before sacrificing yourself for a role whose ability may be spent.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TP when needed; give a full claim when coordination or a contradiction makes it useful. Keep exact protection plans out of unnecessary public discussion.

## Read the information

A guard attempt is not a save. A death despite your guard can involve additional attacks or an astral bypass.

## Common mistakes

Do not say you grant a normal barrier. Do not expect to stop all attacks. Do not assume dying to protect someone proves their faction.

## Interactions and limits

Barrier protection can preserve you during a counterattack. Astral attacks bypass interception; Shroud can make an otherwise friendly visitor harmful.

## Worked decision

A revealed leader is threatened by ordinary killing: guarding them may trade your life for theirs, whereas an expected astral attack calls for another plan.
