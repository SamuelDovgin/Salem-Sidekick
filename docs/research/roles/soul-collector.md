# Soul Collector

Default role family: **Neutral Apocalypse**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6562. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Astrally Reap players for extra souls when they die, plus passive nightly progress. Reap also frames targets. Seven souls transform you into Death.

## Win condition

Win with the Apocalypse faction by eliminating its opponents. Transformation advances your power; it is not itself the final win condition.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Reap likely deaths rather than safe survivors.
2. Track passive and target-death gains separately.
3. Maintain a cover consistent with astral movement.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Reap likely deaths without breaking a no-visit story.
- **Middle:** Track passive and extra souls and prepare for transformation visibility.
- **Late:** After Death, survival through the day is the urgent objective.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Oracle is movement-compatible but lacks real protection. Sheriff can align with framing effects only as an information story and conflicts with your astral no-visit pattern.

## Read the information

A target appearing suspicious does not expose their actual role. Your framing cannot be treated as a genuine Town check.

## Common mistakes

Do not use a normal visiting cover without acknowledging the risk. Do not assume Reap itself kills. Do not miss the day urgency after transformation.

## Interactions and limits

Reap can override other apparent-faction effects. Source prose differs on some faction nuances; default advice is explicitly normal Apocalypse.

## Worked decision

A prominent player is likely to die tonight: reaping them can advance progress, while your public will must not reveal private soul knowledge.

## Transformed state

Death: survive the day after the public transformation so the end-of-day Apocalypse can occur. Vote pressure, not ordinary night defense, becomes the central threat. Keep private soul counts out of a cover will.
