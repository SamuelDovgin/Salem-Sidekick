# Crusader: complete interface copy

**Actual alignment: TP — Town Protective.** Default faction: Town. Copy review: 2026-10-15.

The compact text and its expanded text belong to the **same section on the single page**. The expansion is additional explanation, not a second role page or reading drawer. Examples are illustrative and must never be inserted into live match fields.

## Your role and immediate job

**Compact copy**

Fortify grants Powerful Defense and attacks one random direct visitor with Basic Attack, regardless of whether that visitor is friendly. TP — Town Protective.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Fortify grants Powerful Defense and attacks one random direct visitor with Basic Attack, regardless of whether that visitor is friendly.

**Win condition:** Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

Your actual alignment is TP — Town Protective. When the lobby asks for alignment, TP is your default shorthand.

Baseline: vanilla ToS2 text chat, normal faction assignments. An eligible target means one the current client permits. Check the role card, enabled controls, setup modifiers, and actual feedback before following a conditional action. Grace Period can suppress harmful opening actions. Unknown setup is not evidence that an action succeeded.

## First night: action and target

**Compact copy**

Fortify one eligible player while remembering that your ability can attack a visitor, including a Town visitor. Prefer a credible useful ally away from an obvious crowded call.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Fortify one eligible player while remembering that your ability can attack a visitor, including a Town visitor.

**How to choose:** Prefer a credible useful ally away from an obvious crowded call. With no reliable opening target, choose a less congested eligible house or hold if the collateral risk is unjustified; ask for coordination rather than visiting a pile blindly.

**Your opening checkpoint:** Protect without inviting a Town pileup. You do not need a perfect read of the lobby to begin; you need a legal action or a deliberate hold and an honest record of what happened.

## At dawn: record and interpret

**Compact copy**

Record your target and actual attack/protection feedback.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Record your target and actual attack/protection feedback. A visitor’s death does not prove they were evil, and your selected target is not automatically confirmed by receiving defense.

**What this does and does not establish:** Your attack does not identify an evil visitor. Failure to kill can reflect defense rather than the absence of a visitor.

Keep intended action, actual feedback, and your interpretation in separate sentences. “No result” must not silently become a negative result, a successful save, or confirmation of another player’s faction.

## D2: what to say

**Compact copy**

Claim TP when asked.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Claim TP when asked. When coordination is needed, make the Crusader danger clear to trusted protectives so they do not pile onto the same house. Do not announce an unconditional safe visiting target.

Your actual alignment is TP — Town Protective. When the lobby asks for alignment, TP is your default shorthand.

Claim TP first where appropriate. Full claim when your visitor hazard matters to allied plans.

Private sharing needs its own trust reason: a loud claim, a friendly whisper, or your untested guess is not enough to confirm the recipient. Before sending a private plan, ask what is gained by sharing that detail now.

## N2 and later: next decisions

**Compact copy**

If D2 provides a better protection target, move with a reason and consider who will visit.

**Expanded copy — same section**

If D2 provides a better protection target, move with a reason and consider who will visit. If your prior fortification killed a Town visitor, change the traffic plan instead of repeating it mechanically.

**How priorities change:**

- **Early:** Avoid uncontrolled crowded protection calls.
- **Middle:** Coordinate a valuable low-traffic protection target.
- **Late:** Choose protection that preserves the necessary vote while reducing allied visitor casualties.

Reassess from the actual outcome, not from the outcome you hoped for. If you are redirected, converted, retrained, or transformed, keep the previous action history and switch to the current legal role/state instructions.

## Mistakes and important interactions

**Compact copy**

Do not pile onto a crowded TP/LO call unannounced. Do not treat a surviving visitor as a unique role. Do not assume you attack every visitor.

**Expanded copy — same section**

Do not pile onto a crowded TP/LO call unannounced. Do not treat a surviving visitor as a unique role. Do not assume you attack every visitor.

**Important interaction:** Astral actions avoid visitor retaliation. A visiting protector can die in a rampage even when Fortify saves the target.

**Worked decision:** Two allies intend to visit the same leader: coordinate another protection target rather than risking killing one of them.

A counterexample is a reason to narrow your conclusion. If an unresolved interaction matters to this decision, use the source note and the actual client feedback rather than turning it into a hard clearance or accusation.

## Your subgoals

**Compact copy**

1. Protect without inviting a Town pileup.
2. Separate a visitor kill from guilt.
3. Coordinate the next night’s traffic.

**Expanded copy — same section**

1. Protect without inviting a Town pileup.
2. Separate a visitor kill from guilt.
3. Coordinate the next night’s traffic.

- Choose valuable targets with controlled visitor traffic.
- Coordinate enough to reduce friendly casualties.
- Record separately whether your target was attacked and you struck a visitor.

These checkpoints describe useful decisions, not guaranteed outcomes. A correct hold, a failed action recorded accurately, or a revised case can satisfy the learning goal even when the match goes badly.
