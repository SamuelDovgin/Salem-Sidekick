# Jailor

Default role family: **Town Power**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6571. No live-client test is claimed.

## Role and ability

Select a prisoner during the day, question them privately at night, and optionally execute from N2. Three executions; killing Town removes further abilities.

## Win condition

Win with Town by eliminating opposing factions; the role’s ability is a tool toward that shared objective.

The statement assumes the default faction profile. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the applicable faction objective. A role swap alone does not change your faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose unresolved players whose claims can be tested.
2. Ask for role, will, and reasoning before deciding.
3. Reserve executions for supported cases and changing vote control.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Question unresolved players and preserve a complete private log.
- **Middle:** Execute supported evils while accounting for dangerous prisoner types.
- **Late:** Count votes and remaining charges; the wrong execution can remove both your power and a needed Town vote.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Consider how public identity affects survival. Use alignment-first disclosure where appropriate; reveal when coordination or defense requires it.

## Read the information

Jail blocks ordinary direct visits but has exceptions. No kill while someone is jailed is a clue rather than unique proof.

## Common mistakes

Do not execute N1. Do not repeat the same prisoner on consecutive nights. Do not rely on jail to stop every astral effect.

## Interactions and limits

Execution is Powerful, not universally unstoppable. Some astral protection can save a prisoner; dangerous prisoners can retaliate.

## Worked decision

D2 select #7; N2 compare their exact claim with D2 statements before choosing whether to execute.
