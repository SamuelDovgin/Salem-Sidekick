# Tavern Keeper

Default role family: **Town Support**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 4009. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

Drink with a player to attempt to roleblock their night action.

**Key rule:** After a successful block, Hangover prevents an immediate repeat block or Control. You are roleblock immune; visiting certain killers can make them retaliate.

## Win condition

Help Town eliminate its enemies, including Coven and hostile killing factions. You share Town’s victory even if dead; your ability has no separate win quota.

This assumes the default Town faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Block a credible harmful-action suspect.
2. Compare their claimed outcome with your actual feedback.
3. Alternate targets rather than assuming a permanent block lock.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Find a reasoned disruption target.
- **Middle:** Use target feedback, claim conflicts, and Hangover to plan new blocks.
- **Late:** Distinguish preventing a lethal action from merely seeking a reassuring no-kill night.

## Claim timing and disclosure

Claim TS where appropriate; full claim when needed to coordinate or explain a disputed result.

## Read the information

No kill after a block is a lead, not proof of the killer. A target’s immunity or Hangover can explain a failed block.

## Common mistakes

Do not claim to block day abilities. Do not confuse Hangover with a new successful block. Do not assume risky killers are safe targets.

## Interactions and limits

Uncautious SK and Full Moon Werewolf can counterattack. Hangover also grants Control Immunity in the current patch baseline.

## Worked decision

Drink with 4 on N1; choose someone else on N2. No deaths after the block does not prove 4 is the killer.
