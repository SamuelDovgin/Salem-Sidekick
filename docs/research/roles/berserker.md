# Berserker

Default role family: **Neutral Apocalypse**. Context: vanilla text chat, normal factions. Research date: 2026-10-15. Source revision: 6632. No live-client test is claimed. Core summary reviewed 2026-10-19 against installed R1.5.14 role/faction text.

## Role and ability

You start attacking on N2. Actual kills build your strength toward War.

**Key rule:** First kill: Powerful attack. Second: Rampage. Third: transform into War, gaining two-target Unstoppable Rampages and Invincible Defense. A survived attempt adds no kill.

## Win condition

Win with Apocalypse by eliminating its opponents. Transforming unlocks your stronger form; transformation alone is not a win.

This assumes the default Apocalypse faction. Conversion or Flexible Factions can change the faction objective; a role change alone does not change faction.

## Beginner priorities

1. Choose killable targets rather than known strong protection.
2. Track confirmed kills and current attack state.
3. Plan for collateral effects once Rampage unlocks.

## Early, middle, and late game

These are recommendations, not forced actions:

- **Early:** Survive N1 and seek a killable first target afterward.
- **Middle:** Track escalating attack strength and collateral.
- **Late:** As War, choose the legal target set and count surviving votes; Invincible Defense does not stop hanging.

## Claim timing and disclosure

A visiting protection cover is only a fallback and cannot reproduce protection. Vigilante is a fragile kill-cover because phase, ammunition, and death labels differ.

## Read the information

An attack is not automatically a kill or progression. Avoid updating state merely because you selected a victim.

## Common mistakes

Do not use obsolete alternate-night rules. Do not begin with Powerful in the normal baseline. Do not forget visitors once Rampage unlocks.

## Interactions and limits

Solo versus allied Apocalypse affects factional defense. Witch redirection can be especially dangerous before transformation.

## Worked decision

N2 attack and kill gives progress; N3 attack stopped by defense does not. Count kills, not the nights you pressed Attack.

## Transformed state

War: select up to two Rampage targets, with Unstoppable attacks and Invincible Defense. It bypasses ordinary obstacles and can attack a Jailor when jailed. Public transformation makes maintaining a cover a voting problem rather than ordinary invisibility.
